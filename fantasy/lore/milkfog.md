# Milkfog (Mleczna Mgła)

*[Alchemical Substance, Military Application]*

> *"The fog comes not from the swamp, but from the hands of those who wish you to never find what you're looking for."*
> — Eremon the Cartographer

## Description

Milkfog is an alchemical preparation that, when released, expands rapidly into a thick, milky-white mist capable of blanketing entire districts, valleys, or battlefields. Unlike natural fog, it clings to the ground and resists dispersal by wind. A prepared canister of Milkfog can fill a city square in minutes; larger quantities can obscure a military march or hide a siege.

### Properties

| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Opacity** | Blocks vision beyond arm's length |
| **Persistence** | Lasts 2-4 hours in still air; disperses faster with wind |
| **Density** | Slightly heavier than air; settles into low areas |
| **Scent** | Faintly sweet, like cream left too long in sunlight |
| **Sound** | Dampens echoes; footsteps become muffled |
| **Chemical Stability** | Varies by maker; cheap versions dissipate unevenly |

### Uses

The practical applications are obvious:

- **Military sieges** — Conceal troop movements, approach walls undetected
- **Urban crime** — Assassinations, robberies, disappearances made easier
- **Executions** — Public killings where the crowd shouldn't see the details
- **Ceremonial** — Mystics and cultists use it to create "misty temples"
- **Escape** — Cover retreats, mislead pursuers

### Weaknesses

Milkfog is not invincible. It fails in predictable ways:

- **Running water** — Rivers and streams disperse it quickly
- **Fire** — Candles, torches burn small patches clear
- **Elevation** — Climbing above the fog line reveals everything below
- **Time** — Eventually dissipates regardless; cannot be maintained indefinitely

## History

### Ancient Origins

The oldest references to Milkfog appear in pre-Hegemony texts, where it is called "the Veil of Seleth." The elven nation of Seleth used it to protect their forest cities from human expansion. When the Nater Hegemony absorbed those territories, they captured alchemists and systematized production.

### The Hegemony's Monopoly

For centuries, the Nater Hegemony held the secret of Milkfog production. They deployed it in the Consolidation Wars to devastating effect—enemy armies marched through fog and emerged having accomplished nothing, while Hegemony forces struck from hidden positions. The monopoly broke only when defecting alchemists sold the formula to interested parties.

### Modern Proliferation

Today, Milkfog is restricted but not rare. The formula exists in multiple versions—the Hegemony's original, the simpler elven variant, and bastardized approximations that work half as well. Organized crime, mercenary companies, and wealthy individuals all have access. Armies still use it, though counter-measures have evolved.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- **Visibility and truth** — What happens when no one can see clearly?
- **Power through obscurity** — Who benefits when actions go unwitnessed?
- **Technology and warfare** — Alchemy changes the battlefield as surely as tactics

### Story Hooks
- A Milkfog attack on a crowded market—innocent victims caught in a criminal's escape
- A military campaign hidden by Milkfog; the party must navigate through enemy territory
- Someone is killing people in Milkfog—no witnesses, no clues, only the victims' fear
- An alchemist who refuses to make Milkfog for the Hegemony and flees for their life

### World-Building Notes
- Can serve as a recurring element in urban or military campaigns
- The toxicity of cheap variants creates secondary hazards
- Control of Milkfog production remains a source of political power
