# The Ancient Ones (Pradawni)

A theoretically once-existing people who vanished under unexplained circumstances. Research conducted independently over centuries — primarily into terrain formation — has presented clues suggesting that over 10-12 thousand years ago, portions of currently known territories may have been inhabited by an unknown civilization.

> **Popular Theory:** Some scholars suggest they may have been ancestors of the [[hegomonai nateriańska|Pure]], which — if proven — could shake the current world by convincing people that the Pure were the first intelligent beings to arise.

No hard evidence has been found to support this theory, though research continues despite unfavorable views from devout followers of nearly all religions (except Naterian).

| Evidence | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Artificial terrain** | Certain regions show deliberate, unnatural shaping |
| **Stone structures** | Remnants of bridges, towers, fortresses, even cities |
| **Underground corridors** | Wall outlines reported during tunnel excavation in mines |

**Possible explanations for their disappearance:**
- Natural catastrophe
- Catastrophe caused by opening a passage to the [[otchłań]]
- Goddess's wrath

# The Truth

## Actual Origins

**Dwarves — not the Pure — are descendants of the Ancient Ones.**

The religion and beliefs based on "The First and the Pure" conveniently claim that all intelligent races descended from the First's children. This is a comfortable excuse explaining dwarven origins while maintaining the claim of unified creation. In reality:

> Ancient Ones, ancestors of what we call dwarves, ruled these and other lands, wielding magic similarly to how we use electricity or atomic reactions.

---

## The Fall of the Ancient Ones

### The Connected Abyss

Thousands of years ago, the [[otchłań]] was far more connected to the world of the living than today. Demons could much more easily pierce into this world through summoning — and did so quite willingly.

### The Threat

The Ancient Ones, as a powerful civilization governing nearly the entire continent, understood an uncomfortable truth:

> Magic in its current form could not be effectively controlled. Eventually, inevitably, some group of mages would cross every boundary and bring demons upon the earth.

### The Civil War

| Faction | Goal |
|---------|------|
| **Loyalists** | Limit access to the Abyss, control magic |
| **Traditionalists** | Keep things as they were |

The traditionalists were winning. Their armies — controlled by demons — devastated what remained of loyalist territories.

### The Desperate Ritual

The most eminent mages of the Ancient Ones had been preparing the ritual for decades. When civil war forced theory into practice, they acted in desperation.

**What the ritual achieved:**
- Trapped the [[otchłań]] within a barrier
- Drew power from:
  - Mana wells within the Abyss itself
  - The power of demons (whose influence was thus limited)

**The cost:**
The accompanying explosion of magic caused **"mana burning"** — something like magical napalm that destroyed infrastructure and buildings. This explains why no surface ruins exist (except undiscovered bunker ruins).

The burn killed **all beings who wielded magic.**

Few survivors remained — too weak and scattered to rebuild civilization. They perished shortly after.

# The Survivors

## The Bunkers

The most eminent loyalists had hidden in several underground bunkers. Each was equipped with:

| Device | Function |
|--------|----------|
| **Magic Stabilizers** | Maintain high magic levels for "working" with magic; act as a failsafe preventing magical effects from escaping the designated area |
| **Magic Absorbers** | Handle and neutralize mana leaks that could occur |

## Fate of the Bunkers

- **Some bunkers** didn't survive the explosion
- **Some** were severely damaged and became death traps
- **Two survived** with Ancient Ones in hibernation

After several hundred years, these bunkers finally opened. Magic had stabilized but become far rarer than before.

## Lost Heritage

The survivors faced a cruel fate:

1. **Lost magical abilities** — prolonged exposure to magic absorbers stripped their power
2. **Lost memories** — the same exposure damaged their memory centers
3. **Found themselves** disoriented in a world both foreign and familiar
4. **Eventually found other survivors** and began anew

Thousands of years later, after long wanderings — trying to find their place in a world nature had rebuilt — they finally encountered fertile, resource-rich lands and other intelligent beings:

- **Naterians**
- **Humans**
- **Eventually, elves**

# GM Notes

- **Ancient One ruins** remain undiscovered — potential adventure sites
- **The Pure's claim** of being first intelligent beings could be shattered by finding Ancient One records
- **The ritual's barrier** still holds the Abyss sealed — but is it permanent?
- **Dwarven heritage** connects them to the world's greatest magical catastrophe
- **Magic stabilizers and absorbers** are lost technology — perhaps recoverable?
- The **mana burning** event explains why so few ruins exist from such an advanced civilization
- Ancient One bunkers could contain **preserved knowledge, artifacts, or even survivors in deeper hibernation**

### Adventure Hooks

1. Scholars seeking Ancient One proof clash with religious authorities
2. Discovery of an intact bunker with preserved technology
3. Signs that the Abyss barrier is weakening
4. Dwarven faction seeking to reclaim their "true" heritage
5. Demon remnants that survived the sealing
