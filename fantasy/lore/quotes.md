## In-World Lore

### On Aspects

> *"Nasze starania, cała nasza magia jest dla nich niczym kilka kropli pośród niezmierzonych opadów delikatnego deszczu. Pojawia się i ginie niezauważona"*

*(Our efforts, all our magic is to them like a few drops amidst the endless fall of gentle rain. It appears and vanishes unnoticed)*

### The Soldiers' Wisdom

Found among soldiers on the battlefield after fighting the undead:

> "You see Ivan, when you have a sword, stab a walker into his head like I do. This dead man want you to be as pretty as he is, you know? And don't just cut off both of his arms and legs like you did before. It's just a flesh wound for him! He can still bite you in your sorry ass, so always remember - **aim for a HEAD**."

*Best delivered with a thick Russian accent.*
