# Origins of the Races

> The fall of the Cleansed and the birth of the peoples who inherited their shattered world.

*The creation myth as told through legend and ancient song.*

---

## Overview

According to songs and legends, several thousand years ago (10,000–15,000 years), the world was inhabited by the **[The Cleansed](the-cleansed.md)**. They were an unblemished people, free of magic, originating from the small islands of the **[Archipelago of Light](../places/archipelago-of-light.md)**.

During this time, there were no wars, and all conflicts between the people were resolved by **[The First One](../characters/the-first-one.md)**, who herself designated her successor. Those who trust the ancient records say they lived in paradise — in the glow of justice, abundance, and peace. This period is called the **[Age of Light](age-of-light.md)**.

---

## The Fall of the First One

Everything changed with the enthronement of a new [The First One](../characters/the-first-one.md). Unlike her predecessors, she was characterized by curiosity and inquisitiveness.

When a strange, unprecedented element was discovered in one of the underground caves, she delved deeply into its study. It is not precisely known what was found, but many scholars theorize it was either a large deposit of **[Blue Ore](../items/blue-ore.md)** or one of the legendary **[Black Stones of Ur](../items/black-stones-of-ur.md)** — both are heavily saturated with pure magic.

According to legend, this was the **first contact of the [The Cleansed](the-cleansed.md) with magic**, which led to the end of the era.

---

## The First One's Transformation

Exposed to prolonged magical energy, the soul of [The First One](../characters/the-first-one.md) was blessed (or tainted, depending on interpretation) by establishing a connection with the **[Void](abyss.md)** — a multi-layered, immaterial dimension.

Suspended within the [Void](abyss.md), beings of indeterminate consciousness, commonly called **[Aspects](aspects.md)**, involuntarily (and according to some, deliberately) left an imprint on her soul. They granted her the ability to pass into their dimension after death.

Contact with our dimension caused a small piece to tear away from each [Aspect](aspects.md). These fragments mixed together and formed greater and lesser **[Demons](demons.md)** — chaotic beings pulled by various forces often aligned with violent emotions from living beings they contact. During contact, the dominant emotion shapes them.

The [Aspects](aspects.md) and [Demons](demons.md), now nested within [The First One's](../characters/the-first-one.md) soul, began to speak to her. She changed, and according to some records — went mad.

---

## The Birth of the Three

Shortly after, [The First One](../characters/the-first-one.md) was found to be pregnant. Her people, not knowing exactly what had happened, cared for her and begged her to name her successor. She did not do so, and contrary to tradition, commanded them to obey her child.

She died during childbirth — but did not bear one child, but **three**.

### The Three Children

| Name | Appearance | Nature |
|------|------------|--------|
| **[Torr](../characters/torr.md)** | Dark, rough skin covered in scales in places | Harsh, prone to violence, yet honorable |
| **[Daraster](../characters/daraster.md)** | Unknown from legend | Bold, wicked, yet most open and ambitious |
| **[Lesandra](../characters/lesandra.md)** | Exceptionally tall, unusually pale complexion | Secretive, helpful, most balanced; could fully control the magic in her blood |

All three inherited from their mother the ability to contact the [Void](abyss.md). Whether madness also slumbered within them remains uncertain.

---

## The Civil War

What followed is not precisely known, but the most credible records speak of the siblings turning against each other — a civil war using unmastered magical reserves that devastated the island.

Finally, [Torr](../characters/torr.md) departed with his followers in search of new lands. [Daraster](../characters/daraster.md) and [Lesandra](../characters/lesandra.md) formed an alliance that led to their marriage — the ultimate insult to [Torr](../characters/torr.md), who despised such "corruption."

Soon they, too, departed the island, which was no longer fit for habitation, sailing in the opposite direction from [Torr](../characters/torr.md).

---

## The Spreading of the Races

Over millennia, the [The Cleansed](the-cleansed.md) ceased to exist, and their place was taken by their descendants:

| Race | Polish | Ancestor | Notes |
|------|--------|----------|-------|
| **Naterians** | Naterowie | [Torr](../characters/torr.md) | Wild mountain tribes also called Orcs |
| **Humans** | Ludzie | [Daraster](../characters/daraster.md) | Conceived through his affairs; later partially mixed with legitimate children |
| **Elves** | Elfy | [Daraster](../characters/daraster.md) + [Lesandra](../characters/lesandra.md) | Direct lineage only; did not mix with others |
| **Dwarves** | Krasnoludy | Forbidden unions | Descendants of [Torr's](../characters/torr.md) children with [Daraster's](../characters/daraster.md) children; punished and driven underground |

### Expansion of Torr's Line

[Torr](../characters/torr.md) expanded his newly founded empire and demanded every woman, believing that the multitude of his children would ensure his blood's survival for eternity and strengthen his people when his siblings' forces decided to attack him. His offspring supposedly numbered in the hundreds, if not thousands — each from the marriage bed, by current standards.

### Daraster's Legacy

Despite having children with [Lesandra](../characters/lesandra.md), [Daraster](../characters/daraster.md) was not faithful to her, resulting in dozens or hundreds of bastards after his death. Still, the number of his children remained considerably smaller than his brother's.

### Lesandra's Bloodline

[Lesandra](../characters/lesandra.md) remained faithful to her husband despite numerous betrayals and emphasized blood purity. Her blood was considered the strongest — nearly all children of hers and [Daraster](../characters/daraster.md) closely resembled her (sometimes scholars try to find his infidelity's cause in this). The belief in these legends results in elves viewing their race with superiority.

### The Dwarves' Fate

The [Dwarves](../races/dwarves.md) (also called "Galdanie") are considered descendants of forbidden unions — children of [Torr's](../characters/torr.md) children with [Daraster's](../characters/daraster.md) children. As punishment for this disgrace, they were driven underground by the others — especially because [Naterians](../races/naterians.md) hated the rest ([Humans](../races/humans.md) and [Elves](../races/elves.md)); and the feeling was mutual.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- **Cycle of corruption** — paradise lost through curiosity and power
- **Brotherhood torn apart** — war between siblings echoes through millennia
- **Bloodline as destiny** — do descendants carry their ancestors' virtues and sins?
- **Elven superiority complex** — rooted in pure blood mythology
- **Dwarven outsider status** — punished for unions both sides condemned

### Story Hooks
- A scholar discovers evidence that not all of the three children shared their mother's madness — and some bloodlines may be free of the "curse"
- Dwarven refugees claim their underground exile was unjust — seeking allies to reclaim surface lands
- The discovery of a surviving island from the Archipelago of Light, still unblemished by magic
- Heretical cults worship [Torr](../characters/torr.md) as the only honorable child, denouncing his siblings' "corruption"
- Elven supremacists seek to "purify" mixed bloodlines — including the "impure" elves who married humans

### Cultural Conflicts
| Conflict | Root Cause |
|----------|-----------|
| Naterians vs. Others | [Torr's](../characters/torr.md) hatred passed down; historical grudges |
| Elves vs. Dwarves | Underground exile remembered; elven condescension |
| Humans as Middle Ground | Mixed heritage means they belong (or don't) everywhere |
| Theocratic Debates | Do the theological beliefs contradict the mythological records? |
