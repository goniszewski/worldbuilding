# Magic System

Unlike the Dragon Age series, magic is not inherently dangerous in the hands of unprepared "apostates."

Mages, especially inexperienced ones, are not considered a direct threat. Most of them, without proper years of training, cannot cast the simplest spells. They might produce a few sparks, but instead of setting someone on fire, they would at most burn themselves or ignite a small fire.

There is no such thing as blood magic. Forbidden forms of the arcane arts certainly exist, but they require immense knowledge and long years of painstaking self-improvement.

## Demon Pacts

Pacts with demons are possible, but a mage must be truly powerful for a demon to even notice them. Demons are too chaotic, alien, and beyond the boundaries of human reasoning to simply negotiate with. No mage can be entirely certain what exactly they're doing and what influence it will have on them and the demon during various rituals aimed at interaction between the two worlds.

One of the few known living individuals who have achieved anything in this field is the [[arcymag z Ur]].

# Magic by Race

## Human Magic

In human kingdoms, there are many forms of education and organization for mages:

| Institution | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Universities** | Supported by individual kingdoms; most popular form of education |
| **Regional Guilds** | Provide steady employment for trained mages |
| **Holy Orders** | Serve gods and kingdoms in combat against dangers, help the common folk |
| **Elite Circles** | For the most ambitious with great aptitude |

**Social Perception:**
Depending on their family's origin and wealth, the gift of magic can be seen as either good or bad:

- **Good**: Poor families (education sponsored by the treasury) or progressive noble houses
- **Bad**: Families believing in blood purity and closeness to the [[hegemonia nateriańska|Pure]], or those with political aspirations (a mage brings unwanted attention)

### Oversight

Mages are watched over by the **Order of Paladins**, which:
- Judges mages when doubts arise about their research or "improper use of magic"
- Investigates murders, arsons, harm to others, forbidden rituals, necromancy, and other crimes
- Hunts and eliminates rogue mages

## Elven Magic

For elves, magic holds special significance and is considered an extraordinary gift that must be properly nurtured and developed.

- Every elf with magical talent is **valued and guaranteed free education**
- They have access to **magin centers** for further development
- Noble houses are **proud of their mages** and ensure the best conditions for study
- Mages play important roles in **political, spiritual, and military spheres**

**Historical Note:** The most powerful mages throughout history have been elves — both those meritorious for science, peace, and ending wars, and infamous ones who spread death, destruction, and corruption.

**Research Freedom:** Elves impose fewer restrictions on magical research and often conduct research into black magic — always by experienced mages and under special supervision.

### Oversight

| Organization | Role |
|-------------|------|
| **Council of Magin** | Controls research and actions, commands when necessary |
| **Black Cloaks** | Secretive formation that captures, interrogates, and isolates particularly dangerous mages; instills fear and deters suspicious activities |

## Naterian Magic

On Naterian lands, mages are treated as **property of the Naterian Hegemony** — more precisely, of the **Zakkari Dominion** within the hegemony.

**Process:**
1. Verified Naterian mages are taken from their families at a young age
2. They fall under Zakkari's care, losing their previous citizenship and gaining a new one

**Purpose:**
- **Isolation** of mages from ordinary people
- **Strengthening magic** in future generations (both parents are mages)

> This practice mirrors certain elven houses that wish to strengthen magic in their bloodlines rather than let it dilute.

## Dwarven "Magic"

Dwarves practically have no mages. Their magical abilities are negligible.

**Popular Theory:** Many scholars attribute this to their "lower station" compared to other races (a theory that pleases traditional Naterians greatly).

**Compensation:** Dwarves compensate with extraordinary **engineering abilities**:

- Create practical devices powered by energy from proper blue ore processing
- Heavily research blue ore and methods of extracting greater energy from various sources

**Contribution:** They authored the theory of [[mana]] — pure, unformed energy that magic shapes and uses.

# GM Notes

- Magic is **accessible but regulated** — not a forbidden curse, but a disciplined art
- Demon pacts are **high-level content** — only for the most powerful mages
- Blood magic doesn't exist in this world — other forbidden arts fill that niche
- Each race's approach to magic reflects their **culture and values**
- The paladin orders and Black Cloaks provide **natural conflict hooks** for mage-related adventures
