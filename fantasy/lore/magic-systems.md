# Spirit Binding

## Overview

Spirit Binding is the primary form of magic in the realm. Practitioners, called **Binders**, form contracts with elemental spirits to channel supernatural abilities. Without a bound spirit, no mortal can access magic.

## How It Works

### The Contract

1. **Seeking**: A Binder enters a trance and reaches into the Veil
2. **Calling**: Spirits are attracted by offering a **binding fee**
3. **Negotiation**: Terms are set — spirit power vs. mortal payment
4. **Sealing**: Both parties speak the Unmaking Words (for legal Compact bindings)

### Payment Types

| Payment | Common For | Risk |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Memories | Minor spirits (Ember) | May lose important memories |
| Life Years | Major spirits (Storm) | Accelerated aging |
| Servitude | Any tier | Spirit may demand dangerous tasks |
| The Unmaking | Void spirits | Never fully understood, always fatal |

### Spirit Tiers

#### Ember Spirits (Common)
- Weakest tier, easily bound
- Powers: Small flames, minor illusions, animal speech
- Typical payment: One memory (often trivial)

#### Storm Spirits (Rare)
- Powerful entities, negotiate hard
- Powers: Weather control, lightning, flight, transformation
- Typical payment: 10-20 years of life

#### Void Spirits (Legendary)
- Only two known to exist: **The Whisperer** and **The Silence**
- Powers: Reality bending, time manipulation, creation
- Payment: Always The Unmaking (death)
- **Note**: Compact claims they're all bound. Some doubt this.

## The Compact's Role

The Silver Compact holds a **monopoly** on legal spirit binding:

- They maintain summoning circles in the Silver Woods
- Only Compact-licensed Binders may legally bind spirits
- All contracts must be registered
- Illegal binding = trial by Unmaking (dissolution of soul)

## Illegal Binding

Some practice **wild binding** outside Compact oversight:

- Frees spirits from standard contracts
- More power available, less oversight
- The Hushed leads the largest wild binding network
- Penalties are severe: death or worse

## Limitations

- One spirit per Binder at a time (rare exceptions exist)
- Breaking a contract: Spirit becomes **Hollow** — mindless, violent
- Death with active contract: Spirit hunts the Binder's descendants

## Known Wild Bindings

- Lyra Dawnwhisper: Storm-tier Ember-Sister (see character sheet)
- The Hushed: Claims Storm-tier, possibly higher (unverified)

## For the GM

**Adventure Hooks:**
- A Hollow spirit escapes — players must recapture or destroy it
- A Binder wants their contract broken, seeking forbidden knowledge
- Compact offers players a "voluntary" binding as reward for service

**Balance Tips:**
- Ember spirits for support/roleplay focused games
- Storm spirits for combat-heavy campaigns
- Void spirits as campaign-level threats, never party members
