# Mana

> *"They speak of magic as if it were a force unto itself. But fire does not exist without fuel—and mana is that fuel."*
> — Martin Halgrom, *On the Nature of Arcane Energy*

Mana is the fundamental energy that permeates the world and serves as the fuel for all magical phenomena. Unlike traditional understandings of magic, mana represents a distinct, measurable energy that flows from a single cosmic source: the Well in the depths of [Abyss](abyss.md).

> ⚠️ **Game Note:** At the beginning of the campaign, mana exists only as theory. Player characters and most NPCs do not know of its existence—they simply call it "magic."

---

## The Nature of Mana

### Origin

Mana is pure energy emanating from the Well at the deepest level of [Abyss](abyss.md). This cosmic source continuously releases mana into the surrounding void, where it then disperses outward through unknown mechanisms into the world of the living.

### Density Gradient

Mana concentration follows a predictable pattern:

| Location | Density | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **The Abyss (deepest)** | Maximum | Source of all mana |
| **Upper Abyss levels** | Very high | Decreasing by depth |
| **Surface world** | Trace amounts | Barely perceptible |
| **Remote regions** | Negligible | Nearly undetectable |

The further one travels from the Well, the more sparse and diluted mana becomes.

## The Common Misconception

### What Mages Believe

To ordinary mages—and indeed to most of the world's population—there is no distinction between "magic" and "mana." They sense heightened energy in certain locations and simply call it "magic."

| Term | Common Usage | Technical Meaning |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Magic** | Used for everything magical | The *perceived* phenomenon |
| **Mana** | Unknown to the masses | The *actual* energy source |

### The Pioneers

A small number of scholars, most notably [Martin Halgrom](../characters/martin-guecventrak.md), recognize this fundamental misunderstanding. They work to spread awareness that magic is merely the *effect*—mana is the *cause*.

> *"Call it ignorance. Call it tradition. But when a fire burns, you do not say the flame *is* the energy. You know better."*
> — Martin Halgrom

## The Ancients' Mastery

The [Pradawni](ancient-ones.md) (Ancients) understood mana's true nature and exploited it at a highly advanced technological level. To them, mana was treated much like humans treat **electricity**—a fundamental resource to be harnessed, controlled, and distributed.

### Absorbers

The Ancients developed devices capable of **absorbing mana** from their surroundings. These magical instruments actively drew in ambient mana, depleting the energy within a localized area.

| Device | Function | Modern Equivalent |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Mana Absorber** | Draws in and collects ambient mana | Vacuum pump / solar panel |
| **Capacity** | Varies by device | — |
| **Risk** | Depleted areas may destabilize spells | — |

### Stabilizers

Perhaps the most ingenious invention, mana **stabilizers** were screw-driver-like devices that created isolated zones. These served two critical functions:

1. **Containment** — Prevented rituals or spells from escaping control
2. **Saturation** — Allowed precise mana density control within the isolated area

| Device | Function | Description |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| **Stabilizer (pair)** | Isolates area between them | Screwdriver-shaped, used in pairs |
| **Containment field** | Magical boundary | Prevents spell escape |
| **Saturation control** | Adjusts local mana density | Fine-tune for rituals |

> *"The Ancients did not cast spells—they engineered them. Every ritual was a circuit, every stabilizer a component."*
> — Dwarven archivist studying Pradawni ruins

## Implications for Practitioners

### Sensing Mana

Trained mages can perceive variations in mana density, though they interpret this as "magical energy" or "powerful locations." This sensitivity manifests as:

- Tingling sensations in charged areas
- Enhanced spellcasting in high-mana zones
- Difficulty casting in depleted regions

### Practical Concerns

| Situation | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **High mana area** | Enhanced spells, faster casting |
| **Depleted area** | Weakened magic, increased strain |
| **Abyss proximity** | Overwhelming power, dangerous instability |
| **Remote wilderness** | Minimal magical effect possible |

## For the GM

### Campaign Hooks

- **The Theory**: The party encounters Martin Halgrom's research early on. Understanding mana gives them an edge—or paints a target on their backs.

- **The Absorber**: An Ancient mana absorber is discovered, still functional. It has drained an entire valley to near-zero mana. What was it protecting—or hiding?

- **The Stabilizer Set**: A pair of stabilizers surfaces on the black market. Whoever controls them can contain magical threats—or commit untraceable rituals.

- **The Race**: Several factions are independently researching the Abyss. Competition for access could spark conflict.

### Worldbuilding Connections

| Element | Connection |
|---------|------------|
| **The Well** | The source—where does it lead? |
| **[Pradawni](ancient-ones.md)** | Masters of mana technology—what happened to them? |
| **[Martin Halgrom](../characters/martin-guecventrak.md)** | Living pioneer—ally or complication? |
| **The Abyss** | Dangerous expedition site—rewards and risks |

### Mechanical Notes (Optional)

If adapting this system for TTRPG mechanics:

- **Mana Points**: Spells cost more in low-density areas
- **Absorption Tactics**: Devices can be weaponized against spellcasters
- **Ancient Ruins**: Pradawni sites are inherently dangerous due to residual high-density mana
