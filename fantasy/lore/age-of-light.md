*The era of peace and paradise before magic touched the world.*

## Description

The **Age of Light** is the name given to the period when the [[lore/the-cleansed|The Cleansed]] lived in paradise on the **[[places/archipelago-of-light]]**.

According to legend and ancient songs, this was a time of:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Justice** | Fair dealing and harmony among the people |
| **Abundance** | Prosperity and plenty |
| **Peace** | No wars or armed conflicts |
| **Spiritual Clarity** | A people "unblemished" by magic |

The name "Light" symbolizes these ideals — the antithesis of the "[[lore/abyss|Void]]" that would later corrupt the world.

---

## Characteristics

### Governance
- All conflicts were resolved by **[[lore/the-first-one|The First]]**
- The [[lore/the-first-one|The First]] designated her own successor
- Matrilineal succession was tradition

### Society
- **No warfare** — violence was unknown
- **No magic** — the Cleansed had never encountered magical energy
- **Paradise** — material and spiritual abundance

### The Succession Custom
Each [[lore/the-first-one|The First]] would name her successor before death. This sacred tradition maintained peace and continuity.

The last [[lore/the-first-one|The First One]] broke this custom — commanding her people to obey her unborn child instead of naming a successor.

---

## The End of the Age

The Age of Light ended with the **discovery of magic**:

1. The last [[lore/the-first-one|The First One]] found a strange element in an underground cave
2. Prolonged exposure connected her soul to the [[lore/abyss|Void]]
3. She bore three children touched by magic
4. Civil war erupted among the Cleansed
5. The war devastated the Archipelago of Light
6. The survivors scattered, becoming the ancestors of modern races

## Cultural Legacy

The Age of Light remains significant in religious and cultural traditions:

| Tradition | Reference |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Elven theology** | "The time before corruption" — their bloodline represents the closest to Light |
| **Creation myths** | Many religions reference a paradise before the fall |
| **Theological debate** | Does the Age of Light prove magic is inherently corrupting? |
| **Heretical movements** | Some seek to "restore" the Age of Light |

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **Paradise lost** — a perfect age destroyed by curiosity
- **Cycle of history** — will the current races repeat the Cleansed's fate?
- **What came before** — was the Age of Light truly peaceful, or idealized in memory?
- **Light vs. Void** — the eternal conflict between order and chaos

### Story Hooks
- Scholars debate whether the Age of Light was truly peaceful or merely lacked records of conflict
- A cult seeks to recreate the conditions of the Age of Light
- Artifacts from the Age could reveal whether the Cleansed had any flaws
- The "Light" may have been magical energy — magic existing before the Fall
