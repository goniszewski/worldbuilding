## The True Nature

> ⚠️ **GM Information — Not Common Knowledge**
> 
> The Cleansed were **not** the first people. They were the **last survivors** of the Ancient One civilization — refugees from the Fall who lost their memories, their magic, and their identity.

---

## Origin: The Fall of the Ancient Ones

### The Catastrophe

Thousands of years ago, the Ancient Ones' civil war reached its climax:

| Event | Consequence |
|-------|-------------|
| **The Ritual** | Loyalists sealed the Abyss using all available magical energy |
| **Mana Burning** | Uncontrolled magical release destroyed surface infrastructure |
| **Mass Death** | Nearly all magical beings perished |
| **Survivors** | Those in shielded bunkers — stripped of magic and memory |

### The Bunker Survivors

The few survivors emerged from underground shelters after centuries:

| Trait | Cause |
|-------|-------|
| **No magic** | Prolonged exposure to magic absorbers |
| **Memory loss** | Same exposure damaged memory centers |
| **Confusion** | Emerged into a world they didn't recognize |
| **Dispersal** | Scattered, unable to rebuild |

Over millennia, these survivors:
1. Wandered in small groups
2. Encountered other survivors
3. Settled the **Archipelago of Light**
4. Forgot everything that came before

| **No war** | Too few people and too weak to fight |

## The Rediscovery

### The Cave Discovery

When the last [The First One](../characters/the-first-one.md) found "strange elements" in a cave, she wasn't discovering magic. She was **finding her ancestors' technology**.

> The cave was likely an Ancient One bunker or storage site. The "blue ore" or "Black Stone" was preserved magical equipment.

### What Happened

| Religious Account | Historical Reality |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Curious leader explored** | Survivor accessed forbidden site |
| **Blessed by the gods** | Magical exposure reactivated latent abilities |
| **Connection to the Void** | Genetic memory + magical resonance |
| **Voices in her head** | Actual Aspects responding to magic |

### The Transformation

The First One's exposure changed her:
- Connected her soul to the Abyss
- Gave her children magical abilities
- Doomed her people to repeat the cycle

## The Three Children

### Born of Rediscovery

The children weren't divine — they were **contaminated**:

| The Child | Interpretation | Reality |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| **Torr** | God of warriors, harsh | Clan leader affected by magical mutation |
| **Daraster** | Bold, ambitious, human ancestor | Survivor who adapted quickly |
| **Lesandra** | Pure elven bloodline | Keeper of old ways, least affected |

### The Bloodline Problem

Every subsequent generation carried **traces of Ancient One contamination**. This is why:
- Naterians are considered "impure" by other races
- Elves believe their bloodline is pure (it's not — just less tainted)
- Dwarves were exiled (genetic contamination most visible)
- Magic users exist at all (latent ability from Ancient One heritage)

---

## The Cleansed Diaspora

After the civil war triggered by the children's conflict:

### The Islands

| Group | Destination | Outcome |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Torr's followers** | Mainland north | Became Naterians |
| **Daraster + Lesandra** | Mainland south | Became Humans + Elves |
| **Mixed offspring** | Underground | Became Dwarves |

### The End

Within a few generations, the Cleansed **ceased to exist as a people**. Their language faded, their bunkers collapsed, their history became myth.

Only the [Age of Light](age-of-light.md) stories survived — twisted by time into creation myths.

## The Cleansed as Slaves Theory

> *From the original ideas: "Czyści byli niewolnikami Pradawnych?" (Were the Cleansed slaves of the Ancient Ones?)*

### Alternative Interpretation

Some scholars propose the Cleansed were **not survivors** but **escaped slaves**:

| Evidence | Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Memory loss** | Deliberately induced to prevent rebellion |
| **Magic stripping** | Control mechanism to keep them weak |
| **Bunker placement** | Slave quarters, not shelters |
| **The forgetting** | Convenient for hiding the truth |

### The Theory

> The Cleansed were a slave population of the Ancient Ones. During the Fall, they were freed — by death of their masters, not by their own action. Their "paradise" was a bunker colony, their "purity" was enforced weakness.

### Which Is True?

| Version | Implications |
|---------|--------------|
| **Survivors** | Tragic accident, no one to blame |
| **Slaves** | Ancient Ones were oppressors; revolution was justified |
| **Both** | Complex history — masters and slaves both perished |

**Let players discover which interpretation fits your world.**

## For the GM

### Key Revelations

| Discovery | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| **Bunker ruins** | Physical proof of Ancient One existence |
| **Cleansed inscriptions** | Records that don't match religious texts |
| **The Sky Torch design** | Blueprints for monument repair |
| **The Slave Records** | Ancient Ones had a dark side |
| **Martin's Analysis** | Scientific proof of timeline discrepancies |

### Using the Cleansed

#### Before Revelation
- The Cleansed are the **sacred origin** of all races
- Their Age of Light is the **ideal** to aspire to
- Magic is a **gift** that should be respected

#### After Revelation
- The Cleansed were **victims and perpetrators**
- Their paradise was **bunker aftermath**
- Magic is a **curse that repeats**

#### Moral Complexity
- Ancient Ones weren't purely good or evil
- Cleansed myths hide uncomfortable truths
- The cycle can be broken — or reinforced
- Magic isn't inherently evil, but it's dangerous

| **The Cleansed Cult** | Heretics who worship the Cleansed as they truly were |
