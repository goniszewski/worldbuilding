## Description

**Demons** are chaotic beings that originated from fragments of [[lore/aspects|Aspects]] — pieces that tore away when the [[lore/abyss|Void]] made contact with the physical world through [[characters/the-first-one|The First One]].

Unlike their parent entities, demons are:
- **Emotion-driven** — shaped by the dominant emotion present during their formation
- **Violent in nature** — often aligned with violent emotions from living beings
- **Malformed** — greater and lesser demons based on how much of the Aspect fragmented
- **Transient** — many exist only briefly before dissipating

### Formation Process

According to legend:
1. [[characters/the-first-one|The First One]] established a connection with the [[lore/abyss|Void]]
2. Contact with the physical dimension caused fragments to tear from each [[lore/aspects|Aspect]]
3. These fragments mixed together in the Void
4. During contact with living beings, the dominant emotion shaped the demon's form
5. Violent emotions created violent demons; other emotions created other types

## Characteristics

| **Violent Affinity** | Many are drawn to violent emotions |

### Emotion Types

Demons are commonly associated with:

| Emotion | Manifestation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Rage/Fury** | Combat-oriented, aggressive |
| **Fear/Terror** | Horror-inducing, psychological |
| **Despair** | Nihilistic, corrupting |
| **Lust** | Manipulation, seduction |
| **Pride** | Arrogant, demanding worship |
| **Greed** | Collecting, hoarding |

## Interaction with the Physical World

Demons can cross into the physical world, but:
- **Temporary** — most cannot sustain physical manifestation
- **Corrupting** — contact with demons can influence mortals
- **Dangerous** — even lesser demons pose significant threats

### Demon Pacts

Pacts with demons are possible but dangerous:
- A mage must be truly powerful for a demon to even notice them
- Demons are too chaotic and alien to negotiate with reliably
- No mage can be certain what influence the pact will have

One of the few known individuals who have achieved anything with demon pacts is the [[characters/archmage-of-ur]].

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **Corruption** — the price of power gained through demons
- **Emotion as weapon** — demons as manifestations of inner demons
- **Chaos vs. order** — demons represent the antithesis of civilization

### Story Hooks
- A demon pact goes wrong, corrupting the pact-maker
- Lesser demons begin appearing, signaling something wrong with the Void
- A greater demon seeks to reunite all its fragments
- Someone claims to have made peaceful contact with a demon
