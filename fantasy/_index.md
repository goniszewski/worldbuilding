## Campaign Summary

**Setting**: Post-Sundering fantasy realm, 523 P.S.

**Core Conflict**: The Silver Compact maintains power through magical monopoly. A rogue network (The Unbound) fights to free spirit binding. Characters navigate between truth and order.

**Player Tone**: Moral ambiguity, exploration-focused, world-reactive.

**Current Arc**: Lyra's past catches up while investigating Compact secrets.
