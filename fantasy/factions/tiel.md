## Description

The lords of Tiel rule over the rocky, mineral-rich valleys that the early settlers deemed worthless — until they found the *błękitna ruda* (blue ore). This mysterious mineral, when refined, produces metal of extraordinary strength and faint magical properties. Tiel controls the only known deposits.

Unlike other houses who accumulate wealth through trade or agriculture, Tiel's power comes from the earth itself. Their mines are vast, their forges never sleep, and their weapons/armor command premium prices throughout the realm.

### Blue Ore

The *błękitna ruda* (literally: "blue ore") is Tiel's most valuable resource:

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Appearance | Faint blue veins in dark rock |
| Refined Form | Steel-blue metal that holds edges indefinitely |
| Magical Affinity | Minor mana amplification |
| Rarity | Found only in Tiel's valleys |
| Control | Tiel holds monopoly |

The ore is strictly controlled — selling unrefined ore to outsiders is punishable by death. Refined blue ore weapons are status symbols among the nobility.

### Mining Culture

Tiel society revolves around the mines:
- Miners are the highest-status workers
- Children as young as 10 work in "light" mining areas
- Fatal accidents are considered "the mountain's tithe"
- The mines never close — three shifts run continuously

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- The cost of progress
- Environmental exploitation
- Monopoly and its consequences
- Class division (lords above, miners below)

### Story Hooks
- A new blue ore vein has been discovered — in Mort territory
- A mine collapse reveals something ancient beneath
- Kowal Elara has disappeared with a batch of ore
- Slaves (or indentured workers) are staging a rebellion

### GM Notes
> Tiel represents industrial power — wealthy, resource-rich, but ethically ambiguous. Their workers are often exploited, making them potential allies for reform-minded parties.

## Trivia

- Tiel's weapons never need sharpening — but they also never break
- The mountains are said to "sing" when ore is struck
- Miners who die underground are never brought up — "the mountain keeps its own"
- Blue ore has been found in the Ash Wastes — but no one has successfully mined it
