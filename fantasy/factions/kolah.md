## Description

The house of Kolah dwells in the highest, most treacherous peaks of the realm — lands so harsh that even goats struggle to survive. Here, among the clouds, they worship the *Kozi Bóg* (Goat God), an ancient deity whose nature is neither wholly good nor evil but simply *mountain*.

For over a thousand years, the Kolah have maintained their faith and their isolation. The Naterian Church calls them heretics. Other houses call them savages. The Kolah call them insignificant.

### The Goat God

The *Kozi Bóg* is a deity of the highlands:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Nature** | Neither light nor dark — the mountain itself |
| **Worship** | Survival is prayer; hardship is blessing |
| **Symbols** | Horns, hooves, steep paths, treacherous ledges |
| **Clergy** | Called *Pasterze* (Shepherds) — lead by example |
| **Sacred Acts** | Walking impossible paths, surviving impossible odds |

The Goat God's teachings are simple:
1. The mountain does not care if you live
2. Therefore, *you* must care enough for both
3. Those who cannot climb were not meant to
4. The view from the top justifies the climb

### Mountain Ways

Kolah society is defined by the peaks:

- **Children** climb before they walk
- **Death** comes often; mourning is brief
- **Marriage** requires both partners to climb the Honeymoon Ledge (couple must survive)
- **Justice** is simple: the mountain judges (if you fall, you deserved it)
- **Warriors** fight with horns on their helmets, hooves on their boots

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Faith without doctrine
- The mountain as teacher
- Survival as virtue
- The cost of isolation

### Story Hooks
- A southern noble is exiled here as "punishment" — must survive
- The old mountain path has been blocked — something lives there now
- The Goat God hasn't answered prayers for a season — doubt spreads
- A Kolah warrior seeks to prove themselves in the lowlands

### GM Notes
> Kolah represents a completely different way of life — harsh, honest, and deeply tied to the land. They make interesting allies for survival scenarios or contrasts to "civilized" society.

## Trivia

- Kolah warriors can outclimb horses and most beasts
- They carve their totems from live cliff-rock — one mistake kills them
- A saying: "Kolah's blessing is a scar"
- They consider most humans "valley-soft"
