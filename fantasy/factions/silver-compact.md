# The Silver Compact

## Overview

The Silver Compact is the ruling body controlling all legal spirit binding in the realm. Founded 100 years after the Sundering to "prevent another catastrophe," it operates as both government and religious order, enforcing arcane law through licensed Binders.

## Core Philosophy

> "Order through control. Control through binding. Binding through the Compact."

The Compact believes spirit magic is too dangerous for unregulated use. They position themselves as protectors of the realm — preventing another Sundering by monopolizing the very power that caused it.

**The Official Narrative:**
- Spirits are wild, dangerous forces that require binding to be safe
- The Compact ensures fair contracts and protection for mortals
- Wild binders are selfish criminals who risk unleashing devastation

**The Truth (known only to leadership):**
- Spirits are sentient beings being enslaved
- The "payment" system drains spirit essence, not mortal
- The Compact has no plan to ever release bound spirits

### Council of Scholars
- Interprets binding law
- Maintains spirit summoning circles
- Conducts forbidden research (void spirits)

### Enforcer Legion
- Compact's military/police force
- Hunt wild binders
- Guard headquarters

### Binder Guild
- Trains and licenses all legal Binders
- Oversees contract registration
- Evaluates binding potential in children

## Resources

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Binding Circles** | Sacred sites in Silver Woods; only place stable summoning occurs |
| **Spirit Essence** | Harvested from broken contracts; powers Compact wards |
| **Information Network** | Spies in every major city; known as "Silver Ears" |
| **Void Spirits** | The Whisperer and The Silence — claimed bound, unverified |

## The Enforcer Legion

### Organization
- 500 active enforcers
- Led by **First Enforcer Sable** (see NPCs)
- Divided into: Hunters (wild binders), Wardens (cities), Sentinels (HQ)

### Capabilities
- All members are licensed Binders (minimum Ember-tier)
- Access to Compact tracking magic
- Authority to detain, try, and execute without civilian oversight

### Known Tactics
- "Soft infiltration": Enforcers posing as merchants, travelers
- "Demonstration": Public punishment to discourage wild binding
- "Acquisition": Children with binding potential are "invited" to training

## Public Opinion

**Rural Areas**: Grateful for protection from "wild magic"
**Cities**: Tolerant but wary; too powerful to resist
**Scholars**: Increasingly skeptical of Compact doctrine
**The Poor**: Bitter; Compact takes talented children

## Internal Factions

Not everyone in the Compact believes in the current path:

- **Reformers**: Want opening of binding access; led by Binder-Guild Master Thess
- **True Believers**: Current doctrine is correct; led by Archon Vael
- **Secret Doubters**: Know the truth, too afraid to act; Lyra's former friends

## Current Situation

Archon Vael has grown more paranoid. Recent actions:
- Doubled Enforcer patrols
- Restricted Binder travel permits
- Increased "recruitment" of talented children
- Authorized execution of minor wild binders

The Whisperer's Shrine activity has Vael distracted — potentially the party's window to act.

## Flags and Symbols

- **Symbol**: Silver spiral bound by chains
- **Colors**: Silver and white
- **Greeting**: "The Compact holds." (response: "Through binding, order.")

## For the GM

**Using the Compact**: They're not mustache-twirling villains. They believe they're doing right. This makes them more dangerous — they won't question themselves.

**Moral Complexity**: Compact enforcers are people who genuinely believe they're protecting the innocent. Some know the truth and justify it as "necessary evil."

**Adventure Hooks**:
- Compact offers party "amnesty" in exchange for taking down The Unbound
- A Compact enforcer defects, seeking party help to expose the truth
- The Compact's response to Shrine activity creates collateral damage
