## Description

The house of Czar began as minor nobility with no particular advantages — no great wealth, no legendary warriors, no ancient bloodlines. What they had was patience, cunning, and an unmatched talent for *knowing things*.

Through generations of strategic marriages, carefully cultivated alliances, and an intelligence network that rivals Graz's trade contacts, Czar has risen to become the power behind the throne. The current head, **Lonen Czar**, serves as Royal Advisor — the first non-noble to hold the position.

### The Puppet Masters

Czar's power is invisible. They rarely act directly; instead, they:
- Place informants in every major house
- Manipulate marriages to produce favorable heirs
- Spread rumors that shape political discourse
- Own debts that give them leverage over half the nobility

> *"Czar doesn't need to conquer — everyone already owes them something."*

### The Lonen Situation

Lonen Czar's rise as Royal Advisor has made the house simultaneously more powerful and more vulnerable. His position is resented by old nobility who see him as an upstart, and his family's methods are increasingly scrutinized.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Power without honor
- The price of knowledge
- Manipulation and free will
- What happens when the puppet master is exposed?

### Story Hooks
- A party discovers they are pawns in a Czar scheme
- Lonen offers a "simple task" — steal a document from another house
- Someone is trying to assassinate Lonen — protect or exploit?
- The Czar intelligence network is for sale

### GM Notes
> Czar makes excellent antagonists or morally ambiguous patrons. Their schemes can involve any house, and their resources are vast — but their methods are rarely clean.

## Trivia

- Czar agents can be recognized by their silver cufflinks (subtle, but real)
- Lonen keeps no personal guards — "any assassin would be my own agent"
- The phrase "Czar's eyes" means someone is watching — probably true
- They have files on every noble, dating back 200 years
