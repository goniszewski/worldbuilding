# Timrel

> "Morze oczyści wszystkich." *(The sea cleanses all.)*

## Overview

| Field | Value |

| **Type** | Noble House |

| **Words** | Dominion, Commerce, Judgment |

## Description

The house of Timrel rules the waves. As Sea Stewards, they claim hereditary authority over all maritime matters — shipping licenses, port rights, naval defense, and *judgment of crimes at sea*. Their word is law from the docks to the horizon.

Their navy, the *Grabarze Morscy* (Sea Gravediggers), is the largest in the realm. Their ships patrol trade routes, collect tariffs, and ensure "proper conduct" — which usually means Timrel's interests.

### The Sea Gravediggers

The *Grabarze Morscy* reputation is fearsome:

| Vessel Type | Purpose | Count |
|------------|---------|-------|
| **Łupaczka** (Breaker) | Warship | 40 |
| **Handlowiec** (Trader) | Cargo | 120 |
| **Śledź** (Herring) | Patrol | 80 |
| **Grobarz** (Gravedigger) | Flagship | 3 |

Their tactics emphasize speed and intimidation. Most pirates surrender rather than face the Gravedigger fleet.

### Maritime Law

Timrel enforces the *"Prawo Wodne"* (Water Law):

- Ships without Timrel licenses may be boarded and taxed
- Maritime disputes are judged by Timrel courts
- Pirates captured by Timrel face the "cleansing" — drowning as ritual purification
- Smugglers lose their ships and cargo — plus a fine

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Power over vital resources
- The blur between law and tyranny
- Justice versus vengeance
- Freedom of the seas

### Story Hooks
- A party is accused of smuggling — they must prove their innocence
- Pirates are getting bolder — Timrel offers a pardon for assistance
- A foreign ship refuses to pay tariffs — war looms
- Someone is sabotaging Timrel ships from within

### GM Notes
> Timrel represents maritime power — useful for naval campaigns and sea-based adventures. Their authority is often resented, making them potential antagonists.

## Trivia

- Timrel captains are buried at sea, never on land
- The phrase "Visit the Gravediggers" is a death threat
- Their ships are painted black with white bone emblems
- Timrel has sunk more ships than any war in history
