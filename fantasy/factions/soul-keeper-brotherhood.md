## Description

Few know the Brotherhood exists. Fewer still have seen its members. They appear only in the darkest legends, whispered about by those who glimpsed a figure carrying a blade that drank the very light from the air. They are the Keepers of the Soul Breaker — a cursed weapon that could end civilizations, and the only ones trusted to bear its terrible burden.

The Brotherhood operates in absolute secrecy. They have no permanent home, no banner, no name they speak aloud. Members are identified only by a silver chain worn beneath their clothing — a chain forged from the same darkness that creates the Soul Breaker itself, granting minor resistance to its corruption.

### Purpose

The Brotherhood exists for one reason: to ensure the Soul Breaker never falls into wrong hands. This means keeping it contained, controlled, and when necessary, deployed against threats too great for ordinary armies.

### The Covenant

Every five years, the burden passes to a new bearer. The old bearer, now touched by the blade's whispers, is relieved of duty — a mercy enacted before full corruption takes hold.

## Structure

### The Council of Three

The ruling body that chooses bearers, decides when to deploy the weapon, and performs the grim final rites.

| Role | Duty |
|------|------|
| **The Elder** | Guides candidates, administers tests |
| **The Voice** | Speaks for the Brotherhood when contact is necessary |
| **The Mercy** | Performs the final rite when a bearer must be ended |

### The Bearer

The current wielder of the Soul Breaker. Cannot refuse the honor. Cannot abandon the blade. Serves until the next transfer or until consumed by corruption.

### The Candidates

Potential bearers, identified years in advance. They undergo rigorous testing to ensure their will is unbreakable.

## Membership Requirements

| Test | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Trial of Shadows** | Spend 24 hours in absolute darkness |
| **Trial of Whispers** | Resist mental suggestions for one week |
| **Trial of Mercy** | Make an impossible choice for the greater good |
| **Trial of Sacrifice** | Give up something irreplaceable |

## The Final Rite

When a bearer's corruption reaches dangerous levels, the Mercy enacts the Final Rite. The bearer is given a choice: accept peaceful death, or be slain and one's soul used to strengthen the blade for the next bearer.

Most bearers accept the mercy. Some fight. The Brotherhood does not speak of those who fight.
