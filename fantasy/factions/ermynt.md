# Ermynt

> "Na krew naszych przodków!" *(By the blood of our ancestors!)*

## Description

The house of Ermynt guards the *Biały Trakt* (White Road) — the ancient highway connecting the human lands to the elven realms beyond. For over a thousand years, they have maintained strict neutrality in all human conflicts, focusing instead on their sacred duty: keeping the road open and safe.

The White Road is not merely a trade route; it is humanity's link to the outside world. Elven caravans, foreign merchants, and diplomatic missions all pass through Ermynt territory. The house has become experts in diplomacy, linguistics, and the art of offending no one.

### The Neutral Guard

Ermynt's neutrality is their greatest strength and constant burden:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Human Wars** | Never take sides; offer mediation |
| **Elven Relations** | Sacred duty; protect all travelers |
| **Criminals** | Cannot judge; must escort to neutral ground |
| **Debt Disputes** | Will not involve themselves |
| **Threats to Road** | Will fight to protect traveler's safety |

This neutrality is enforced ruthlessly. Anyone who disrupts the White Road — regardless of their noble status — faces Ermynt's wrath.

### The Old Ways

Ermynt remembers the time before the Sundering, when elves and humans were allies. They preserve ancient traditions:
- Elven greetings and customs are taught to all children
- The old language (*Starodawnej*) is still spoken
- Elven wine is blessed during formal ceremonies
- Their heraldry includes both human and elven symbols

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- The burden of neutrality
- Bridging cultures
- What happens when neutrality fails?
- Ancient alliances and their consequences

### Story Hooks
- Two warring houses meet at Rozdroże — tempers flare
- An elven dignitary is threatened — Ermynt calls for help
- Someone has been killing travelers on the White Road
- A young Ermynt heir questions the neutrality doctrine

### GM Notes
> Ermynt makes excellent neutral ground for negotiations and provides access to elven connections. They are never enemies but rarely full allies.

## Trivia

- Ermynt guards are forbidden from entering human wars — even to defend their own families
- The White Road is lit by eternal torches (magical, elven-made)
- Anyone who raises arms on the road is marked an enemy by all houses
- Their neutrality has saved more lives than any army
