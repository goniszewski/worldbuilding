## Description

The lords of Daever control the fog-shrouded Dolina Mgieł (Mist Valley), a realm where visibility rarely exceeds twenty paces. For centuries, they have cultivated their most terrible weapon: the *Mleczna Mgła* (Milk Mist) — an alchemical fog that causes hallucinations, paralysis, and eventually death. Enemy armies that march into the valley emerge as hollow-eyed shells, or never emerge at all.

Daever tactics revolve entirely around their fog. Their scouts are called *Zmora* (Nightmares), warriors trained from childhood to navigate zero-visibility terrain with supernatural ease. They strike from the mist and vanish before retaliation arrives.

### The Milk Mist

The *Mleczna Mgła* is Daever's most closely guarded secret:

| Stage | Effect | Duration |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Initial Contact | Mild euphoria, disorientation | Immediate |
| Short Exposure | Hallucinations, paranoia | 1-3 hours |
| Prolonged Exposure | Paralysis, blindness | 4-6 hours |
| Extended Exposure | Death, peaceful | Hours to days |

The mist is colorless and odorless, impossible to detect until symptoms appear. The Daever have become immune through generations of exposure.

### Religion: Mother Mist

The Daever revere the mist itself as a living deity called *Matka Mgła* (Mother Mist). Unlike the Naterian Church's rigid doctrine, Mother Mist worship is personal, mystical, and deeply tied to the land.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Isolation as protection and prison
- The ethics of weapons that cannot be fought
- Truth-telling in a society that values lies
- What does it mean to worship something that kills?

### Story Hooks
- An adventurer's companion was lost to the Mist Valley years ago — and has returned changed
- Lady Morwena offers a bargain: serve the house for one year, receive a vial of Milk Mist
- The Mist Keeper has vanished, and the mist is behaving strangely
- A child born in the valley seeks to understand why they can never leave

### GM Notes
> Daever make intriguing patrons — their resources are vast, their methods are questionable, and their isolation makes them unpredictable. The Milk Mist is a plot device waiting to be misused.

## Trivia

- Daever soldiers paint their armor matte white — in the fog, they appear as floating heads
- They have won 47 wars in the valley without losing a single open-field battle
- Outsiders who enter the valley without a guide are never seen again
- The Milk Mist formula has never been stolen — those who attempt it lose their minds before they succeed
