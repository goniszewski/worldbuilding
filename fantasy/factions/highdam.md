# Highdam

> "Patrzymy z góry." *(We look from above.)*

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|

| **Headquarters** | Zapor (The Dam) |

| **Founded** | ~100 P.S. |
| **Words** | Authority, Control, Legacy |

## Description

The lords of Highdam rule from atop the great mountain dams — engineering marvels that control the flow of rivers feeding the lowlands. Their power is both literal (they can flood valleys) and political (the lowland farms depend on their controlled water flow).

For generations, Highdam was one of the most devout houses, funding temples and hosting Naterian priests. That changed fourteen years ago when **Lord Eggar Highdam** publicly abandoned the faith, declaring *"The gods watch but do not help."*

### The Schism

Lord Eggar's apostasy created a rift in the house:
- **Eggar's faction** (minority, but holds the lordship) — believes faith is superstition
- **The traditionalists** (majority among the people) — view Eggar with barely concealed contempt

The tension has never erupted into open conflict, but it simmers. Eggar's heir, **Lorian**, has become a lightning rod — his bisexuality is considered another sign of the house's moral decline by the devout, and a point of pride by those who value reform.

### Military Power

The dams themselves are Highdam's greatest weapon:

| Dam | Purpose | Threat |
|-----|---------|--------|
| **Zapor Główny** (Main Dam) | Controls Great Bessam flow | Can flood entire lowlands |
| **Zapor Wschodni** (East Dam) | Seasonal water storage | Can create flash floods |
| **Zapor Północny** (North Dam) | Drinking water for highlands | Less strategic value |

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Faith versus rationalism
- The burden of legacy
- What happens when tradition meets progress?
- Identity and acceptance

### Story Hooks
- The dams are failing, and Eggar needs help from unexpected sources
- Lorian seeks allies who will accept them as they are
- A Naterian Inquisitor arrives demanding answers
- The lowland farmers are threatening rebellion over water rights

### GM Notes
> Highdam represents a house in transition — their power is immense, but internal divisions make them vulnerable. They can be patrons, obstacles, or battlegrounds.

## Trivia

- Highdam engineers are considered the finest in the realm
- Lord Eggar keeps no religious symbols in his castle
- Lorian once saved a village from flood — they remember
- The house motto is often shortened mockingly: "We look down on you"
