# Naterian Hegemony

> A disciplined northern power balancing mage, warrior, and civic rule under a single hard-edged order.

## Overview

The Naterian Hegemony is the closest approximation to a unified Naterian nation. Unlike the scattered clan structures of other races' early histories, the Hegemony represents a **territorial bigemony** that evolved into a **tri-dominium system** — three semi-independent bodies sharing coordination at a level rivaling even the Elf Kingdom.

> Think of it as the European Union on steroids: separate flags, laws, and customs, yet cooperation so seamless it appears monolithic.

---

## Structure: Three Dominions

| Dominion | Role | Nature |
|----------|------|--------|
| **Dominium Zakkariatów** (Zakkari) | Mages | The original magical authority |
| **Dominium Agno** | Warriors | The martial power — *larger* than Zakkari |
| **Intar** | Parliament | Common representation, occupies the capital city of the same name |

### How It Works

- **Each dominion is nearly fully sovereign** — its own laws, scepter, customs
- **Intar serves as neutral ground** — a single city functioning as both capital and parliamentary seat
- **Coordination is paramount** — Naterian efficiency ensures the three bodies act in concert despite independence

The creation of Intar was deliberate: to force mages and warriors into shared governance, preventing the isolation that often fractures societies.

---

## History

### Before the Hegemony

The Naterians were once a **scattered collection of clans** — fragmented, competitive, traditional. Centuries of conflict and eventual unification brought them under the Hegemony's banner.

### Modern Era

Today, only a **negligible handful of small independent clans** remain in the far north, relics of the old ways. The Hegemony encompasses virtually all Naterian civilization.

## Military

### Iron Discipline

The Naterian army is legendary for its **discipline and intimidation factor**. Where other races rely on brute strength or magical firepower, the Naterians weaponize **rhythm and unity**.

### The Hammering

Their signature intimidation tactic:

1. **Thousands of soldiers** strike weapons in unison
2. **Swords clash against shields**
3. **Pikes and halberds hammer the ground**
4. **The combined noise is deafening**

**Effects on enemies:**
- Overwhelming psychological pressure
- Inability to focus or think clearly
- Ground tremor with sufficient numbers
- *"The earth itself seems to shake with their fury."*

## Law & Justice

### The Naterian Approach

Their legal system is **harsh but pragmatic**. Unlike human kingdoms that embrace torture as interrogation, Naterians consider it **beneath honorable warriors** — a mark of weakness, not strength.

> Some offenses result in *less* harm to the offender than human equivalents would inflict. This isn't mercy — it's efficiency. Torture produces nothing useful.

### Swift Judgment

For **obvious crimes** or **caught-in-the-act situations**:

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| Arrest | Instantaneous |
| Charges presented | Seconds |
| Verdict delivered | Within the minute |
| Execution (if applicable) | Same guard, same moment |

No appeals. No bureaucracy. No doubt.

> *"Sędzia Dredd"* — the Naterian guard becomes prosecutor, judge, and executioner in a single fluid motion. One minute from capture to death for the guilty.

### Labor Camps

For **lesser offenses** (lighter than murder, conspiracy, or treason):

- **Duration**: Can last a lifetime
- **Purpose**: Productivity over punishment — idle cells produce nothing
- **Philosophy**: Confinement without work is torture by boredom and helplessness

**Camp Organization:**
- Prisoners divided into **work groups** — never left alone
- Prevents both internal conflicts and collective rebellion
- **"Model groups"** earn privileges: bonfires, one rest day per week, easier assignments
- **Problematic groups** face harder labor and stricter conditions
