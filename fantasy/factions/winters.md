## Description

The house of Winters rules the frozen north — a land so harsh that most humans consider it uninhabitable. Through generations of adaptation, the Winters have become something more than human. They are winter.

Their fortress, *Zimowy Gród* (Winter Fortress), is carved into a glacier, invisible to outsiders until they are already inside. Their warriors hunt the great ice-beasts of the north. Their women paint themselves with white war-marks before battle. They are isolationists, survivalists, and legends.

### The Frost-Born

Generations in the frozen north have changed the Winters:

| Adaptation | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Pale Skin** | Reflects snow-glare, looks almost luminescent |
| **Cold Immunity** | Comfortable at temperatures that kill others |
| **Night Vision** | Hunt in the endless northern dark |
| **Silent Movement** | Walk on snow without sinking |
| **White Hair** | All Winters have white/silver hair by age 30 |

These changes are physical, not magical — natural selection at work.

### Ice Beast Hunters

The northern wastes contain creatures unknown to southern lands:
- *Śnieżny Lew* (Snow Lion) — Apex predator
- *Lodowy Wilk* (Ice Wolf) — Pack hunters
- *Biały Niedźwiedź* (White Bear) — Massive, territorial
- *Ciemnotek* (Darkling) — Pale subterranean horrors

Winters hunt these creatures for:
- Fur and leather
- Meat (only they can digest some)
- Glory
- Subsistence

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Survival in hostile conditions
- Isolation and identity
- Adaptation versus loss of humanity
- The allure of the unknown

### Story Hooks
- A southern party is stranded in the north — must survive until spring
- The ice beasts are migrating south — why?
- The Snow Queen offers a marriage alliance
- Something has awakened in the glacier — something old

### GM Notes
> Winters represent extreme isolation and adaptation. They make interesting allies or mysterious background figures. Their resources are limited but unique.

## Trivia

- Winters believe their ancestors made a pact with the Winter itself
- Southern diseases ravage them — they cannot visit during plague years
- Their fortress has never been taken by force
- They consider themselves "purest of human blood"
