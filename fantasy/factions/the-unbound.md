# The Unbound

> A resistance born from the belief that no bond imposed by terror can ever be called sacred.

## Overview

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Type** | Underground Resistance Network |
| **Character** | Secretive, fractured, morally compromised |
| **Scale** | Regional, with hidden cells across major trade routes |
| **Status** | Active and expanding under pressure |
| **Primary Enemy** | [The Silver Compact](silver-compact.md) |

## Description

The Unbound are not a single army, church, or kingdom. They are a web of safe houses, smugglers, deserters, rogue binders, and ordinary people who decided that the Compact's version of order costs too much. Some joined after witnessing spirit cruelty. Others lost children to Compact "recruitment." Still others simply refuse to accept that magic must belong to one ruling power.

Their name has two meanings. To outsiders, it suggests freedom from Compact law. To those inside the movement, it also means refusing the lie that spirits exist only to be used. This conviction gives the faction moral force, but not moral purity. The Unbound shelter idealists, fanatics, opportunists, and broken survivors in equal measure.

## Structure

The Unbound survive by avoiding centralized control:

| Layer | Function |
|-------|----------|
| **Cells** | Local groups handling shelter, messages, theft, and intelligence |
| **Couriers** | Carry names, routes, and coded warnings between distant allies |
| **Binders in Hiding** | Offer magical help, often reluctantly and at personal risk |
| **Silent Patrons** | Nobles, merchants, and clergy who privately fund resistance work |

No one cell knows the whole network. This keeps the movement alive, but also means rumor often travels faster than truth.

## Beliefs and Fractures

Most members agree on a few core ideas:
- Spirits are not property
- The Compact's monopoly is built on coercion
- Information matters as much as force
- Surviving long enough to expose the truth is itself a kind of victory

Beyond that, agreement breaks down quickly. Some want reform and public trials. Some want the Compact dismantled entirely. Some want revenge, not justice. The greatest tension within the Unbound lies between those who still believe institutions can be saved and those who think the realm must burn before anything honest can be built.

## Methods

The Unbound prefer methods that weaken the Compact without open war:
- Moving fugitives between safe houses
- Stealing records and exposing recruitment abuses
- Smuggling spirits, relics, or witnesses out of Compact reach
- Feeding rival powers just enough information to keep the Compact unstable

When violence happens, it is usually improvised, desperate, and costly. The movement's public myth paints it as disciplined and righteous. The reality is more human and far more dangerous.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- Resistance without purity
- Freedom bought through secrecy
- The cost of fighting necessary evils with compromised methods

### Story Hooks
- A safe house has been betrayed, but no one knows by whom
- Two Unbound cells want incompatible outcomes from the same operation
- A captured spirit remembers something that could destroy the Compact

### Adaptation Notes
- The Unbound work best as a tense alliance rather than a cleanly heroic faction
- Internal disagreement should feel as dangerous as outside persecution
