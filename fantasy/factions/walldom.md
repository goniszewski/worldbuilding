## Description

The lords of Walldom have stood as unyielding guardians for over a millennium, their ancestral seat commanding the critical chokepoint known as Brama Torra. They are renowned for their iron discipline, legendary patience, and an almost supernatural ability to resist corruption and fear.

The current lord, **Lord Korn Walldom**, is rumored to be over three centuries old, having undergone the infamous *Ritual Krwi* (Black Blood Ritual) — a dark pact said to slow aging at terrible cost. Many whisper that the real power behind the house has quietly shifted to his descendant, **Zygfryd**, a cunning strategist who manages the house's political affairs while Korn remains a feared but distant figurehead.

### Culture & Customs

- **Fortitude** is the highest virtue — fear is considered a sickness of the spirit
- **Blood oaths** are sacred and unbreakable
- Gate duty is considered **holy service**, not mere labor
- The house maintains an elite guard called *Strażnicy Bram* (Gate Keepers)

### The Black Blood Ritual

The legendary ritual that grants extended life is forbidden by the Naterian Church. Its exact nature is unknown, but witnesses describe:
- Darkened veins that pulse with unnatural rhythm
- Immunity to cold and pain
- An unsettling calm in the face of death
- Each use shortens the subject's humanity

## History

The house was founded during the *Unification Wars* when humanity needed guardians at the mountain passes. The Walldoms proved so effective that they were granted hereditary lordship — a title that has passed through twenty-seven generations.

Notable events:
- **The Siege of 1,000 Days** (412 P.S.) — Repelled a Nater invasion; the gates never opened
- **The Black Blood Controversy** (489 P.S.) — Church attempted to investigate Korn's longevity; envoys were politely refused entry
- **The Trade Dispute** (501 P.S.) — Skjor accused Walldom of taxing their merchant caravans unfairly

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- The cost of eternal vigilance
- Duty versus personal desire
- What does it mean to be human when you stop aging?
- The tension between tradition and pragmatism

### Story Hooks
- A young gate guard suspects corruption in the upper ranks
- Lord Korn offers a desperate adventurer the Black Blood Ritual... for a price
- The Naterian Church sends an Inquisitor to investigate Walldom's "heretical practices"
- Zygfryd seeks to arrange a marriage that would unite two rival houses

### GM Notes
> Walldom makes excellent patron figures for parties seeking honor and glory — or those who need access through the northern passes. Their enemies are numerous but respect their word.

## Trivia

- A saying exists: *"Gdy Walldom pada, padną wszystkie"* (When Walldom falls, all fall)
- They have never surrendered their gates in 1,200 years
- The Black Blood Ritual is said to run in certain bloodlines — not all who attempt it survive
