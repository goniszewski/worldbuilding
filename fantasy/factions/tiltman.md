## Description

The Tiltman archers are legends — the finest marksmen in the known world, dwelling in the Windy Hills where the constant gusts have forced generations to develop impossible accuracy. They rarely leave their highlands, rarely admit outsiders, and rarely need to: their arrows have ended more conflicts than entire armies.

For over a millennium, Tiltman has maintained a policy of deliberate isolation. They do not seek trade, do not participate in politics, and do not forgive insults. Their territory is marked by *"Pamiątki"* (Memorials) — perfectly spaced markers where trespassers were killed, left as warnings.

### The Wind-Born

Training begins at age five. By ten, children can hit a moving bird at fifty paces. By adulthood, Tiltman archers demonstrate abilities that seem supernatural:

| Skill | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Wiatrowy Strzał** (Wind Shot) | Adjusts for wind in real-time, never misses |
| **Mgławy Cel** (Fog Target) | Hits what cannot be seen, guided by sound |
| **Deszcz Strzał** (Rain of Arrows) | Rapid fire that saturates areas |
| **Orli wzrok** (Eagle Eyes) | Can spot movement at impossible distances |

### The Lonely Vigil

Tiltman maintains ancient agreements with [[factions/ermynt|Ermynt]] — the two houses share intelligence on threats to the realm, communicating through signal fires. Beyond this, they trust no one.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- The cost of perfection
- Isolation as survival strategy
- When loners must engage with the world
- The weight of legend

### Story Hooks
- A Tiltman archer is exiled — and seeks a new purpose
- The Windy Hills are being invaded — only outsiders can help
- Someone wants to hire Tiltman for an assassination
- A sacred relic was taken from their highlands years ago — they want it back

### GM Notes
> Tiltman are perfect for parties seeking a powerful but reluctant ally. Their archers are devastating in combat but have no interest in treasure or glory — only honor and the wind.

## Trivia

- Tiltman archers can hit a target's eye at 300 paces
- They carve their arrows from sacred wind-oak — taking one is a death sentence
- Outsiders who enter the hills without permission are given one warning
- Their arrows are worth more than gold in some markets
