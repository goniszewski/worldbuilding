# The Whispering Ruins

## Hook

**Setup**: Lyra receives a coded message from her contact, **Mira the Cartographer**, who was investigating the Whispering Ruins:
> "Found the third door. It's not what they said. Come alone — or don't come. -M"

Mira was due in Verath three days ago. She's always reliable. Something's wrong.

**The Pitch**: Investigate the ruins, find Mira, discover what the Compact is hiding — all while competing with Compact enforcers who received the same tip through different channels.

## The Ruins: Overview

The Whispering Ruins are the remains of **Aetheron**, a major city destroyed in the Sundering. Located in the northern mountains, access is difficult. The ruins "whisper" — audible voices with no clear source, speaking fragments of conversation from before the Sundering.

## Key Locations

### 1. The Broken Gate
**Entry point**: Massive stone archway, half-collapsed.
- Compact enforcers have established a perimeter (5 guards)
- Alternative: Climbing path for stealthy approach
- **Encounter**: Guards may be bribed, distracted, or avoided

### 2. The Square of Echoes
**Before**: Central marketplace
**Now**: Overgrown with crystalline plants (Sundering magic residue)
- The whispers are loudest here
- **Mystery**: Whispers include arguments about "the binding" and "what we've done"
- **Clue**: One whisper mentions a "third door behind the third fountain"

### 3. The Three Fountains
**Three fountains in a row**, heavily damaged.
- Third fountain has a hidden mechanism (DC 15 Investigation)
- Reveals stairs down to **The Sealed Archives**

### 4. The Sealed Archives *(Main Location)*
Pre-Sundering library, protected by wards that have partially failed.

**Rooms:**
- **Central Hall**: Collapsed shelving, scattered documents
- **Binder's Study**: Intact room with working desk, spirit-binding research
- **The Third Door**: Hidden vault behind a false wall

**The Third Door Contents:**
- Documents proving Compact knew spirits were sentient before the monopoly
- A partially bound Storm spirit (dying, needs immediate help or death)
- **The Real Secret**: Map showing location of *The Silence* (Void spirit)

### 5. Mira's Last Camp
Outside the ruins, partially dismantled:
- Signs of struggle
- Compact supply pack (she was taken by enforcers)
- Her journal: Final entry suggests she found something "worse than wild binding"

## NPCs

### Mira the Cartographer
- **Status**: Captured by Compact enforcers
- **Location**: Being transported to Silver Woods HQ
- **If Rescued**: Will share everything she knows; needs medical attention (spirit exposure)

### Compact Enforcer Team
**Led by**: Enforcer Sable (see NPCs)
- 4 regular enforcers
- Mission: Retrieve documents, secure anything spirit-related
- **Orders**: Eliminate witnesses if necessary

### The Dying Spirit
**Name**: Unknown
**Tier**: Storm
**State**: Partially bound, fading
- Will communicate weakly if approached
- Payment: "Free me... or kill me... please"
- **Player Choice**: This determines the spirit's fate

## Complications

| Complication | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| Compact reinforcements | 1d4 more enforcers arrive if shots fired |
| The Whispers intensify | Spirits of the dead growing agitated |
| Lyra recognizes a name | Former Compact instructor mentioned in whispers — he's still alive? |
| Mira's trail | Leads somewhere unexpected (GM discretion) |

## Resolution Options

| Outcome | Trigger | Consequences |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| **Full Success** | Documents secured, Mira rescued, spirit freed or killed | Party gains major leverage against Compact |
| **Partial Success** | Some documents, Mira saved | Compact knows they were here |
| **Failure** | Captured by enforcers | Must escape; Compact gains leverage |
| **Dark Choice** | Party keeps spirit's essence for themselves | Power now, corruption later |

## Rewards

- **Documents**: Proof of Compact deception (political power)
- **Spirit Essence**: If harvested (morally questionable)
- **Pre-Sundering Artifacts**: Research value, collector interest
- **The Map**: Location of The Silence — campaign-changing information

## Appendix: Quick Stats

### Enforcer (Standard)
- HP: 30
- AC: 14 (leather + minor wards)
- Abilities: Spirit bolt (1d8), binding chains (restraining)
- Tactics: Fight as unit; one calls for backup before engaging

### Dying Spirit
- HP: 5
- State: Fading; cannot attack
- Action: Can share one piece of information before dying or being freed

## Session Flow

```
Session 1:
├── Travel to Ruins
├── Bypass/Deal with Enforcer perimeter
├── Explore Square of Echoes
└── Discover the Fountains puzzle

Session 2:
├── Enter Sealed Archives
├── Navigate whispers/dangers
├── Encounter Mira's situation
└── The Third Door revelation + escape
```

## GM Notes

**Theme**: The truth is complicated. The Compact isn't cartoonishly evil — the documents show they *struggled* with what they discovered. They chose control over honesty.

**Tone**: Mysterious, tense, morally gray. The whispers provide atmosphere and foreshadowing without giving direct answers.

**If Players Hesitate**: Mira's life is on the line. Time pressure from enforcers keeps momentum.
