## Description

The **[[pierwsza|Pierwsza]]** was the last ruler of the [[Czyści|Czyści]] during the **[[epoka-światła|Epoka Światła]]** (Age of Light). Unlike her predecessors — who maintained peace and tradition — she was characterized by **curiosity and inquisitiveness**.

This thirst for knowledge led her to investigate a strange element discovered in an underground cave. Scholars believe it was either a large deposit of **[[Blue Ore|Błękitna Ruda]]** or one of the legendary **[[Czarny Kamień Ura|Czarnych Kamieni Ura]]** — both saturated with pure magic.

Her prolonged exposure to magical energy transformed her fundamentally.

---

## Transformation

Exposed to magic, [[pierwsza|Pierwsza's]] soul was:
- **Blessed** (or tainted, depending on interpretation)
- **Connected to the [[abyss|Otchłań]]** (Void) — a multi-layered dimension

Within the [[abyss|Otchłań]]:
1. **[[Aspekty|Aspekty]]** — beings of immense power with indeterminate consciousness — imprinted on her soul
2. They granted her the ability to pass into their dimension after death
3. Fragments tore from each [[Aspekty|Aspekt]], mixing to create **[[Demony|Demony]]**

The [[Aspekty|Aspekty]] and [[Demony|demony]] nested within her soul began to speak to her.

### The Madness

According to some records, [[pierwsza|Pierwsza]] went **mad**. The voices of the [[abyss|Otchłań]] changed her. Whether this was possession, inspiration, or mental breakdown remains debated by scholars.

---

## Death and Legacy

[[pierwsza|Pierwsza]] died in childbirth — but bore not one child, but **three**:

| Child | Nature |
|-------|--------|
| **[[torr|Torr]]** | Dark, scaly, harsh yet honorable |
| **[[daraster|Daraster]]** | Bold, ambitious, wicked |
| **[[lesandra|Lesandra]]** | Tall, pale, balanced, could control magic |

She broke tradition by not naming a successor from among her people. Instead, she commanded them to obey her child.

All three children inherited her ability to contact the [[abyss|Otchłań]] — and possibly, her madness.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **Curiosity's price** — seeking knowledge led to catastrophe
- **Madness or enlightenment** — was she blessed or cursed?
- **Maternal sacrifice** — her death gave birth to new races
- **The voice within** — entities whispering from beyond

### Story Hooks
- Heretics claim [[pierwsza|Pierwsza]] was not mad but achieved enlightenment
- A mage seeks to recreate her transformation to gain Aspect-level power
- Descendants experience echoes of her "madness" during magical contact
- The cave where magic was first discovered may still exist
