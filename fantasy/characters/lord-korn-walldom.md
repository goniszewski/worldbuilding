# Lord Korn Walldom

> An ancient lord whose name survives because fear and ritual refuse to let it die.

## Description

Lord Korn Walldom is a walking contradiction — a man who should have died centuries ago but instead stands eternal vigil at Brama Torra. His black hair has long since faded to iron-grey, his eyes hold the weight of impossible memories, and his voice carries the resonance of someone who has said goodbye to too many generations.

The Black Blood Ritual transformed him long ago, slowing his aging to a crawl while preserving his mind and strength. The cost is written in his darkened veins, his unsettling calm, and the way animals flee from his presence.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Tall, lean, whipcord muscles
- **Hair**: Iron-grey, kept short in military style
- **Eyes**: Pale grey, almost colorless — said to see lies
- **Distinguishing Features**: Dark veins visible at temples, always cold to touch

### Personality

Korn speaks rarely, and each word carries the weight of centuries. He has outlived friends, enemies, wives, and children. Nothing surprises him anymore — which makes him either the wisest man in the realm or the most broken.

> *"I have watched empires rise and fall. Your concerns are... noted."*

He is not cruel, but he has lost the capacity for casual warmth. He performs his duty because it is all that remains.

## Skills & Abilities

- **Combat**: Master swordsman — has fought in wars that are now history
- **Political Savvy**: Intuitive understanding of power (3 centuries of observation)
- **The Black Blood**: Immunity to cold, pain, and fear; accelerated healing
- **Gate Mastery**: Can assess threats with preternatural accuracy

## Equipment

- **Wieczny Strażnik** (Eternal Guard) — Ancient sword, said to have been blessed by a forgotten god
- **Zbroja Przodków** (Ancestor Armor) — Black plate that has not needed repair in 200 years
- **Pieczęć Walldom** — The lord's seal, carries emergency powers

## Relationships

- **[Zygfryd Walldom](zygfrd-walldom.md)**: His descendant and the true political operator; Korn trusts him completely
- **The Church**: Knows what he is; cannot prove it
- **His People**: Respected but not loved — he is too distant for love
- **Other Lords**: Feared and avoided

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- The burden of immortality
- Duty as substitute for purpose
- What remains when everything mortal is lost?
- The ethics of the Black Blood Ritual

### Story Hooks
- A young guard seeks Korn's wisdom before a seemingly impossible battle
- The Church finally has proof — and demands he submit to judgment
- Korn reveals he knows a secret that could change everything
- Someone offers him release from his eternal vigil

### GM Notes
> Korn should feel ancient and weary, yet dangerous. He is not a monster, but he is no longer entirely human. His presence should make players feel the weight of centuries.
