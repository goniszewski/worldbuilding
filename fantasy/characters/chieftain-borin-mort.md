# Chieftain Borin Mort (Wódz Borin Mort)

> A battle-scarred chieftain whose age has only sharpened the danger in him.

## Description

Chieftain Borin Mort has led the Mort clans for forty years — longer than any chieftain in living memory. His body is a canvas of scars, each one a story of survival, battle, or ritual. His axe has killed more enemies than most armies. His voice has rallied warriors through defeats that should have broken them.

He is old by Mort standards, kept alive by the blessing of the old gods and sheer stubborn will. Some say the gods have marked him for greatness; others whisper that he should have died decades ago and clings to power beyond his time.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Massive, barrel-chested, scarred
- **Hair**: Braided white, nearly to his waist
- **Eyes**: Wild, fever-bright
- **Distinguishing Features**: 
  - Wolf-claw scars across face (marks of his Trial of Fire)
  - Missing two fingers on left hand
  - Tribal tattoos covering 80% of body

### Personality

Borin is everything a Mort chieftain should be: fierce, loyal, unpredictable, and utterly unafraid of death. He fights with the *Szał Boży* (Divine Fury) better than anyone alive, entering berserker rage at will.

But beneath the fury is a cunning strategist. He has survived forty years as chieftain not just through combat prowess but through political acumen within the clan confederation.

> *"The gods gave us two gifts: fists and fury. Everything else is weakness."*

He respects strength above all else and despises weakness, treachery, and false pride.

## Skills & Abilities

- **Combat**: Legendary — fought in 47 battles, never defeated
- **Berserker Rage**: Can enter Szał Boży at will, ignoring pain and fear
- **Leadership**: Inspirational; warriors fight harder knowing he's with them
- **Shamanic Connection**: Receives occasional visions from the old gods

## Equipment

- **Topór Przodków** (Ancestor's Axe) — Ancient ceremonial axe, said to be blessed
- **Skóra Wilka** (Wolf Skin) — Cloak made from the beast he killed for his Trial
- **Runy Bojowe** (Battle Runes) — Ritual scars that provide minor protection

## Relationships

- **[Hera of Vengeance](hera-of-vengeance.md)**: His champion; they have a complicated relationship
- **The Five Clans**: Rules through consensus, not decree
- **[Tiel](../factions/tiel.md)**: Mortal enemies; the valley dispute is personal
- **The chieftain's line**: Three wives over the years; all dead of childbirth

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Leadership in violent societies
- The cost of endless war
- Tradition versus wisdom
- When the old chief must step aside

### Story Hooks
- Borin is dying — the clans will war for succession
- A young warrior challenges his authority
- The valley dispute with Tiel is about to escalate
- A priest of the old gods offers Borin a vision of the future

### GM Notes
> Borin should feel ancient and dangerous, like a wolf too old to hunt but still able to bite. He is honorable by Mort standards and ruthless by any other.
