## Description

Once, the Czars were minor nobility — useful, forgettable, safe. Then Lonen's grandmother decided they would be more than that. Three generations of careful cultivation later, Lonen sits on the Small Council, and every lord in the realm owes him something.

He is the first common-born (or nearly so) to serve as Royal Advisor. This makes him hated by old nobility, feared by rivals, and indispensable to the Crown. He intends to remain indispensable forever.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Medium, unremarkable
- **Hair**: Dark, immaculately maintained
- **Eyes**: Brown, warm-seeming, utterly calculating
- **Distinguishing Features**: 
  - Silver cufflinks (house badge)
  - Always impeccably dressed
  - Never wears armor, even when advised to

### Personality

Lonen is charming, patient, and completely without conscience. He does not think in terms of good or evil — only win or lose. He is not cruel for pleasure, but he is ruthless for advantage. If destroying someone serves his goals, he destroys them without hesitation.

He genuinely believes he is making the realm stronger — that his manipulation prevents wars, his information stops assassinations, and his control maintains stability. Whether this justifies his methods is a question he never asks.

> *"I don't need to be loved. I need to be necessary. Love fades. Necessity is forever."*

He collects secrets like others collect art, and his memory for favors owed is perfect and unforgiving.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Power without morality
- The corruption of control
- Whether good ends justify evil means
- The loneliness of those at the top

### Story Hooks
- A party is offered a contract by Czar — simple job, terrible implications
- Someone is trying to assassinate him — or is it one of his own agents?
- His past comes back to haunt him
- He offers something no one expected: genuine help

### GM Notes
> Lonen is the ultimate manipulator — useful when aligned, dangerous when crossed. He should always seem friendly, never threatening, until the moment he destroys you. He is evil, but efficient evil.
