# Daraster

> The second child of the First One, remembered through two bloodlines and the damage they cost.

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Category** | Character |
| **Race** | The Cleansed (First Generation) |
| **Sex** | Male |
| **Status** | Deceased |
| **Period** | ~10,000–15,000 years ago |

## Description

**Daraster** was the second of the First One's three children. Unlike his brother [Torr's](torr.md) dark, scaled appearance, less is recorded of Daraster's physical form — the legends focus on his **nature**.

### Nature

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Bold** | Unafraid of risk or consequence |
| **Wicked** | Willing to act against custom and morality |
| **Open** | Most receptive to change and new ideas |
| **Ambitious** | Driven to expand and claim power |

His nature is often contrasted with [Torr's](torr.md) rigid honor — Daraster achieved through cunning and desire what his brother sought through strength.

## The Civil War

Daraster played a pivotal role in the conflict:

1. **Alliance with [Lesandra](lesandra.md)** — United against [Torr](torr.md) when tensions escalated
2. **Marriage** — Wed his sister in defiance of tradition, provoking [Torr's](torr.md) fury
3. **Departure** — After [Torr](torr.md) left, he and [Lesandra](lesandra.md) departed the devastated island in the opposite direction

## Legacy: Dual Bloodlines

Daraster's complex romantic life created two distinct racial legacies:

### With [Lesandra](lesandra.md): The Elves

Their union produced children with **strong magical blood** — inheriting Lesandra's control and Daraster's ambition.

| Trait | Manifestation |
|-------|---------------|
| Elven purity | Did not intermix with other races |
| Magical affinity | Strong connection to magic |
| Pale complexion | Children resembled Lesandra |

Elves consider themselves **superior** due to their "pure" lineage — Daraster's ambitious nature combined with Lesandra's balanced, magical blood.

### Outside the Marriage: Humans

Daraster was **unfaithful** to Lesandra, fathering numerous children outside their union.

| Trait | Manifestation |
|-------|---------------|
| Mixed heritage | Diverse appearances |
| Adaptability | No fixed racial "type" |
| Numerical strength | Large population from many bloodlines |
| "Middle ground" | Belong everywhere and nowhere |

Humans are thus the most **varied** of the races — carrying fragments of all three original bloodlines (through later intermixing).

## The Question of Fidelity

Scholars debate why Daraster was unfaithful:

| Theory | Argument |
|--------|----------|
| **Inherent nature** | He was simply wicked by disposition |
| **Bloodline strengthening** | Sought diverse children to expand his influence |
| **Magical influence** | Demons/aspects drove him to chaotic action |
| **Lesandra's dominance** | He chafed under her stronger magical blood |

Some even suggest his infidelities were an attempt to create children resembling [Torr](torr.md) — to reunite the bloodlines — but this remains speculation.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **The progenitor of division** — his choices split one race into two
- **Legitimacy vs. love** — marriage vs. passion, heirs vs. bastards
- **Ambition's cost** — his drive for power created lasting divisions
- **Human diversity** — as the "mixed" race, Humans embody his chaotic nature

### Story Hooks
- A Human discovers their bloodline carries dormant magical potential
- Elven supremacists hunt "impure" Elves who share Human ancestry
- Scholars debate whether Daraster's infidelity was planned or compulsive
- A lost record of all his illegitimate children — and what happened to them
