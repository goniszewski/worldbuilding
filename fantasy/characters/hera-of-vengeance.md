> Mort's undefeated champion, feared as much for what she enjoys as for what she wins.

## Description

In a culture of warriors, Hera of Vengeance stands above them all. For twenty years, she has been undefeated in single combat — a record that has become legend. She earned her name when, as a young warrior, she killed the man who murdered her family, then killed his killer, and his killer's killer — seven in total, all for *zemsta* (revenge).

She is the leader of the *Klan Wilczycy* (She-Wolf Clan), one of the Five Clans, and considered by many to be the true warrior heart of Mort. Chieftain Borin respects her, fears her, and needs her — a dangerous combination.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Tall, athletic, scarred
- **Hair**: Shaved on one side, braided on the other
- **Eyes**: Burning amber
- **Distinguishing Features**: Seven tally-marks tattooed on her neck (one per revenge kill); white warpaint worn as armor; massive double-headed axe

### Personality

Hera is everything Mort values: fierce, direct, loyal to her clan, and contemptuous of weakness. She does not seek power for its own sake but will not step aside for those she considers unworthy.

She has no patience for politics, diplomacy, or compromise. If something angers her, she says so — usually with her axe nearby. This bluntness has made her enemies, but also earned her respect.

> *"I don't want your war. I want your champion. If you send anyone less, I'll be disappointed."*

## Skills & Abilities

- **Combat**: Master — undefeated in single combat for 20 years
- **Berserker Rage**: Among the strongest seen — she has killed wearing wounds that should have killed any normal warrior
- **Intimidation**: Can silence a room by walking in
- **She-Wolf Leadership**: Commands 200 female warriors

## Equipment

- **Siedem Zemsty** (Seven Revenges) — Her double-headed axe, named for her kills
- **Zbroja Bólu** (Armor of Pain) — Iron armor deliberately notched to cause pain (focuses her rage)
- **Wilcza Czapka** (Wolf Hood) — Trophy from her Trial of Fire beast

## Relationships

- **[[characters/chieftain-borin-mort|Chieftain Borin Mort]]**: Complicated — respects him, challenges him, needs him
- **Klan Wilczycy**: She is their champion and voice
- **[[factions/tiel|Tiel]]**: Personal hatred — a Tiel mining accident killed her first love
- **The Other Clans**: Respected but watched warily

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Female power in patriarchal violence culture
- Revenge and justice
- The cost of being the best
- Finding something beyond war

### Story Hooks
- Hera seeks allies for a personal vendetta against Tiel
- Someone challenges her champion status
- A young female warrior seeks her mentorship

Hera is a warrior first and always. She can be a powerful ally or terrifying enemy. Her revenge arc against Tiel can drive campaigns.
