# Archmage of Ur

*[Antagonist]*

> *"I feel as if I'm constantly drowning in something bottomless. But that something needs a conscious mind. It needs me."*

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Category** | Character / Antagonist |
| **Type** | Immortal Wizard / Demiurge |
| **Status** | Undead (soul bound to the Void) |
| **Origin** | Ur, ancient kingdom of mages |

## Description

### The Fall

The Archmage of Ur was once the most powerful mage in the world. He discovered the power of the [[items/black-stones-of-ur|Black Stones of Ur]] and was the only one capable of mastering them.

However, in pursuing immortality, he committed a terrible mistake — **he merged his soul with a chaotic entity** from the [[lore/void|Void]], becoming independent of the body.

### Death

He was killed by the [[characters/mighty-eight|Mighty Eight]] — a group of eight of the world's most powerful heroes. However, having already completed the soul-merging ritual with the Void, **he survived**.

### Current State

The Archmage now exists as an **immeasurably powerful, yet incomplete being**:
- Immortal, independent of the body
- Defiled soul, stained with chaos
- Loss of senses — feels as if **constantly drowning**
- Lack of full control over what he has become

### Ultimate Goal

The Archmage noticed that the Void (chaos) needs a **conscious, orderly mind** to give it meaning. Throughout the game, he has been trying to master what he has become.

**Two paths:**
1. **With the hero's help** — they will create a new god together. The Archmage watches over the world, ensuring eternal peace.
2. **Without the hero** — he remains in the soul-rending cradle of chaos.

## The Cult of the Archmage

### Structure

| Level | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Followers | Cultists | Believe in their lord's return |
| Fanatics | Zealots | Suicide bombers, terrorists |
| Commanders | Archpriests | Know the truth about immortality |

### Doctrine

The cult believes in the **Revelation** — the moment when the Archmage fully returns to the world. They prepare for this through:
- Collecting [[items/blue-ore|blue ore]]
- Searching for [[items/black-stones-of-ur|Black Stones]]
- Sabotaging the [[factions/silver-compact|Silver Compact]] from within

## The Offer to the Hero

At the climactic moment, the Archmage contacts the hero:

### The Proposition

> *"I see similar ambition in you. We are the same — we crave something more. Help me understand what I have become. Together, we will create something new. You will become my prophet, and I — your god."*

### Consequences

**Acceptance:**
- The hero becomes immortal (in part)
- Souls connected by "a delicate, yet unbreakable thread"
- Shared vision: eternal peace under divine control
- End of freedom, beginning of something... new

**Refusal:**
- The Archmage remains in chaos
- But he still exists — the war continues
- Need for ultimate destruction (is it even possible?)

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Character

- **Charismatic** — capable of persuasion
- **Honest** — doesn't lie, but omits truth
- **Tired** — eternal drowning exhausts him
- **Similar to the hero** — the temptation is effective

### The Key Dilemma

This is **not an easy choice between good and evil**. Both options have consequences:
- With the god = order, but loss of freedom
- Without the god = freedom, but eternal war

### Preparation

1. **Scatter hints** about the Archmage's nature throughout the game
2. **Show his suffering** — he's not evil, he's torn apart
3. **Build parallels** between him and the hero
4. **Leave doors open** — the game can end in various ways
