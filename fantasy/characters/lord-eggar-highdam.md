> A river lord whose public break with faith became both scandal and doctrine.

## Description

Fourteen years ago, Lord Eggar Highdam stood before his court, removed his holy symbol, and declared: *"The gods watch but do not help. We have relied on their empty gaze long enough."*

The realm has not been the same since.

Eggar was once the most devout lord in the highlands — a patron of temples, a defender of faith. His apostasy shocked everyone, most of all his wife, who died three years later, some say of a broken heart. Now Eggar rules through cold logic, treating his people as resources to be managed rather than souls to be saved.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Tall, gaunt, severe
- **Hair**: Completely white, kept short
- **Eyes**: Grey, analytical
- **Distinguishing Features**: 
  - No holy symbol (deliberately absent)
  - Wears engineer's robes, not lordly finery
  - Hands always stained with ink or oil

### Personality

Eggar is brilliant, cold, and increasingly isolated. He genuinely believes he abandoned faith for reason — that the gods are comfortable lies, and only engineering provides real answers. This belief has cost him his wife, his people's respect, and his own peace of mind.

He does not hate the gods — that would require emotional investment. He simply *dismisses* them, which is somehow worse.

> *"Faith is a dam built without foundations. Eventually, the water breaks through. I choose to build with stone."*

He is obsessed with making Highdam self-sufficient — dams, water, food, defense. He does not see that a house is more than infrastructure.

## Skills & Abilities

- **Engineering**: Master — has redesigned all three dams
- **Leadership**: Effective but cold — people follow orders, not loyalty
- **Writing**: Author of several treatises on "Rational Governance"
- **Political Survival**: Has resisted Church pressure for 14 years

## Equipment

- **Praca i Rozum** (Work and Reason) — His manifesto, taught to all Highdam administrators
- **Architekta Narzędzia** (Architect's Tools) — Fine engineering instruments
- **Brama Studni** (Well Gate) — Emergency override for all three dams

## Relationships

- **[[factions/highdam|Lorian Highdam]]**: His heir, whose bisexuality he privately accepts but publicly cannot defend
- **The Naterian Church**: Hostile — they want his head
- **His People**: Divided — half follow his reforms, half pray for his downfall
- **His Dead Wife**: Haunts his dreams

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Faith versus rationalism
- The cost of conviction
- Family duty versus personal belief
- When being right means being alone

### Story Hooks
- An Inquisitor arrives demanding Eggar's submission
- The dams are failing — and Eggar needs help from the "old ways"
- Lorian confronts Eggar about his failures
- A vision of the dead wife suggests she might have been right

### GM Notes
> Eggar is tragic — his rationality has made him lonely, and his certainty has cost him everything soft. He is not evil, but he is broken. This makes him sympathetic and frustrating.
