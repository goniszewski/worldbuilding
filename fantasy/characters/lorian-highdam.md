# Lorian Highdam

> An heir pulled between inherited duty, public scandal, and a future he did not choose cleanly.

## Description

Lorian Highdam was born to lead a house in crisis. His father abandoned the faith, his mother died of grief, and he — the heir — is himself a scandal. His bisexuality is considered another sign of Highdam's decline by the devout: *"First the lord abandons the gods, now the heir abandons nature."*

He endures this with grace he does not feel, balancing his father's cold logic with his mother's buried faith, his own desires with political necessity.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Medium, athletic, handsome
- **Hair**: Dark brown with a single streak of white (mother's heritage)
- **Eyes**: Blue, warm when he wants to be
- **Distinguishing Features**: 
  - Wears his mother's holy symbol hidden under his shirt
  - Has a knight's bearing but scholar's hands
  - Smile that never quite reaches his eyes

### Personality

Lorian is kind in a way his father never learned to be. He understands people, genuinely cares about their suffering, and wants to reunite the fractured house. But he is also shrewd — he knows he cannot simply restore the faith without betraying his father.

He is caught between:
- **His father**: Who he loves but cannot follow
- **His mother's memory**: Whose faith he shares but cannot openly practice
- **The people**: Who need hope he cannot give
- **His own heart**: Which beats for both men and women, but must marry a woman to secure the succession

> *"I am the bridge between two sides of a chasm. If I fall, Highdam falls with me."*

## Skills & Abilities

- **Diplomacy**: Natural talent — inherited his mother's warmth
- **Political Awareness**: Learned from watching his father's mistakes
- **Combat**: Trained knight; competent but not great
- **Engineering**: Knows enough to maintain the dams

## Equipment

- **Strój Godowy** (Duty's Armor) — Formal armor for state occasions

## Relationships

- **[[factions/highdam|Lord Eggar Highdam]]**: Complicated love — they respect but do not understand each other
- **The Traditionalists**: Secret sympathies — but cannot openly support them
- **The Reformists**: Supports their goals, not their methods
- **His Mother's Memory**: The ghost that guides him

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Living between worlds
- Identity and expectation
- Bridging irreconcilable differences
- Finding one's own path

### Story Hooks
- Lorian seeks allies who accept him as he is
- The traditionalists approach him with a proposal
- He falls in love with someone forbidden
- A crisis forces him to choose between father and faith

### GM Notes
> Lorian is the heart of Highdam's story — the character players can invest in. He is fundamentally good but trapped by circumstances. His journey is about finding a way forward that doesn't require betrayal.
