# Zara Siwerna

*[Infiltrator, Eridann Campaign]*

> *"Everyone has a secret. The trick is knowing which one they'll trade for yours."*

## Basics

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Race | Half-elf (Human mother, unknown elven father) |
| Sex | Female |
| Age | 26 |
| Nationality | Eridanni (by birth and documents; papers forged twice since) |
| Affiliation | Silver Compact |

## Description

### Appearance

Zara moves through a room the way water moves through cracks — finding every gap, taking the path of least resistance, and emerging somewhere unexpected. She is handsome rather than beautiful, with the angular features of her elven blood softened by a jaw that belongs to the human side of her heritage. Her eyes shift between pale grey and pale green depending on the light, and she uses this ambiguity deliberately — sometimes a merchant's daughter, sometimes a wood-elf's child, never quite what she seems.

She dresses well but never ostentatiously: good fabrics in muted colours, jewellery that looks expensive but can be pawned quickly, hair worn long or braided as the situation demands. Her hands are her most expressive feature — gesturing, steadying a wine glass, palming a coin, touching a shoulder. She has learned that people remember touch long after they've forgotten words.

A thin scar runs along her left forearm, hidden under her sleeve. She does not talk about it.

### Personality

Zara is **funny, fast, and never quite present**. She deflects with charm, redirects conversations with a question, and has an unsettling ability to make people feel like they're being genuinely seen — which is exactly why they're not watching her anymore. She laughs easily, forgets names deliberately, and asks follow-up questions about things she already knows to create the impression of genuine interest.

Underneath this performance, Zara is **precise, anxious, and deeply transactional**. She trusts information over people, loyalty over sentiment, and survival over honour. She does not believe in causes — she believes in leverage. The Silver Compact interests her because the right conspiracy, in the right place, is worth more than gold.

Her relationships are **managed**: she decides what she gives, to whom, and when. Genuine connection unsettles her more than danger.

### Backstory

Zara was born in the upper district of a city she refuses to name — the daughter of a minor human merchant family with a quiet elven father who was never quite acknowledged and never quite gone. She grew up fluent in two worlds and trusted by neither. The other half-elven children called her a half-breed. The human children called her an elf. By twelve, she had learned to be whatever the room needed her to be.

Her mother died when she was fifteen — a death that looked natural at the time and has since come to look anything but. By then Zara had already been running small cons for two years. By eighteen she was a professional: pickpocket, con artist, messenger, and finally, discreetly, an information broker. She sold secrets to anyone who could pay. She told herself this was neutrality.

At twenty-two she stumbled — not her words — into information that connected a string of deaths in the merchant class to a single, unnamed conspiracy. Her mother's name was on a list she should never have found. She began investigating.

Three years of dead ends, false leads, and one near-fatal encounter later, she found the Silver Compact. She joined not because she believed in it, but because it was the only organisation she had encountered that was also asking the right questions. She is still not sure she made the right choice. She suspects the Compact is not sure either.

## The Truth About Her Loyalty

Zara joined the Silver Compact with one agenda: access. Documents. Faces. Names. She was collecting answers about her mother, and the Compact was in the way of those answers.

She has never told the Compact this.

What Zara did not expect was that the Compact's questions and her own would begin to overlap. That Velan's obsessive search for truth and Damara's grief-fuelled priesthood would start to feel less like data points and more like people she recognised. That she would — despite every instinct — begin to care whether they lived or died.

This terrifies her more than the conspiracy.

The moment that will define her arc: **the Compact discovers she withheld critical information for personal advantage and confronts her.** She has a choice — leave, protect her secrets, survive — or stay and be honest for the first time in her adult life. The Compact does not make this easy. Neither does she.

### Her Mother's Death — What She Knows

Zara has a single, fragmentary piece of information: her mother was poisoned by someone with access to a specific alchemical compound that is difficult to acquire outside certain noble households. The same compound, she has reason to believe, appears in at least two other deaths connected to the conspiracy. This is not proof. It is barely a thread. But it is hers.

## Skills & Abilities

- **Infiltration**: Zara can enter almost any social space undetected, unnoticed, and underestimated. She reads room dynamics within minutes and identifies every exit, every drunk, and every guard who isn't paying attention.
- **Information extraction**: She can extract more from a person in ten minutes of casual conversation than most interrogation specialists manage in an hour. People tell her things because she makes them feel safe, clever, or important — sometimes all three.
- **Deception & sleight of hand**: Card tricks, coin drops, forged documents, forged identities, forged smiles. She is careful about which one she uses when.
- **Escape**: Zara runs first and fights never. She has never won a direct confrontation and intends to keep that record intact.
- **Mundane**: Lockpicking (basic), picking pockets, urban navigation, safe passage through checkpoints, reading a room for threats.

## Role in the Party

| Strength | Zara's Contribution |
|----------|-------------------|
| Social situations | Zara is the party's entry point into any closed room, private event, or exclusive circle |
| Information | She can identify who knows what, who is lying, and what is being hidden — if she chooses to share |
| Negotiation | She reads desperation and desire and exploits both — or, occasionally, refuses to |
| Mobility | Zara's job is to get in, get the information, and get out; she does not linger where she is not wanted |

## Relationships

- **[[Velan Kivar Zan Ehkovok|Velan]]**: The one person in the party she cannot read. Velan's obsessive commitment to truth makes him simultaneously the most useful and most dangerous person in her orbit. She is drawn to him and wary of him in equal measure. She suspects he already knows she withholds things — and she cannot decide whether to admire his restraint or fear it.
- **[[Erin von Adeler|Erin]]**: Mutual, wary respect. Erin does not like liars and makes no secret of it. Zara respects this more than she will admit. There is a grudging understanding between them — Erin needs someone who can get into places she cannot, and Zara needs someone who will notice if she disappears.
- **[[Damara eth Rusvyn|Damara]]**: The one person whose sincerity Zara cannot deflect. Damara's openness is so foreign to her experience that it reads as either naivety or a performance so perfect that Zara cannot find its edges. She finds herself, against her will, wanting to protect it.
- **[[Martin Halgrom|Martin]]**: A comfortable alliance. Martin's absent-minded warmth is genuinely disarming — he forgets to ask her questions she doesn't want to answer. She enjoys his company more than she should and trusts his discretion more than she trusts anyone.
- **[[Gwenn]]**: Two people who survived by being underestimated, and who both know it. Gwenn watches Zara the way a wolf watches another wolf. Zara finds this exhausting and oddly reassuring.
- **[[Angar]]**: Professional courtesy. Angar is everything Zara is not — direct, loyal, physically dangerous. She does not understand him, which makes her cautious around him.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore

- **Information as power vs. information as shared truth** — Zara treats knowledge as currency; the Compact demands it as community
- **Survival identity vs. genuine self** — she has performed so many roles she can no longer be certain who she is underneath
- **Conditional loyalty** — what would it take for her to commit fully, and what would break that commitment?
- **Half-elf outsider** — never fully human, never fully elven, always reading the room for which version of herself is needed

### Story Hooks

- The Compact discovers Zara joined for personal reasons and must decide whether to trust her — or expel her
- Zara's mother's name appears in a Compact document from years ago — was she a member? An informant? A victim?
- A rival information broker surfaces in a city the Compact needs access to, and Zara must choose between eliminating the competition and forming an alliance
- Velan confronts Zara directly: he knows she is withholding something, and he is tired of waiting

### Arc Summary

**Beginning**: Zara is embedded in the Compact for personal gain. She collects information, gives the minimum, and plans her exit.

**Middle**: The Compact's enemies and her own begin to converge. She cannot investigate without them, and they cannot act without her intelligence. She starts sharing more than she planned — and noticing she misses it when the information flow stops.

**End**: The conspiracy that killed her mother is within reach. But pursuing it requires trusting the Compact with the truth she has hidden — or leaving them blind. Zara must decide what her information is actually for.
