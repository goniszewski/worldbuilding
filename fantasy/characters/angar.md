## Appearance

Black-skinned, broad-shouldered, solidly built. Wears Brotherhood insignia openly and carries [[Łamacz Dusz]] at his side. A long black coat marks him as a member of the order, weathered from years of travel.

A member of the Brotherhood of the Soul Breaker trying to recover a sword from cultists. Black-skinned, honorable, steadfast, with truly fascinating stories to tell (unlike Velan's embellished tales—he constantly challenges Angar to "story-offs" and always loses). He carries the cursed sword [[Łamacz Dusz]].

## Companion

- **Raven** — His pet raven who has a appetite for Damara's bat

## The Truth About the Sword

Angar knows from the beginning what happens to every wielder of [[Łamacz Dusz]] and what the Brotherhood does to prevent it (essentially: kill the current bearer and give the sword to someone new). He tells no one until confronted by the hero about this knowledge, when he admits he learned it days after his Brotherhood fell apart.

## Faith Crisis

Angar may experience a crisis of faith and break down when he realizes he may be deceiving himself, trying to fulfill a mission he imposed on himself—finding the sword and restoring the order, which will inevitably result in his own death. The hero can:

- Help him gather himself and continue his mission
- Talk him out of it by suggesting the sword could be destroyed (false), bound by magic (temporary), buried (it "calls" nearby magic-sensitive creatures, someone will dig it up), or thrown into the sea (it washes ashore eventually and starts "calling" again)

## Relationships

- **Damara Lo'Ehen** — Her bat and his raven have a predator/prey dynamic
- **Player** — Can help him through his crisis or talk him out of his quest

- His stories are genuine and compelling, contrasting with Velan's exaggerations
