> The cold matriarch of Daever, ruling through distance, patience, and controlled fear.

## Description

Lady Morwena Daever rules from the top of her tower, visible above the mist for one hour each dawn. She rarely descends. She rarely meets visitors. She rarely needs to. Her reach extends through the mist itself, carried by her *Zmora* (Nightmare) scouts and her legendary patience.

She is not cruel — that would require emotional investment she does not possess. She is simply *efficient*. The Milk Mist flows to her customers. The valley remains impenetrable. The house grows stronger each year. These are the only things that matter.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Thin, pale, almost translucent
- **Hair**: White, always bound in elaborate braids
- **Eyes**: Pale blue, said to see through fog
- **Distinguishing Features**: Always wears white and silver; carries a mist-making device as a scepter; has never been seen without her ceremonial mask

### Personality

Morwena is ice given human form. She speaks softly, plans decades ahead, and considers emotion a weakness that clouds judgment. Her only passion is the mist itself — she studies it constantly, seeking new applications.

She is a brilliant strategist who has never lost a negotiation. She never rushes, never shows urgency, and never forgets a debt. Her smile is more terrifying than her frown.

> *"The mist does not hate you. It does not love you. It simply is. I find this... comforting."*

## Skills & Abilities

- **Political Intrigue**: Master — plays houses against each other
- **Alchemy**: Expert — has spent decades studying Milk Mist
- **Mist Manipulation**: Can sense anything in the valley
- **Psychological Warfare**: Masters of fear without violence

## Equipment

- **Maska Mgły** (Mist Mask) — Ancient artifact that lets her breathe in any fog
- **Laska Panowania** (Scepter of Dominion) — Controls the mist vats
- **Księga Tajemnic** (Book of Secrets) — Encrypted formulas and blackmail

## Relationships

- **Strażnik Mgły** (Mist Keeper): Her most trusted advisor; they share everything
- **[[characters/lonen-czar|Lonen Czar]]**: Business arrangement; she finds him amusing
- **Her Children**: All dead or estranged; she considers this an acceptable loss
- **The Valley**: She loves it more than any person

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Cold logic versus warm chaos
- Power through isolation
- The cost of emotional detachment
- What drives someone who has everything?

### Story Hooks
- Morwena offers a party a contract: infiltrate a rival house for mist samples
- Her Mist Keeper has vanished — the mist is behaving strangely
- Someone is stealing Milk Mist — and she's offering fortune for information
- A lost relative seeks an audience with the terrifying Lady

Morwena should feel inhuman — brilliant, cold, and utterly without sentiment. She can be useful but never warm. She is a force of nature more than a person.
