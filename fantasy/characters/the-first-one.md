# The First One

> The last ruler of the Cleansed, whose fateful curiosity changed the world forever.

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Category** | Character |
| **Race** | The Cleansed |
| **Sex** | Female |
| **Status** | Deceased (died in childbirth) |
| **Period** | ~10,000–15,000 years ago |

## Description

The **First One** was the last ruler of the [[races/the-cleansed|The Cleansed]] during the **[[lore/age-of-light|Age of Light]]**. Unlike her predecessors — who maintained peace and tradition — she was characterized by **curiosity and inquisitiveness**.

This thirst for knowledge led her to investigate a strange element discovered in an underground cave. Scholars believe it was either a large deposit of [[items/blue-ore|blue ore]] or one of the legendary [[items/black-stones-of-ur|Black Stones of Ur]] — both saturated with pure magic.

Her prolonged exposure to magical energy transformed her fundamentally.

## Transformation

Exposed to magic, the First One's soul was:
- **Blessed** (or tainted, depending on interpretation)
- **Connected to the [[lore/abyss|Void]]** — a multi-layered dimension

Within the Void:
1. **[[lore/aspects|Aspects]]** — beings of immense power with indeterminate consciousness — imprinted on her soul
2. They granted her the ability to pass into their dimension after death
3. Fragments tore from each Aspect, mixing to create [[lore/demons|demons]]

The Aspects and demons nested within her soul began to speak to her.

### The Madness

According to some records, the First One went **mad**. The voices of the Void changed her. Whether this was possession, inspiration, or mental breakdown remains debated by scholars.

## Death and Legacy

The First One died in childbirth — but bore not one child, but **three**:

| Child | Nature |
|-------|--------|
| **[Torr](torr.md)** | Dark, scaly, harsh yet honorable |
| **[Daraster](daraster.md)** | Bold, ambitious, wicked |
| **[Lesandra](lesandra.md)** | Tall, pale, balanced, could control magic |

She broke tradition by not naming a successor from among her people. Instead, she commanded them to obey her child.

All three children inherited her ability to contact the Void — and possibly, her madness.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **Curiosity's price** — seeking knowledge led to catastrophe
- **Madness or enlightenment** — was she blessed or cursed?
- **Maternal sacrifice** — her death gave birth to new races
- **The voice within** — entities whispering from beyond

### Story Hooks
- Heretics claim the First One was not mad but achieved enlightenment
- A mage seeks to recreate her transformation to gain Aspect-level power
- Descendants experience echoes of her "madness" during magical contact
- The cave where magic was first discovered may still exist
