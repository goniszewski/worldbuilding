# Zygfryd Walldom

> The mind behind Walldom's power, more dangerous for preferring strategy to spectacle.

## Description

While Lord Korn commands the gates, Zygfryd commands the shadows. The true power in Walldom is not the ancient lord who cannot die — it is his descendant who refuses to let his house fade into irrelevance.

Zygfryd is everything Korn is not: young, ambitious, politically savvy, and hungry. He has spent twenty years quietly building a network of alliances, debts, and intelligence that makes him the most dangerous man in Walldom — possibly the realm.

### Appearance

- **Build**: Medium, comfortable in both armor and courtly robes
- **Hair**: Dark brown, starting to grey at temples
- **Eyes**: Sharp blue, always calculating
- **Distinguishing Features**: Always impeccably dressed; wears Korn's old war-medals as jewelry

### Personality

Zygfryd is charming when he needs to be, ruthless when required, and always three moves ahead. He genuinely loves his ancestor — but he also knows that Korn's time is over. Walldom needs a leader who will *live*, not merely endure.

> *"My grandfather taught me that patience is power. I taught myself that patience without action is just waiting to die."*

He is not evil, but he is ambitious. He believes Walldom's isolation is a mistake, and he intends to change that — carefully, incrementally, inevitably.

## Skills & Abilities

- **Political Intrigue**: Master — has fingers in every major house's affairs
- **Intelligence Network**: Commands spies in seven cities
- **Negotiation**: Silver-tongued, can sell anything to anyone
- **Combat**: Competent but not exceptional — he relies on others for violence

## Relationships

- **[Lonen Czar](lonen-czar.md)**: Old "friends" — each knows the other is dangerous

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Ambition and its costs
- Tradition versus progress
- Family duty and personal desire
- The ethics of manipulation

### Story Hooks
- Zygfryd offers a party a contract: disrupt Czar's operations in exchange for Walldom's support
- He seeks to arrange a politically advantageous marriage — and needs someone's help
- His spy network has been compromised — he needs outsiders to root out the traitor
- He is dying of a mundane illness — one his grandfather's blood cannot cure

### GM Notes
> Zygfryd is a patron, antagonist, or complex ally depending on party alignment. He plays the long game and always has contingencies. He should never seem desperate.
