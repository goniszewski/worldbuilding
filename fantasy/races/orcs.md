## Description

Orcs are strong, fierce humanoids known for their physical power and combat prowess. Unlike the structured societies of other races, orcish culture varies dramatically between tribes, making generalizations difficult.

## Characteristics

### Physical Traits
- **Muscular build**: Naturally strong and durable
- **Height**: Typically around 180 cm
- **Tusks**: Lower canines protrude
- **Skin tones**: Various greens, grays, browns

### Strengths
- **Physical power**: Natural strength and endurance
- **Combat effectiveness**: Instinctive warriors
- **Tribe bonds**: Loyalty within the tribe
- **Adaptability**: Can survive in harsh environments

### Weaknesses
- **Short lifespan**: Quick to mature, quick to die
- **Tribe divisions**: Little unity between tribes
- **Reputation**: Other races often assume aggression

## Society & Culture

### Tribal Structure
Orcish society is primarily tribal, but tribes vary widely:
- **Warrior clans**: Focus on raids and conquest
- **Hunter bands**: Nomadic, game-focused
- **Trader tribes**: Exchange goods with other races
- **Shamanic circles**: Spirit-focused, more mystical

### Values
- Values vary widely by tribe — warrior clans prize strength, hunter bands value self-reliance, trader tribes honor fair dealing, and shamanic circles revere the spirit world

### Common Perceptions
Orcs are often misunderstood by other races. While some tribes are indeed violent raiders, others are peaceful hunters or traders. The diversity of orcish tribes is frequently overlooked.

## Notes

Orcish tribes exist throughout the continent, from the northern wastes to the southern jungles. Their relationship with other races depends heavily on the specific tribe and its current leadership. Some tribes have established peaceful trade routes, while others raid settlements without warning.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- The danger of judging a people by their worst examples
- Tribal diversity vs. monolithic stereotypes
- What happens when a "savage" culture encounters "civilized" politics

### Story Hooks
- A trader tribe seeks peaceful contact but is mistaken for raiders
- Two orc tribes at war each approach the party for alliance
- An orc shaman possesses knowledge lost to other races
