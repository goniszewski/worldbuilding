## Description

Elves are known for their adherence to strict principles and traditions. Their long lifespans make them deliberate and principled in all dealings, earning cold respect from other races—especially the Naterians.

Their bloodline is considered the **purest** of the races — direct descendants of [[characters/daraster|Daraster]] and [[characters/lesandra|Lesandra]] without admixture. This purity is central to elven identity and their belief in their own superiority.

## Characteristics

### Strengths
- **Long-term thinking**: Decisions consider centuries, not years
- **Principled**: Strict ethical codes govern behavior
- **Graceful**: Natural poise and physical refinement
- **Cultural preservation**: Detailed history and traditions

### Weaknesses
- **Inflexible**: Difficulty adapting to rapid change
- **Detached**: May seem cold or dismissive of shorter-lived races
- **Slow decisions**: Everything considered from multiple angles

## Political Reality

The elven political arena is not as "clean" as outsiders believe. Beneath the facade of noble deliberation and ancient wisdom lie:

- **Political manipulations** — Subtle maneuvering for influence and advantage
- **Corruption** — Wealth and favors changing hands in shadows
- **Threats and coercion** — Less visible but present in aristocratic circles

Elves are extremely careful to conceal anything that might damage their carefully cultivated public image. This attention to appearances extends to:

- Controlling information flow to outsiders
- Ensuring embarrassing incidents never reach public knowledge
- Maintaining the illusion of pure, principled governance

This careful curation of reputation is similar in execution—though different in nature—to the approach taken by the Naterians.

## Cultural Notes

The elves' long lifespans mean they remember events others have forgotten. They often serve as historians and advisors, though their advice can be frustratingly long-term in perspective.

## Notes

Elves' respect from the Naterians is notable—the Nater Hegemony typically views other races with disdain but acknowledges the elves' principled nature. This mutual respect creates a rare diplomatic channel between the two powers.
