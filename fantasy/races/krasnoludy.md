## Origin

The [[krasnoludy|Dwarves]] are considered descendants of **forbidden unions** — children born from the joining of [[torr|Torr's]] bloodline with [[daraster|Daraster's]] bloodline.

As punishment for this "disgrace," they were driven underground by the other races — especially by the [[naterowie|Naterians]], who despised their mixed heritage (and received that hatred in return).

The dwarves call themselves **Galdanie**, though this name is rarely used by outsiders.

## Description

Dwarves are a short, sturdy people who have made their homes in the vast underground caverns between human and [[Naterianie (Naterians)|Naterian]] territories. They are renowned as master craftsmen, expert miners, and notoriously stubborn allies.

## Characteristics

### Physical Traits
- **Compact build**: Dense muscle, low center of gravity
- **Height**: Typically around 130 cm
- **Underground adaptation**: Excellent night vision, resistant to stone-dust lung

### Strengths
- **Craftsmanship**: Metalwork, stonework, and engineering unparalleled
- **Mining expertise**: Find and extract minerals others miss
- **Stubborn determination**: Once committed, nearly impossible to turn
- **Clan loyalty**: Deep bonds within and obligation to clan

### Weaknesses
- **Tunnel vision**: Literally and figuratively—focused on their craft
- **Grudge-holding**: Remember wrongs for generations
- **Surface discomfort**: Many distrust open spaces and sunlight

## Homeland

### Underground Kingdoms
Dwarven kingdoms are located underground, situated between human and Naterian territories. This strategic position creates:
- **Trade opportunities**: Both surface races need dwarven goods
- **Border tensions**: Especially with the Naterians who view dwarves poorly
- **Defensive advantage**: Natural fortifications in cavern systems

## Society & Culture

### Social Structure
- **Clan-based**: Identity tied to birth clan
- **Guild system**: Craftsmen organized by specialty
- **Council of Elders**: Governance by experienced clan leaders
- **Master-Apprentice**: Skills passed through direct teaching

### Values
- **Craft above all**: A masterwork outlasts any king
- **Word is bond**: Oaths are sacred and binding
- **Clan first**: Family and clan loyalty supersedes all
- **Honor in work**: Sloppy craft is shameful

### The Naterian View
The [[Naterianie (Naterians)|Naterians]] hold dwarves in their lowest regard, considering them either:
- An evolutionary step backward
- A failed human-Naterian hybrid experiment
- Simply beneath notice

This creates ongoing border tensions between dwarven kingdoms and the Nater Hegemony.

## Notes

Dwarven craftsmanship is sought throughout the known world. Their weapons, armor, and mechanisms command premium prices, and their mines produce metals and gems found nowhere else. However, their stubborn nature and long memories for grudges make them difficult diplomatic partners.
