# Pierwsi (The First Ones)

> The ancient ancestors of all modern races. Now lost to time.

## Overview

## Description

The primordial race from which all current thinking races evolved. Legends speak of them as giants, though details are lost to time. Their exact appearance varies in myth—some accounts describe them as towering figures, others as seemingly ordinary humanoids of extraordinary age.

## Historical Significance

The Pierwsi hold special reverence in Naterian culture. They particularly venerate **Torr**, the legendary leader of the Pierwsi, whose name appears in important locations like [[Torrs Gate (Brama Torra)|Torr's Gate]].

### The Legacy

- All modern races trace their lineage back to the Pierwsi
- Their language may have influenced Common and other tongues
- Ruins attributed to them dot the continent
- Some scholars believe the Sundering was caused by the last Pierwsi

## Cultural References

- **Naterian Religion**: Torr is venerated as the First One
- **Human Legends**: References in older folklore, often as nameless giants
- **Elven Tradition**: Viewed as the origin of their adherence to ancient principles

## Notes

The complete history of the Pierwsi remains one of the great mysteries of the world. Whatever their fate—whether they died out, evolved beyond recognition, or transcended to another realm—their legacy lives on in every thinking being.
