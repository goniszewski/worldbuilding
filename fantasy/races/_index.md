# Sapient Races

> The intelligent, thinking races of the world.

## Overview

The sapient races are the thinking, feeling peoples who shape the history and future of the realm. Each evolved from the ancient [[Pierwsi (The First Ones)|Pierwsi]], though the details of that evolution remain debated.

## Evolution

All modern races evolved from the extinct **[[Pierwsi (The First Ones)|Pierwsi]]** (The First Ones), though the timeline and circumstances of this divergence are lost to history.
