## Description

Humans are the dominant race in many regions of the known world. Known for their ambition, adaptability, and remarkable diversity of cultures, kingdoms, and philosophies.

## Characteristics

### Strengths
- **Adaptability**: Thrive in almost any environment
- **Diversity**: Wide range of cultures, religions, and political systems
- **Ambition**: Drive to explore, conquer, and create
- **Short Lifespans**: Lead to faster generational change and innovation

### Weaknesses
- **Ambition**: Can tip into greed and corruption
- **Division**: Noble-commoner gaps, political infighting
- **Short Memory**: History often repeats due to forgotten lessons
