## Description

The **[[Czyści|Czyści]]** (literally "the Cleansed" or "the Pure Ones") were the original inhabitants of the world as described in creation legends. They were an unblemished people, **free of magic** — not because magic didn't exist, but because they had never encountered it.

According to legend, they lived in paradise during the **[[epoka-światła|Epoka Światła]]** (Age of Light): in the glow of justice, abundance, and peace. There were no wars among them.

All conflicts between the people were resolved by **[[pierwsza|Pierwsza]]** (The First), who herself designated her successor. This succession was sacred tradition — until the last [[pierwsza|Pierwsza]] broke with custom.

- Succession was matrilineal and voluntary (the ruler chose the next)

### Values
- Peace and non-violence
- Community harmony
- Traditional succession customs

### Known Territories
- Primary: [[archipelag-światła|Archipelag Światła]] (Archipelago of Light) — a group of small islands

---

## The Transformation

When the last [[pierwsza|Pierwsza]] discovered magic (likely Blue Ore or a Black Stone of Ur), she became the first [[Czyści|Czystych]] to make contact with the [[abyss|Otchłań]] (Void).

Her transformation led to:
1. Her pregnancy with the three ancestors — [[torr|Torr]], [[daraster|Daraster]], and [[lesandra|Lesandra]]
2. Civil war among the people
3. The devastation of their home island
4. The eventual extinction of the pure-blooded [[Czyści|Czyści]]

Over thousands of years, the [[Czyści|Czyści]] ceased to exist as a distinct people. Their descendants — [[naterowie|Naterians]], [[ludzie|Humans]], [[elfy|Elves]], and [[krasnoludy|Dwarves]] — replaced them.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **Lost paradise** — the fall from innocence mirrors many religious traditions
- **Ancestral weight** — do modern races carry the sins/virtues of the Czyści?
- **What was lost** — could the Archipelago hold records of the true Czyści?

### Story Hooks
- Discovery of pre-magic artifacts from the Archipelago of Light
- A cult attempting to "restore" the Czyści by eliminating magic users
- Scholars debating whether the Czyści were truly peaceful or if legends are idealized
