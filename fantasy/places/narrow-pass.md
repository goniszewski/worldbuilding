# Narrow Pass (Wąski Przesmyk)

> The Narrow Pass, where the Mort clans turn geography itself into an act of violence.

## Description

Narrow Pass, known in Polish as *Wąski Przesmyk*, is exactly what it sounds like: a treacherous squeeze through the northern mountains, the only viable route between the highlands and the lowlands for those who cannot climb like the Mort.

The pass itself is barely wide enough for three men abreast in places, flanked by sheer cliff walls that create perfect ambush conditions. Mort warbands have held this pass against armies ten times their size, not through superior numbers but through superior position.

### The Three Gates

The Mort have fortified three positions within the pass:

1. **Brama Zewnątrz** (Outer Gate) — First defense, warning position
2. **Brama Środkowa** (Middle Gate) — Main fighting position, barracks
3. **Brama Wewnętrzna** (Inner Gate) — Final defense before Mort territory

Each gate is a stone fortification manned by a different clan, creating layered defense.

## Culture

The pass is sacred to the Mort — it is where their ancestors made their last stand against the unified human armies, holding the mountains against all comers. *"Our grandfathers held this pass with bones and fury. What excuse do we have?"*

Outsiders are permitted through, but:
- Weapons must be surrendered at the Outer Gate
- An armed escort is mandatory
- No mapping or sketching allowed
- Passage fees are paid in goods, not gold
