## Description

**Gullveig** (also spelled **Gullvig**) is a prominent dwarven merchant city known throughout the underground kingdoms and beyond for its commerce and financial institutions.

### Trade Specialization

- Banking and financial services
- Precious metal exchange
- Long-distance caravan operations
- Credit and lending

### Relationship with Andvari

Gullveig and [[places/andvari|Andvari]] are major economic rivals, competing for trade routes and influence across dwarven territories and with surface nations.
