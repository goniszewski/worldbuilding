# Barrenport

> A mining port of hard labor, Blue Ore wealth, and industrial hunger.

| Field | Value |

## Description

Barrenport earns its grim name from the surrounding landscape — rocky, barren hills scarred by centuries of mining, where nothing grows and the mountains have been hollowed out like rotten teeth. Yet this wasteland contains the realm's most valuable resource: *błękitna ruda* (blue ore).

The city is divided into two worlds: the gleaming port above and the grimy mines below. Merchants come for ore; miners rarely see the sun.

### The Mines

The Barrenport mines are legendary — and infamous:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Depth** | 2 miles deep at deepest |
| **Length** | 400+ miles of tunnels |
| **Workers** | 15,000+ miners (three shifts) |
| **Casualties** | ~500 per year |
| **Blue Ore Yield** | Monopoly control |

The mine entrances are guarded day and night — stealing ore is punishable by death. Even touching unrefined ore without authorization is illegal.

## Culture

Barrenport culture is shaped by the mines:
- Miners are aristocracy (highest status workers)
- Children work "light" sections from age 10
- Fatal accidents are "the mountain's tithe"
- Songs are about buried treasures and buried workers

> *"We dig for blue ore and find our graves. Still, someone must dig."*

## For Writers / Game Designers

- A mine collapse reveals something ancient below

### GM Notes
> Barrenport is perfect for industrial intrigue, labor disputes, and dungeon crawls. The mines can contain anything — including things that should stay buried.
