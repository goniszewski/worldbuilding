*The island home of the Cleansed, now destroyed.*

## Description

The **Archipelago of Light** was a group of small islands that served as the ancestral homeland of the [[the-cleansed|The Cleansed]]. It was here that the [[cleansed|Cleansed]] lived during the **[[age-of-light]]** — a period of paradise, peace, and prosperity.

The name "Light" reflects the era's ideals: justice, abundance, and spiritual clarity. Whether the archipelago itself was literally luminous, or this was metaphorical, remains unknown.

---

## The Fall

When the last [[the-first-one|The First One]] discovered magic in an underground cave, the archipelago's fate was sealed:

| Event | Consequence |
|-------|-------------|
| Magical exposure | Transformation of [[the-first|The First]], birth of three children |
| Civil war | Siblings turned against each other using unmastered magic |
| Magical devastation | The war scarred the land itself |
| Departure | Survivors left seeking new lands |

The island was no longer fit for habitation. [[torr|Torr]] departed with his followers in one direction; [[daraster|Daraster]] and [[lesandra|Lesandra]] in the other.

---

## Current Status

The **Archipelago of Light no longer exists** — either destroyed entirely, sunk beneath the sea, or so transformed by magical warfare that it is unrecognizable.

### Theories

| Theory | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Destroyed** | The civil war literally destroyed the islands |
| **Sunk** | Magical upheaval caused the islands to sink |
| **Transformed** | The landscape was so altered it cannot be identified |
| **Hidden** | The archipelago still exists but is magically concealed |

---

## Legacy

Despite its destruction, the Archipelago remains significant:

- **Origin point** — All sapient races trace their lineage here
- **Mythological weight** — Legends of the Age of Light reference the archipelago
- **Archaeological potential** — If discovered, ruins could reveal pre-magic civilization
- **Symbolic meaning** — "Light" as opposed to the "[[abyss|Void]]" that corrupted it

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes
- **Paradise lost** — the archetype of a perfect land destroyed by its inhabitants
- **Searching for origins** — what would finding the archipelago mean?
- **Pre-magic knowledge** — artifacts from before magic could be revolutionary

### Story Hooks
- A cartographer claims to have found evidence of the archipelago's location
- Magical anomalies in a remote sea region hint at buried magical residue
- Cultists seek to "restore" the Age of Light by recreating the archipelago
- Ruins discovered on a small island contain pre-magic artifacts
- A Naterian prophecy claims the archipelago will rise again when Torr's descendants reclaim their birthright
