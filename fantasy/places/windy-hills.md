# Windy Hills (Wiatrowe Wzgórza)

> High winds, open ground, and generations of archers who learned to live by both.

## Description

Windy Hills, known in Polish as *Wiatrowe Wzgórza*, are exactly as named: endless rolling highlands where the wind never stops. It howls through the passes, tears at exposed skin, and has been shaping the landscape and the people for longer than history records.

The Tiltman archers have made these hills their home for millennia, developing skills that seem supernatural. Their arrows fly true in winds that knock men down. Their eyes have adapted to constant motion. Their patience has become legendary.

### The Wind

The constant wind is the defining feature:

| Wind Speed | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| Normal Day | 30-40 mph, steady |
| High Wind | 60+ mph, gusting |
| Storm | 100+ mph, dangerous |

This wind:
- Makes normal archery impossible
- Has trained generations of Tiltman to compensate
- Creates natural "wind tunnels" in certain passes
- Never stops — even in summer

### Memorials

Throughout the hills stand the *Pamiątki* (Memorials) — stone markers where trespassers were killed. Each is precisely placed, exactly one mile from the next, creating a perfect grid of warnings.

## Culture

Tiltman culture is shaped by the wind:
- Silence is sacred — only the wind speaks
- Children learn archery before reading
- Outsiders are given one warning, then arrows
- The wind is the only deity they acknowledge

> *"The wind does not forgive. Neither do we."*

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Mastery through adversity
- Isolation as identity
- The weight of legend
- Environmental training

### Story Hooks
- A party must pass through — with Tiltman permission
- An exiled Tiltman seeks to return
- Something is disrupting the wind patterns
- A sacred arrow tree has been destroyed

### GM Notes
> The Windy Hills should feel desolate, exposed, and watched. The Tiltman should seem superhuman in their accuracy. Trespassers should be terrified.

## Trivia

- The wind blows from a different direction depending on the season
- Outsiders can get lost in minutes — the hills all look the same
- Tiltman arrows can be heard miles away — they whistle
