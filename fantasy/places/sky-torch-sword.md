# Sky Sword (Niebiański Miecz)

> A monumental blade of engineering and omen, standing where history meets myth.

*A blade that pierces the sky. No magic. No explanation. Just impossible.*

---

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Type** | Monument |
| **Location** | Sacred Peninsula — northern tip |
| **Height** | ~200 meters (above ground) |
| **Depth** | Unknown (driven deep into bedrock) |
| **Status** | Intact, purpose debated |

## Description

At the northern tip of the Sacred Peninsula, embedded in the earth as if struck by a god, stands the **Sky Sword**.

Unlike any weapon ever forged, this monument defies explanation. A blade of impossible proportions, driven point-first into solid rock. The metal has not corroded. The stone has not crumbled. And no one has ever discovered how it was placed there.

> *"I've seen the calculations. I've walked every inch of that blade. And I still can't tell you how it got there. No magic. No arcane arts. Just... physics breaking its own rules."*
> — Martin Guecventrak, frustrated

| **Hilt Design** | Crossguard extends 60m in each direction |
| **Edge** | Sharp enough to split atoms (metaphorically and literally) |

### The Impossible Edge

Objects that pass too close to the blade's edge are cut. Not by force — by geometry. The edge exists in a state that simply **is**. It does not need to swing. It does not need pressure.

---

## Engineering vs. The Impossible

Where the [Sky Torch](sky-torch.md) can be explained by gas and physics, the Sky Sword cannot be explained by **anything**.

### What We Know

| Attempt | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Chemical analysis** | Metal composition matches nothing known |
| **Structural study** | Blade should have buckled under its own weight |
| **Dating attempts** | Results vary wildly (1,000 to 50,000 years) |
| **Depth probing** | Ground-penetrating attempts return contradictory data |

### What We Don't Know

- How was it placed there?
- Why was it placed there?
- Is it a weapon, a marker, or something else?
- Is it related to the Sky Torch?

## The Sacred Peninsula's Twin Pillars

Together, the Sky Sword and [Sky Torch](sky-torch.md) define the peninsula:

| Monument | Position | Symbolism |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Sky Sword** | North | Judgment, cutting truth from lies |
| [Sky Torch](sky-torch.md) | South | Illumination, eternal hope |

The peninsula between them is holy ground. To desecrate either monument is the gravest crime in the region's laws.

## For the GM

### Adventure Hooks

| Hook | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **The Wobbling Blade** | Recent seismic activity has the Sword shifting. If it falls, what happens? |
| **The Hidden Message** | Engravings along the blade, visible only at certain angles, spell out a warning |
| **The Excavation** | Someone wants to dig up the base — what lies beneath? |
| **The Cut** | An experiment goes wrong — something was sliced, and it shouldn't have been possible |
| **Twin Purpose** | What if the Sword and Torch were built to work together? |

### Encounter Ideas

- Scholars arguing about the blade's origin
- Pilgrims touching the edge and being "tested" (minor cuts that reveal hidden truths)
- Guards preventing excavation attempts
- The Sword "speaks" to those who listen — or seems to

### Atmosphere

The Sky Sword dominates the northern skyline. Even in daylight, its metal gleams. At night, moonlight seems to gather around it. The air near the blade feels different — heavier, more deliberate.

There is no wind on the peninsula's north tip. The blade cuts it.
