# Sky Torch (Niebiańska Pochodnia)

> An eternal flame of craft and devotion, raised without magic yet revered like a miracle.

*A beacon of engineering mastery, burning without magic since ancient times.*

---

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Type** | Monument / Natural Landmark |
| **Location** | Sacred Peninsula (Przeklęta Południowa) — southern tip |
| **Height** | ~180 meters |
| **Status** | Active, eternal flame |
| **Construction** | Pure engineering, no magic used |

## Description

The **Sky Torch** is one of the greatest engineering achievements in recorded history — and the source of endless theological debate.

At the southern tip of the Sacred Peninsula stands this impossible monument: an obelisk of fused stone and bronze alloys, crowned with an eternal flame that has burned for millennia. Unlike the magical constructs of other faiths, the Sky Torch uses **no magic whatsoever**. Its flame is sustained by natural gas deposits venting through its internal shaft.

> *"Every other holy site in the world relies on blessing and prayer. This one? This one runs on gas and physics. And it has for ten thousand years."*
> — Martin Guecventrak, upon first seeing it

---

## Technical Specifications

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Foundation** | Carved directly into bedrock, 40 meters deep |
| **Main Shaft** | Internal corridor for maintenance access |
| **Gas Vents** | Natural deposits channeled through lower chambers |
| **Pressure Regulation** | Intricate valve system preventing explosion |
| **Flame Basket** | Bronze alloy basket designed to contain and project the flame |
| **Drainage** | System of channels managing moisture and gas buildup |

### The Gas Complication

The gas deposits beneath the Torch operate under **tremendous pressure**. Without careful engineering, the structure would explode. The internal maintenance corridor allows technicians to:

- Monitor pressure levels
- Clear blockages
- Adjust flow rates
- Perform repairs without shutting down the flame

This is not mere decoration — it is **survival engineering**.

---

## The Engineering Marvel

What makes the Sky Torch remarkable is its age. Built when other civilizations were still discovering bronze, the architects understood:

| Concept | Application |
|---------|-------------|
| **Geology** | Located precisely over stable gas deposits |
| **Pressure dynamics** | Calculated safe venting points |
| **Thermal expansion** | Materials chosen to withstand constant heat |
| **Structural load** | Counterweights and buttresses for the height |

No magic was used in its construction. No spirits were bound. No divine intervention was required.

This fact alone makes it the most controversial holy site in existence.

---

## Religious Significance

The Sky Torch represents the **Sacred Peninsula's** central theological argument:

> *"While others pray for fire, we built it. While others rely on gods, we relied on knowledge. And our flame still burns."*

### Opposing Viewpoints

| Faith | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| **Dorenism** | A test of faith — the gas is a blessing from the goddess |
| **Oprenism** | Proof that magic corrupted humanity; pure craft was once enough |
| **Arth'Adal** | Respect for the ancestors' practical wisdom |
| **Alar** | Suspicion — if no magic was used, where did the first builders get their power? |

## The Sacred Peninsula

The Sky Torch is one of two great monuments on the peninsula:

| Monument | Location | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Sky Torch** | Southern tip | Eternal flame, hope |
| **[Sky Sword](sky-torch-sword.md)** | Northern tip | Massive blade monument |

The peninsula itself is considered holy ground — a bastion of faith in an increasingly magical world.

## For the GM

### Adventure Hooks

| Hook | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **The Dying Flame** | Gas deposits are running low. The holy site faces extinction — and religious war. |
| **Sabotage Suspected** | Someone is tampering with the pressure valves. Who wants the Torch to fall? |
| **Engineering Secrets** | Hidden within the maintenance corridors are schematics for other lost technologies |
| **The Gas Road** | Ancient pipelines connect to other locations — what else did they build? |
| **The Other Peninsula** | The Sky Sword monument may have different secrets |

### Encounter Ideas

- Maintenance technicians who guard the internal corridors jealously
- Pilgrims debating theology at the base
- Scholars measuring and documenting the flame for academic papers
- Saboteurs from a rival faith attempting to "prove" magic superior

### Atmosphere

The Sky Torch burns day and night. At its base, a constant warm wind carries the scent of hydrocarbons. The flame can be seen for miles out to sea — it has guided ships and inspired poets for millennia.
