> *Mysterious, untamed, and majestic. Each of these terms can be applied to the three daughters of the Great Bessam: the Hidden Bessme, the Frenzied Bessie, and the Lazy Bess.*

## Overview

The Great Bessam is the largest river in the Delathor lands — and one of the greatest in the known world. Fed by mountain springs and seasonal rains, it carves through the landscape in three distinct sections, each with its own character and dangers.

## The Three Daughters

### The Hidden Bessme *(Skryta Bessme)*

The river's source is narrow, winding, and partially subterranean. It bursts forth from the earth where it has carved its way through steep, tall, and inaccessible cliffs — finally emerging, shrouded in mist, on the northern hills.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Width | Narrow |
| Depth | Shallow to moderate |
| Flow | Hidden, winding |
| Terrain | Mountain passes, cliffs |
| Danger Level | High (inaccessible) |

---

### The Frenzied Bessie *(Oszalała Bessie)*

Flowing downward, already less winding than her sister, the river gains strength and becomes much wider and deeper. Swollen by rainfall or the onset of spring, the increased water volume makes the river at this section unpredictable — it overflows its banks and sweeps away whatever stands in its path, even entire city districts.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Width | Wide |
| Depth | Deep |
| Flow | Powerful, swift |
| Terrain | Open valleys, floodplains |
| Danger Level | Extreme (flooding) |

### The Lazy Bess *(Leniwa Bess)*

The widest and shallowest section of the river — the third sister stretches across most of the land before emptying into the sea. This is the safest stretch, allowing easy transport of goods and fishing.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Width | Very wide |
| Depth | Shallow |
| Flow | Slow, calm |
| Terrain | Flat plains, delta |
| Danger Level | Low |
