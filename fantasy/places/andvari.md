## Description

**Andvari** is a powerful dwarven merchant guild headquartered in the wealthy underground city of **Advaranat**. The guild controls significant trade routes and commerce throughout the dwarven territories and beyond.

### Operations

- Long-distance trade with surface kingdoms
- Rare mineral and ore commerce
- Guild-to-guild negotiations
- Quality certification for dwarven craftsmanship

### Significance

As one of the most prominent merchant guilds in dwarven society, Andvari holds considerable economic and political influence. Their trade agreements often shape relationships between dwarven clans and surface races.
