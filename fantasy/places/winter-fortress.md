## Description

Zimowy Gród — Winter Fortress — is carved into a glacier so ancient it has forgotten the taste of summer. The fortress is invisible from outside — entrances hidden in ice caves, tunnels winding through the frozen mass. To find it, you must already know where it is.

The Winters have adapted to this frozen realm over generations. They are pale as snow, immune to cold, able to hunt in darkness. They are humans who have become something *other* through the simple brutal mathematics of survival.

## The Frost-Born

Winters inhabitants show strange adaptations:

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Skin** | White/pale, luminescent in moonlight |
| **Hair** | White/silver by age 30 |
| **Eyes** | Ice-blue, night-adapted |
| **Cold Immunity** | Comfortable at temperatures that kill |
| **Silent Movement** | Can walk on snow without sinking |

These are not magical — pure natural selection.

## Culture

Winters culture celebrates endurance:
- Children learn to hunt before walking
- The cold is sacred — warmth is weakness
- The glacier is the true lord
- Outsider diseases are their greatest threat

> *"We do not endure the cold. We are the cold. Those who cannot tell the difference do not survive."*

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Adaptation and transformation
- Isolation and identity
- Survival horror
- The human cost of extremity

### Story Hooks
- A southern party is stranded in the north
- The glacier is shifting — something is awakening
- A Winters heir seeks to marry outside the bloodline
- Ice beasts are migrating south in unprecedented numbers

### GM Notes
> Winter Fortress should feel alien and beautiful — deadly art. The cold is an antagonist as much as any creature. Winters NPCs should seem almost inhuman.

## Trivia

- The fortress has never been conquered
- Outsiders who enter rarely find their way out
- The glacier "sings" on certain nights
- Winters believe their ancestors made a pact with the Winter itself
