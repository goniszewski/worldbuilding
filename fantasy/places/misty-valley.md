## Description

Mist Valley, known in Polish as *Dolina Mgieł*, is a place where the world disappears. Perpetual fog blankets the entire valley, visibility rarely exceeding twenty paces. For most travelers, entering means never returning. For the Daever, it is home.

The fog is not natural — it has been cultivated for centuries, fed by alchemical vats hidden throughout the valley. The Daever call it *Matka Mgła* (Mother Mist), and they worship it as a divine presence. To outsiders, it is simply the most effective weapon in the realm.

### The Milk Mist

The valley's fog is laced with *Mleczna Mgła* (Milk Mist), an alchemical weapon:

| Exposure | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| 1 hour | Mild euphoria, disorientation |
| 3 hours | Hallucinations, paranoia |
| 6 hours | Paralysis, blindness |
| 12+ hours | Peaceful death |

Daever citizens are immune through generational exposure. Outsiders require protective gear or regular antidote doses.

### Lady's Tower

The only structure visible above the mist is Lady Morwena's tower — a white spire that appears for one hour at dawn before sinking back into the fog. This is where she holds court, when she bothers.

## Culture

The Daever have adapted to perpetual darkness:
- Buildings use sound navigation (bells, echoes)
- Soldiers paint armor white for visibility
- Courtship involves walking blindfolded together
- Truth-telling is sacred (lies poison the mist's clarity)

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Themes to Explore
- Environmental horror
- Sensory deprivation
- Adaptation to the impossible
- Weaponized nature

### Story Hooks
- A lost traveler is found by Daever scouts
- Someone is stealing Milk Mist vats
- The mist is thinning — why?
- A Daever citizen wants to leave but cannot

### GM Notes
> The Mist Valley should feel disorienting and alien. Standard navigation fails; sound becomes more important than sight. The Daever should be unsettling hosts.

## Trivia

- More armies have died in the Mist Valley than in any battle
- The mist has never successfully been replicated or stolen
- Daever born in the valley cannot survive in clear air for long
