## Description

Redwood earns its name from the ancient redwood forest that once surrounded it — most of which is long gone, cleared to make room for warehouses, counting houses, and merchant inns. What remains is a city that never sleeps, where gold flows like water and everyone is either selling something or buying it.

The Graz family transformed this modest trading post into a commercial powerhouse. Their annual Redwood Trade Fair attracts merchants from across the known world, and their banking houses extend credit to nobles who would otherwise starve.

### The Trade Fair

The annual Redwood Trade Fair is the city's crown jewel:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Duration** | Three weeks in autumn |
| **Attendance** | 10,000+ visiting merchants |
| **Volume** | 40% of realm's annual trade |
| **Security** | Neutral ground, all disputes suspended |
| **Entertainment** | Feasts, performances, gambling |

## Culture

Redwood culture revolves around commerce:
- Negotiations begin at the third cup of wine
- Debt is sacred; default is disgrace
- Everyone has a price (admitted freely)
- Banners above shops show credit ratings

> *"In Redwood, even secrets are for sale. You just need the right currency."*
