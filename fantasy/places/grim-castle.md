# Grim Castle (Ponury Zamek)

> Grim Castle, a hard gate between surface realms and dwarven ambition below.

## Description

Grim Castle, known in Polish as *Ponury Zamek*, squats at the mouth of the *Podbramie* (Undergate), the vast underground passage connecting the human lands to the dwarven kingdoms. Half-built into the mountainside, half-dug into the rock, it is a fortress that never sleeps, with traffic flowing through its gates day and night.

The castle's upper levels host human administrators and guards. The lower levels connect directly to the tunnel entrance, where dwarven craftsmen maintain their own quarter. The result is a strange hybrid culture — human architecture decorated with dwarven runes, dwarven metalwork alongside human textiles.

### Structure

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Niebo** (Sky Level) | Human lord's quarters, main gate, defenses |
| **Ziemia** (Earth Level) | Mixed quarters, trading floor, market |
| **Podziemie** (Underground Level) | Undergate entrance, dwarven quarter |
| **Głębokie** (Deep Level) | Skjor vaults, sealed sections |

### The Undergate

The *Podbramie* is Skjor's greatest asset:

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Entry** | Carved tunnel, 30 feet wide |
| **Lighting** | Dwarven eternal torches every mile |
| **Traffic** | Ore carts, luxury goods, diplomats |
| **Dangers** | Occasional cave-ins, lost souls, deep-things |

The tunnel eventually connects to the dwarven realm of *Królestwo Kamienia* (Stone Kingdom), though no human has traveled the full distance.

## Culture

Skjor culture is a blend:
- Dwarven hospitality customs mixed with human formality
- Shared meals of both cuisines
- Mutual language learning encouraged
- Shared defense of the gate

> *"The mountain does not care if you are human or dwarf. It kills both equally. This teaches us humility."*
