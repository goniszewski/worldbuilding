# Torr's Gate (Brama Torra)

> A mountain wall so brutal that kingdoms treat it as both blessing and sentence.

> *Even children don't need to be told that the Gate is one of the greatest blessings we have ever received! No matter how eerie it is, how dreadfully the winds howl there. The stones, nay, even the ice spiders are just waiting to fall on you from above!? None of it matters. It means nothing. Our grandfathers and their grandfathers before them bear witness that without the Gate, we would be lost!*
> — *Adar the Redeemed, during a speech to the people*

## Description

At the northern edge of the Human Kingdom and the southern border of the Nater Hegemony, a powerful mountain ridge stretches across the land — an unwavering boundary that has stood for hundreds of years. Searching the annals of history, one would find no record of a single successful conquest of the lands on the opposite side. The ancient, mighty rocks — most of them impassable for large military formations — have driven more than one soul to ruin. In the higher reaches, deadly falls were commonplace, as travelers would sometimes recall — for fierce winds, rains, and freezing temperatures created treacherous ice formations. Powerful gusts of wind have been known to sweep even experienced climbers to their deaths, assuming anyone dared attempt the crossing in the first place.

Even assuming an attacking force managed to overcome the rocky ridge and begin their descent into what is considered relatively safe territory, the ease with which defenders could hold the line would spell doom for any assault. Thanks to numerous and difficult-to-approach elevations, deep passes, and well-fortified fortresses, even a force ten times larger would be decimated — with no possibility of retreat or regrouping.
