## Description

**Blue Ore**, known in Polish as **Błękitna Ruda**, is a volatile mineral found in select deposits throughout the world. Its primary known property is explosive reactivity when processed or refined, a characteristic that makes it both valuable and dangerous.

### Unique Phenomenon: The Singing

When exposed to **the light of a rising sun**, Blue Ore begins to emit a distinctive sound colloquially described as "singing." This phenomenon occurs:

- Only at dawn or sunrise
- When ore is in raw, unprocessed form
- In deposits receiving direct light

The "song" is described as a low, resonant hum that rises in pitch as the light intensifies. Miners working Blue Ore deposits traditionally begin their shifts at dawn to witness—and heed—the warning.

> *"Słyszysz śpiew, uciekaj w górę."*
> (You hear the song, run for the hills.)
