# Spirit Blade (Ostrze Dusz)

*[Legendary Weapon, Sentient]*

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Category** | Item |
| **Type** | Weapon (Longsword) |

| **Value** | Priceless (Confiscated artifacts cannot be sold) |
| **Attunement** | Required (Storm Binder only) |
| **Sentience** | Yes — bound spirit of a storm elemental |

## Description

The Spirit Blade appears as an elegant longsword with a blade of translucent blue-white crystal that seems to hold captured lightning within its facets. Its crossguard is forged from silver, etched with binding sigils that pulse faintly in the presence of spirit magic. The hilt is wrapped in dark leather, cool to the touch even in summer heat.

Originally crafted during the Age of Binding, these weapons were standard issue for elite Compact Enforcers before the Sundering disrupted production. The blade currently held by Lyra Dawnwhisper was confiscated from a rogue Binder in 200 P.S. and stored in the Silver Vault until she liberated it during her escape.

The blade contains the bound spirit of a minor storm elemental, granting it limited awareness and the ability to sense nearby spirit magic. It communicates through emotional impressions rather than words — unease when danger approaches, approval or disapproval of other magic users, and a fierce loyalty once a bond is formed.

## Properties

| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Bonded Wielder** | Once attuned, the blade can only be wielded effectively by its bonded Storm Binder |
| **Lightning Channel** | Attacks deal an additional 2d6 lightning damage |
| **Spirit Sense** | Can detect active spirit magic within 60 feet |
| **Elemental Fury** | Critical hits unleash a burst of lightning (DC 15 DEX save or 4d6 additional damage) |
| **Compact Tracker** | *Disabled* — sigils were burned out during confiscation |

### Sentience

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Personality** | Proud, protective, wrathful toward Compact |
| **Alignment** | Chaotic Good |
| **Communication** | Emotional impressions only |
| **Bond Tolerance** | One bond at a time; breaking requires mutual release or death |

### Cursed Properties

Once bonded, the wielder cannot willingly abandon the blade. Attempting to discard it causes crippling headaches until the blade is reclaimed.

## Lore

### History

- **Pre-Sundering (Age of Binding)**: Standard issue for Enforcers; dozens created
- **Sundering (0 P.S.)**: Forge-craft lost, production ceased
- **200 P.S.**: Current blade confiscated from Binder "Stormwind" in Verath
- **523 P.S.**: Liberated by Lyra Dawnwhisper during her escape from the Compact

### Cultural Significance

The Spirit Blade represents everything the Compact fears — unbound spirit power in the hands of those who reject their monopoly. For the Unbound, it symbolizes that the Compact's control is not absolute; artifacts can be reclaimed, bonds can be forged outside their structures.

## Notes

### GM Information

- **Acquisition Hook**: Lyra took this from the Silver Vault; its history is traceable
- **Compact Interest**: The Compact will hunt anyone carrying confiscated blades
- **Replacement**: No functional Spirit Blades can be created post-Sundering; this may change if the forge-craft is rediscovered
- **Plot Hook**: The blade may retain memories of previous wielders, including information about pre-Sundering events
