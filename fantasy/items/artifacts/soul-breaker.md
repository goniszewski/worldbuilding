## Description

The Soul Breaker is a terrible weapon of pure malevolence, forged in the Forge of Endless Darkness by demonic hands. Physically, only the hilt exists as solid matter — dark iron wrapped in leather that grows cold to the touch. The blade itself appears as intangible shadow, behaving like smoke and darkness that flows and shifts with each swing.

When the blade cuts through flesh, it does not merely wound — it tears. The ethereal edge rips through the soul of its victim, shattering it into fragments that cannot reform. These soul fragments are absorbed into the weapon, strengthening its power with each kill. The more souls consumed, the more potent the blade becomes.

## The Brotherhood

A secret order has guarded the Soul Breaker for centuries. They understand the weapon's terrible nature and its curse.

### The Covenant

| Rule | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Transfer every 5 years | Limits corruption, ensures controlled descent |
| Candidate testing | Only those with iron will survive |
| Final mercy | The council slays the old bearer to prevent full corruption |
| Silent service | The brotherhood appears only in legends |

### Brotherhood Tactics

- **Recruitment**: Test candidates for unbreakable will and capacity for sacrifice
- **Containment**: When not in use, the blade is sealed in a warded vault
- **Deployment**: Called only in greatest crises, once per few centuries
- **Legacy**: Members fade into legend; their fate is unknown to the common folk

## Lore

### Origin

Forged by demons in the Forge of Endless Darkness, the Soul Breaker was created as a weapon of total annihilation — not merely of body, but of essence. A soul destroyed by this blade cannot pass on, cannot be resurrected, cannot even find peace in death. It becomes part of the weapon forever.

### The Curse Explained

The blade's true curse is not the corruption of its wielder — that is merely a side effect. The true horror is the binding: once a soul is taken, it becomes part of the weapon's hunger. The more the blade devours, the hungrier it becomes, and the more it needs to feed that hunger through its bearer.

## Combat Information

### Statistics

```
Soul Breaker
Martial Weapon, Legendary (requires attunement)
Damage: 2d6 slashing + 1d8 necrotic
Properties: Heavy, Two-handed, Special (Soul Devour)
Weight: 4 lbs
Saving Throw: DC 15 Wisdom or gain 1 level of corruption
```

### Weaknesses

- **Light**: Takes damage in direct sunlight (1d6 per round)
- **Holy Water**: Wielder must make DC 18 CON save or drop the blade
- **True Name**: Speaking the demon's true name that forged it could banish the blade

## Notes

### GM Information

- **Acquisition Hook**: The brotherhood might recruit a PC if one shows exceptional willpower
- **Corruption Mechanics**: Use madness tables; each failure brings the wielder closer to possession
- **The Final Fate**: Brotherhood members know their end — this creates dramatic tension
- **Alternative Resolution**: Discovering the demon's true name could offer a chance to destroy the blade

### The Brotherhood's Dilemma

Every five years, the brotherhood must choose a new bearer. The old bearer has invariably been touched by the blade's whispers and cannot be trusted. The council, knowing this, performs the grim mercy of ending the old bearer before full corruption takes hold. Most bearers accept this fate as the price of service.
