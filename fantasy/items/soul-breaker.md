# Soul Breaker (Łamacz Dusz)

> A cursed blade that leaves flesh untouched while it destroys what mattered most.

## Description

Soul Breaker is a legendary cursed sword that **kills without harming the body**. The victim's soul is severed, leaving an empty vessel behind. It was created for a single purpose: to deliver the [[characters/archmage-of-ur|Archmage of Ur's]] soul into a new host.

> *"The body remains untouched, pristine as a winter morning. Only the soul is... gone."*
> — Excerpt from The Compact's archives

## Physical Description

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Type** | One-handed sword |
| **Length** | Approximately 90 cm |
| **Blade** | Dark steel, almost black, with a faint iridescent sheen |
| **Hilt** | Bone-white, carved with spiraling patterns |
| **Weight** | Unnaturally light |
| **Edge** | Perpetually sharp |

The blade seems to **drink light** — in shadows, it appears to absorb rather than reflect illumination.

## Properties

### Soul Severance (Passive)
When the blade strikes a living being:
- The body remains completely unharmed
- The soul is instantly and irrevocably severed
- Death is instantaneous and painless
- No wound, no blood, no sign of violence

### The Hollowing (Active)
If the blade has been prepared with a specific ritual:
- Instead of destroying the severed soul
- It captures the soul temporarily
- Allows it to be transferred to another vessel
- The Archmage used this to body-hop through the ages

## History

### Creation
The sword was forged in ancient Ur, designed by the Archmage himself:
- Created using forbidden binding techniques
- Tempered in the [[lore/abyss|Void]] itself
- Requires blood sacrifice to activate its true power

### The Mighty Eight
The [[characters/mighty-eight|Mighty Eight]] defeated the Archmage using this blade:
- They had stolen it from his vault
- The Seventh Hero — a warrior — delivered the final blow
- It was the only way to kill his physical form

### Recovery
After the Archmage's temporary death:
- The sword was sealed in the Compact's vaults
- Guarded by the order of Silent Monks
- Forgotten for centuries... until the cult found it

### The Cult's Theft
The cult infiltrated the Brotherhood years before the campaign:
- Corrupted members from within
- Identified weaknesses in security
- Stole Soul Breaker during the chaos
- Used it to transfer the Archmage's soul

## The Soul Transfer Ritual

To use Soul Breaker for its true purpose:

1. **Preparation**: The blade must be bathed in Void-essence (expensive, dangerous)
2. **The Kill**: Strike a willing or subdued victim
3. **Capture**: The soul is held within the blade
4. **Transfer**: Strike the intended host within 7 days
5. **Binding**: The soul merges with the new body

**Complications:**
- The new body must be magically compatible
- Each transfer weakens the soul slightly
- The blade "remembers" each victim

## Current Location

The cult possesses Soul Breaker. They used it to transfer the Archmage into the body of a powerful mage, preparing for the **Revelation**.

The blade may or may not remain with the Archmage — it's a tool, not a relic he worships.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Moral Weight
Soul Breaker represents absolute power over life and death. Key questions:
- Who deserves to die without suffering?
- Is the victim's body a "person" without a soul?
- What happens to the trapped souls?

### Combat Implications
- Perfect assassin weapon — no evidence
- Psychological warfare — fear of the blade
- Temptation — who wouldn't want such power?

### Story Hooks
- **Quest to destroy it** — break the curse, end the threat
- **Quest to recover it** — the Compact wants it back
- **Moral choice** — use it for "justice"?
- **The blade chooses** — it seeks worthy wielders

### The Blade Speaks
Some legends say Soul Breaker is semi-sentient:
- Whispers in the darkness
- Shows visions of its past victims
- Offers "gifts" to those who wield it
