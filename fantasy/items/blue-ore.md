# Blue Ore (Błękitna Ruda)

## Description

Blue ore is a rare crystalline material found primarily in the [[places/ash-wastes|Ash Wastes]] and certain deep veins within human territories. When processed, it becomes an extraordinarily powerful explosive.

> *"The first time I saw blue ore burn... I thought the sky itself was falling."*
> — A survivor of the Verath bombing

## Properties

### Raw Form
- Deep azure crystalline structure
- Faint luminescence in darkness
- Stable when unprocessed
- Moderately valuable as a curiosity

### Processed Form ("Dirty Bomb" / "Black Ore")
When refined through a specific alchemical process (known only to the cult), blue ore becomes:
- **Highly volatile** — detonates on impact or specific trigger
- **Radiating destruction** — affects an area of several dozen meters
- **Contaminating** — leaves residual magical contamination

### Cult Terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Blue ore** | Raw, unprocessed material |
| **Black ore** | Refined, weaponized form |
| **"Dirty bomb"** | Cult slang for explosive devices |
| **"The Light"** | Euphemism for detonation |

## Origin and Sources

### Natural Deposits
- [[places/ash-wastes|Ash Wastes]] — largest known deposits
- Deep mines in human territories (now heavily guarded)
- Rumored veins in elven lands (unconfirmed)

### The Cult's Supply
The cult has spent decades:
1. **Mining** deposits in the Ash Wastes
2. **Stealing** from human mines
3. **Purchasing** from black market dealers

## Uses by the Cult

### "Dirty Bombs"
Basic explosive devices using powdered blue ore:
- **Radius**: 10-20 meters of total destruction
- **Contamination**: Lingering effects on survivors
- **Cost**: Relatively low — accessible to any zealot

### "Black Ore" Bombs
Advanced weapons using refined black ore:
- **Radius**: 30-50 meters of devastation
- **Properties**: Tears at the soul, not just the body
- **Rarity**: Limited — requires the Second Stone to create

### Ritual Components
In large quantities, blue ore can be used for:
- Soul-binding rituals
- Void-connection ceremonies
- Creating unstable gateways

## Countermeasures

### Detection
- Magical detection (minor spirit sight)
- Physical inspection (unusual weight, faint glow)

### Neutralization
- Specific binding spells
- Dispersal in running water
- Sealing in lead-lined containers

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Story Hooks
- **Smuggling plot** — intercept blue ore shipments
- **Infiltration** — join the cult to learn their secrets
- **Race against time** — find the bomb before detonation
- **Moral dilemma** — destroy a town to prevent cult weaponization

### Economic Impact
Blue ore has legitimate uses in:
- Alchemy (at low concentrations)
- Enchanting (as a stabilizing agent)
- Architecture (reinforcement crystals)

This creates interesting tension — banning blue ore hurts legitimate users.

### Supply Chain
```
Raw Deposits → Cult Mining → Black Market → Refinement → "The Light"
      ↑              ↓
  Human Mines    The Cult's Reserves
```
