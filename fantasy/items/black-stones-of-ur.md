# Black Stones of Ur (Czarne Kamienie Ur)

> Three relics of impossible power, each promising divinity and ruin in equal measure.

## Description

Three mythical artifacts created by the [Archmage of Ur](../characters/archmage-of-ur.md), each granting their owner **immeasurable power** over the nature of reality. According to legend, the Archmage was the only one capable of mastering them.

> *"Three stones, three paths to eternity. Whoever unites them shall become a god among mortals."*
> — Fragment of ancient cult writings

## The Nature of Power

Each stone represents a different aspect of power:

| Stone | Aspect | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **The First** | Binding | Power to imprison or release any being |
| **The Second** | Transformation | Ability to change matter and energy |
| **The Third** | Chaos | Connection to the [Void](../lore/abyss.md) |

## Known Stones

### The Sacred Lantern of the Elves
The Sacred Lantern in the elven capital is in truth a **bound First Stone**. For centuries, elves believed it to be a gift from [Lesandra](../characters/lesandra.md) — blessed light protecting their people.

When the cult of the Archmage destroyed the binding spell, the stone exploded with force comparable to a **mini-atom bomb**.

### Stones in the Cult's Possession
Years of searching led the cult to discover the **Second Stone**. They use it to create "black ore" — a stabilized, lethal material.

## Comparison to Other Artifacts

Modeled after the **Infinity Stones** from the Guardians of the Galaxy — each stone alone is powerful, but together they grant absolute control.

## For Writers / Game Designers

### Possible Uses
- **Tempting the players** — every faction wants the stones for themselves
- **Source of conflict** — decision whether to destroy, hide, or use them
- **Cosmic horror elements** — the stones are part of the Void

### Balance
- One stone = legendary item level
- Two stones = world-changing artifact
- Three stones = end of the world or new beginning
