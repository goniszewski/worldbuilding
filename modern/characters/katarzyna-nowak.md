# Maj. Katarzyna "Kasia" Nowak

*Commander, Camp Ashfall — Gate Alpha Far-Side Perimeter*

## Description

Katarzyna Nowak led the first armed crossing of the Reopening. In June 2033, as a captain attached to a mixed insertion company, she took eleven soldiers and a wall of fire through [Gate Alpha](../places/gate-alpha.md) at night, held the far mouth for four hours, and made the engineers' perimeter possible. The crossing made her famous, which she has spent the years since trying to outrun.

She commands Camp Ashfall now: the farthest human outpost in existence, a kilometer from the gate's far mouth, on a world whose sky has a second moon. She is blunt, methodical, and allergic to ceremony. Her garrison adores her because she is always the last one to stop working, and because she never asks them to do anything she has not done first.

## Notable Traits

- **Presence**: The calm of someone who has already decided what she is willing to die for, and hopes it won't be necessary.
- **Talents**: Small-unit command; far-side logistics; the specific courage of walking through a door that leads to another world.
- **The Report**: Her after-action report on the crossing is taught at every JPC staff college — and the final section, on the far-side sky, is the part no one teaches. She wrote that the skyline was "occupied" before the perimeter existed.
- **The Listeners**: She files every sighting of the far-side "listeners" personally. Her standing order is to observe, report, and not approach. She has not explained why, and the garrison has learned not to ask.

## Relationships

- **[Gen. Adaora Mensah](adaora-mensah.md)**: The commander who backed her crossing against the staff college. Nowak has never asked why; Mensah has never told.
- **[Dr. Emrys Lindqvist](emrys-lindqvist.md)**: She sends him the perimeter's observations; he sends her translations. Their correspondence is the only channel where both of them are visibly afraid.
