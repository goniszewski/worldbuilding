# He-Who-Sifts-Ash

*Lord of the Cinder Flats — the first of the Ash-Born to break the silence*

## Basics

| Field | Value |

## Description

**He-Who-Sifts-Ash** is the first devil to speak to a human being, and the only lord of the dead world whose name is known on Earth. He rules the Cinder Flats — a territory of warm ash and buried salvage on the far side, out of the way of the greater lords' wars — and he is, by the standards of his species, a patient man.

He came to the [perimeter](../places/the-far-side.md) alone, unarmed by his people's measure, and stood at the wire until someone came out to speak. He speaks the classical [Devil Tongue](../lore/devil-tongue.md) in the high register — a courtesy the Babel Initiative did not expect from a lord, and has not stopped being suspicious of. His translated name is a statement of occupation: he sifts the ash of his world for what the empire left behind, and he has found enough to be dangerous.

## Notable Traits

- **Presence**: Stillness. He can stand without moving for longer than any human present, and uses it the way other diplomats use volume.
- **Talents**: Patience; the long view; knowing exactly what his words cost him in the lords' politics — and spending them anyway.
- **The Warning**: His first long statement to the negotiating team is now the most argued-over document in the Babel Initiative's archive: *"You think we are the end. We are the ones they broke to show the others. You are the others."*
- **The Grammar**: [Dr. Lindqvist](emrys-lindqvist.md) has noted that the lord's classical Tongue is grammatically older than his own age — as if the language he speaks was already old when he was born. The lord has not explained this.
- **The Offer**: He has offered humanity what he calls "the truth of the Silence" — the Observers' history, the empire's records, everything the Cinder Flats hold — for what he calls "what the dead world needs": water, and the Bound.

## Relationships

- **[Dr. Emrys Lindqvist](emrys-lindqvist.md)**: The only human he addresses in the high register. Their dialogues are the closest thing the war has to diplomacy.
- **[Gen. Adaora Mensah](adaora-mensah.md)**: He has asked to meet her twice. She has declined twice. He has not asked a third time.
- **The other lords**: His dialogue with humanity has cost him. She-of-the-Drowned-Pits has declared him kin-slain; the Pale Thresher trades with him and watches him; Maw-That-Counts, wherever it is, is the reason he asked the perimeter for protection in the first place.

## For Story Use

- The enemy who tells the truth — and the impossibility of knowing whether it is truth, strategy, or both.
- A lord who is old enough, or wise enough, or desperate enough to break his species' silence.
- The question at the center of the setting: is he warning humanity, recruiting it, or herding it?
