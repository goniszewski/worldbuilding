## Campaign Summary

**Setting**: Present-day Earth, mid-2034 — three years into the Reopening.

**Core Conflict**: Nine active gates under military containment; two held open as doors into the enemy's world. On one side, the fractured remnant of a dead civilization. On the other, the Observers — and the quiet question of what humanity's rise will cost.

**Key Hook**: The first translated sentence from a captured Chorister — *"We are the ash that remembers fire."* Humanity is beginning to understand what that means.

**Player Tone**: Military thriller meets cosmic horror — logistics, politics, and the slow dread of a universe with rules.
