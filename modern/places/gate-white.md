# Gate Foxtrot — the White Gate

> The continent had no one on it. That was the point.

## Overview

**Gate Foxtrot** — JPC designation **G-06**, known as **the White Gate** — stands inside an ice cave in the Transantarctic Mountains, one of the most hostile places on Earth and, before the Reopening, one of the most empty. It was the last gate confirmed, found not by the March — the White Gate's horde, such as it was, died in the white within days of emerging — but by a traverse team that stumbled on the cave in the austral summer of 2031.

The team reported two things. The frame — a lintel-ring of not-stone — was **cold**: colder than the ice around it, by a margin the instruments called impossible. And the script bands were **clean**: no ice, no rime, no dust, as if the frame had been wiped that morning. The team spent the rest of the season arguing about which detail was worse.

## Why It Is Contained

The anchor is **unreachable**. The cave entrance was sealed in the winter of 2032 — a serac collapse that, the satellite records show, moved in a way ice does not naturally move. JPC considered reopening it by heavy engineering and declined: the logistics of a major excavation on the coldest, emptiest continent, for a gate that had produced one dying horde, could not be justified. The White Gate is contained by **deciding not to go back**.

It is watched by satellite, by the two research stations' instruments, and by the small fly-in garrison at the nearest bare-rock camp — the coldest rotation in JPC, known as "the white tour".

## Holding It

- **Camp Anvil** — a fly-in garrison on the ice-free rock, staffed by volunteers and people with nowhere else to go.
- The gate itself is watched at a distance. No one approaches the cave. The standing order is older than the garrison: it was written by the traverse team, who left a sign at the cave mouth that has since been photographed — once — turned to face the *opposite* direction.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- The horror of a **door on the empty continent** — the one place where the anchors' placement pattern ("where people were not") was most true.
- **Containment as refusal** — a gate held by the decision not to look.

### Story Hooks
- A survey satellite images the cave mouth — **open**, with tracks leading away from it into the white. The tracks are not human, and they do not cross the crevasses; they go around them, as if whoever made them knows the route.
- The traverse team's logbook is found in a Camp Anvil footlocker. The last entry is dated three months **after** the team was evacuated.
- A signals officer notices the White Gate's script is the **mirror image** of the other anchors — written, as far as anyone can tell, from the far side.
