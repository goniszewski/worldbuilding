# Gate Hotel — the Jungle Gate

> The March came here and dissolved into the trees.

## Overview

**Gate Hotel** — JPC designation **G-08**, known as **the Jungle Gate** — stands in a cave system beneath a sandstone plateau in the Congo Basin. The anchor is a **frame fused into living rock**: not a free-standing ring but a doorway the plateau grew around, its not-stone so integrated with the cave walls that demolitions engineers cannot find the seam. The site was found on Day Two, when the horde that emerged did the one thing no other horde did: **it did not march**. It dispersed — into the forest, the caves, the river valleys — and the war in the basin became a war of patrols, tracks, and voices in the dark.

## Why It Is Contained

The anchor is **unbreakable**. The 2032 demolition attempt collapsed a cave chamber and exposed more of the frame than the survey had ever shown — the anchor is **larger than measured** — and the collapse proved that blasting it would bring the plateau down and bury the frame deeper, not destroy it. Since then the position has been: contain the gate, and contain the dispersed remnant of its horde, which the garrison believes is still in the bush. It is the only contained gate whose garrison functions as much as a **peacekeeping force** as a fighting one, in a region where the host state barely functions at all.

## Holding It

- **The Plateau Garrison** — a multinational force under JPC colors with a local contingent that knows the forest; checkpoints, drone patrols, and the standing rule that no patrol goes into the caves alone.
- **The Voices** — the cave system carries sound for kilometres, and patrols report Devil Tongue drifting out of the dark: the dispersed remnant's voices, never the gate's. The gate itself is silent. The garrison's standing order is to answer nothing.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- **An invasion that dissolved** — the war the enemy chose not to fight, and the garrison left holding the jungle.
- **Voices in the dark** — an enemy that speaks while the door stays silent.

### Story Hooks
- A patrol records the cave voices counting in **Human** — mispronounced, but counting: the dispersed horde has been learning, not hiding.
- The garrison's local tracker — the one person who walks the bush without a drone — reports that the dispersed remnant has been **gathering** at a place the maps call nothing, for a reason no one can translate.
- A drone survey of the plateau finds a second entrance to the cave system, far from the gate — and the entrance is **walled**, from the outside, with stone that is not stone.
