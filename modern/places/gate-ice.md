# Gate Echo — the Ice Gate

> Some doors are not found. They are heard.

## Overview

**Gate Echo** — JPC designation **G-05**, known as **the Ice Gate** — is the only confirmed gate that no human being has ever seen. It sits beneath the Greenland ice sheet, roughly 800 metres down, in a region where the nearest settlement (Ittoqqortoormiit, on the Scoresby Sound) was evacuated on Day Two. It was found by radar, from the air: a standing anomaly in the ice, warm, ring-shaped, humming at a frequency the ice transmits with eerie clarity. The gate's own heat keeps a subglacial pocket of meltwater open around it — a lake under the ice, with a door at its bottom.

## Why It Is Contained

The anchor is **unreachable**. The only drilling attempt, in 2032, was abandoned after the shaft began to close behind the rig: the ice moves at kilometres per year, and the borehole did not survive the season. Demolition was never seriously proposed; you cannot place charges on a frame you cannot reach, through a medium you cannot stand on. The Ice Gate is contained by physics, and JPC has made its peace with that.

It is also the **quietest** gate: nothing has emerged since the first week, and the emergence itself is still described by the evacuation reports rather than by anyone who saw it.

## Holding It

- **The Margin Garrison** — a small station on the ice margin, a hundred kilometres from the gate's surveyed position (which moves with the ice); its job is listening, not fighting.
- **The Borehole Array** — strings of sensors sunk into the ice, relaying the gate's hum to the surface around the clock.
- The rotation is called **"the hermitage"** — the smallest garrison in JPC, and the one that talks to itself most.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- **A gate as a pure signal** — the only portal known entirely through instruments; the door you cannot look at.
- The **subglacial horror** — a warm pocket of water under a kilometre of ice, with a door at the bottom of it.

### Story Hooks
- The borehole array records the script **singing** — a sustained tone where the usual hum used to be. The change happened the same week the Babel Initiative published its first verified translations. The Ice Gate is the only one that appears to listen.
- A meltwater sample from the subglacial lake contains **spores** — not alien, not dead: a pollen from no plant on Earth's current record.
- The margin garrison's new lieutenant asks what the evacuation reports actually say about the emergence. The archive contains no reports. It contains a **single photograph**, and the officer who catalogued it retired the next day.
