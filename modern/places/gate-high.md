# Gate India — the High Gate

> The door is a window, and the window is in the mountain.

## Overview

**Gate India** — JPC designation **G-09**, known as **the High Gate** — is a portal set into a sheer cliff face at roughly 4,700 metres in the Peruvian Andes, at the heart of a pre-Inca ruin complex the locals called the Mouth of the Mountain. The anchor is unusual: the frame is set **behind** the cliff face, so that the gate opens as a window in the rock — a mouth in the mountain, looking out over a valley that had, until Day One, no name on any military map.

The March that came through it did not survive the descent. Thin air, cold, and a cliff that only a mountaineer could love killed more of the horde than the defenders did; the battle of the valley floor, in 2031, is local legend. Nothing has emerged since.

## Why It Is Contained

The anchor is **unreachable and unbreakable both**. The frame is inside the mountain; there is nothing to place charges on except a cliff face that is also a ruin, and the one approach — a fixed cableway to a small observation post at the mouth — is watched, not stood upon. No one sets foot at the window. The order is not written down; it is simply understood, the way the valley's elders understand that you do not knock on the Mountain's mouth.

## Holding It

- **The Valley Garrison** — a small force at the valley floor, with the cableway, the observation post, and the valley's chronicler: a local elder retained as translator and memory, who was there in 2031 and has opinions about what the Mountain is.
- The High Gate is the most beautiful gate in the world, which the garrison considers its own private curse.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- **A gate as a window** — the portal you look out of, not into.
- **Altitude as armor** — a door defended by thin air and a cliff.

### Story Hooks
- The valley's chronicler notes, quietly, that the Mountain has **two mouths** — and the second is not on any survey, satellite image, or map JPC has ever produced.
- The observation post's telescope records the far side of the window: a **city** — intact, lit, and not in any photograph of the dead world's surface.
- A member of the garrison asks the chronicler what the Mountain says. The chronicler answers that it has not needed to say anything since 2031 — and that the valley is still waiting to find out what that means.
