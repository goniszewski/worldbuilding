# Gate Charlie — the Zagros Gate

> A door in the mountain that nobody was allowed to kill.

## Overview

**Gate Charlie** — JPC designation **G-03**, known as **the Zagros Gate** — stands in a ruin-city on a ridge of the Zagros range, in western Iran. The anchor is a **lintel-frame of megalithic scale**, its not-stone beams fused into the bones of the ridge: the frame is load-bearing in a way demolitions engineers still argue about. It is the largest anchor on land, and the only one the Scouring gave up on without a kill-shot.

The gate woke on Day One inside the ruin complex — a place the locals called the City of the Old Door, which the archaeologists had called a fortress and the military now calls a problem.

## Why It Is Contained

The Zagros anchor is **unbreakable by the means JPC was willing to use**. The frame is so deeply fused into the ridge that destroying it means destroying the mountain — a demolition requiring yields no one was prepared to authorize over the Iranian heartland. The nuclear option was debated and lost, for reasons that had nothing to do with the war: fallout corridors, population, and three capitals that refused to sign. The gate survived the Scouring because **politics protected it**.

What the Scouring learned instead is that the frame does not need to be intact to be dangerous — it needs to be *watched*.

## The Far Side

Denial Command's data on the Zagros far side is thin — no one has crossed, by doctrine and by choice. What is known: the far side is **loud**. The emergence was the heaviest of any gate in the first week; the hordes that came through were organized, uniformed in a way no other March was. Analysts believe the Zagros gate opens into a **lord's heartland** — one of the few functioning armies of the dead world. Its silence since 2032 is therefore treated as a held breath, not a peace.

## Holding It

- **The Ridge Line** — a garrison built along the opposing ridgeline, guns zeroed on the ruin from three directions; the gate is watched, not approached.
- **The Old City** — the ruin itself is a no-go zone; patrols walk its edges on a schedule the garrison changes daily.
- The rotation is considered a plum posting: quiet, mountainous, and the best food in Denial Command (the Iranian contingent cooks).

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- A gate protected by **politics, not physics** — the one door the apocalypse could not close because nations would not let it.
- **The unbroken thing** — the largest anchor on land is the one nobody truly understands.

### Story Hooks
- The Babel Initiative completes a full translation of the Zagros script — and finds it is **not** in the same dialect as the other anchors. The Zagros gate is older than the empire that built the rest. Or newer. The translators do not agree.
- A seismic station records a **second heartbeat** under the ridge — one that does not match the frame's known structure.
- The Iranian contingent's commander requests the gate be opened — not to cross, but because something on the far side has begun replying to the garrison's signal-lamp drills in kind.
