# Joint Portal Command

> One command, nine doors, and the only rule that matters: nothing gets through, and nothing gets out — without us.

## Overview

**Joint Portal Command (JPC)** is the unified military authority chartered on 14 February 2032, in the aftermath of the Scouring. It is not a treaty organization and not a standing army; it is a **command**, stood up by the nations that survived the March with the mandate to do one thing — hold the nine remaining gates.

Its authority is narrow and absolute. Member nations contribute forces, funding, and jurisdiction; JPC exercises operational command over every active gate, every perimeter, and every program that touches the far side. No national force operates a gate without JPC's colors. No national laboratory studies an anchor without JPC's oversight. The arrangement is bitterly resented by some and fervently supported by most; everyone understands that the alternative is seventeen national armies each pointing guns at the same nine doorways.

## Doctrine

### The Three Rules
1. **No gate unguarded.** Every active gate is garrisoned, watched, and zeroed. The garrison is rotated; the surveillance is not.
2. **Denial at the mouth.** Firepower is arrayed so that anything emerging is destroyed inside the kill zone. The hordes learned this the hard way in 2031; the gates have been quiet on the emergence side since.
3. **Nothing unilateral.** No member nation experiments, mines, or negotiates on its own. The rule exists because three nations tried, and the results are classified.

### The Argument That Never Ends
JPC is split down the middle on the **two-way doctrine**:

- **Denial Command** holds that the [Crossing](../lore/the-reopening.md) is a slow-motion catastrophe — that every soldier sent through a gate is a hostage given willingly, and that the perimeters are bait for the Observers.
- **The Gate Commands** hold that containment is a slow-motion surrender — that defending a doorstep forever means ceding the initiative to an enemy that outlasts generations.

Both are right, and the argument is by design: Mensah maintains the tension deliberately, so that no doctrine ever ossifies into dogma.

## Culture

JPC is a coalition of armies, navies, air forces, and space commands that had never fought together, fused into a single organization by a common enemy. The culture that emerged is pragmatic, darkly humorous, and superstitious in the way only militaries can be:

- **The Watch** — a superstition born in 2032: someone is always awake near a gate, even when the duty roster says otherwise.
- **Gate rotations** are measured in "tours", not years; a tour is however long your garrison can hold without relief.
- The phrase "**through the door**" means two things in JPC slang: an operation on the far side, and a decision with no way back.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- The **bureaucracy of the apocalypse** — a world government that works because the enemy is real.
- Military culture as a **language** — doctrine, superstition, and the jokes soldiers tell about the end of the world.
- The **tension between defense and expansion** — the argument that never ends, and the people it breaks.

### Story Hooks
- A Denial Command garrison mutinies in miniature: they refuse to rotate through a containment gate that "hummed" the night before.
- The Babel Initiative asks JPC for permission to send a **civilian** through a gate — an archaeologist who can read the far-side ruins faster than any soldier.
- Mensah's replacement is announced. The candidate list contains exactly one name, and the command is split on whether that is reassuring.
