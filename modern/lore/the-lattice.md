# The Lattice

> The doors were not scattered. They were placed — and the placement is a map.

## The Discovery

After the Reopening, analysts had always suspected the gates' placement was deliberate. The anchors stood where **people were not** — ruins, trenches, deep water, empty deserts — as if whoever set them up did not want them found. Suspicion became proof in **2034**, and the proof came from geology, not archaeology: a Babel Initiative geophysicist ran the 47 confirmed anchors' positions through paleogeographic reconstruction software — the same tools used to model continental drift — and the pattern **snapped into place**.

Rotated back into the deep past, the anchors resolve into a **geometric lattice**: regular, deliberate, exact. The reconstruction dates the network's laying to **roughly a million years before the present** — older than any human settlement, older than every cycle in the record. The empire built it at the height of its reach, then spent the cycles raiding through a network it had laid down in an age it no longer fully remembered.

The program that reconstructs and surveys the pattern is **Project Lattice**, a joint JPC–Babel Initiative effort.

## Drift

The anchors are fixed to the crust. For a million years, plate tectonics carried them — landmasses sliding, sea floors spreading, coastlines drowning — so the lattice no longer matches any map of the modern world. Some anchors have moved kilometres from their original positions; one sits on a plate boundary that has been grinding for longer than the network has existed.

The drift is the pattern's own proof. The 47 woken gates only fall onto the lattice when the plates are rotated back to their million-year-old positions — and when they do, the lattice does what a map does: it shows what is **missing**.

## The Network

The complete lattice predicts **85 anchors** on Earth. The Reopening woke **47**. The other 38 did not wake — and most of them are not sleeping. They are gone:

| Outcome | Count | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Woke in the Reopening | 47 | The active gates — destroyed, contained, or held |
| Destroyed — traces remain | 21 | Natural destruction across a million years: subducted (2), crushed by glaciation (1), buried by volcanic collapse (1), drowned (1), and more broken by deep time; wreckage still surveyable |
| Destroyed — no trace | 9 | **The Unmade** — the lattice demands a node; the site holds nothing. No wreckage, no scar, nothing that weathered away. Removal, and the removal was clean |
| Found dormant | 6 | **D-sites** — confirmed where the lattice predicted, cold and silent |
| Missing | 2 | Nodes the lattice demands with no anchor and no geological explanation |

Why only 47 woke: a dormant anchor is **invisible**. No light, no hum, no heat in the script — nothing to find unless you already know where to look. The 47 that woke are the ones the lords could wake from the far side; the Reopening was their experiment, run against the anchors their surviving records still remembered. The other 38 were lost to the empire's fall — their positions preserved in no record the lords kept, or in records that burned with the Observers' war. What the lords' records did not remember, a million years of Earth mostly destroyed anyway.

The survivors were **quite easy to find** — once the pattern existed. The reconstruction supplies coordinates; survey work confirms them: magnetometry, ground-penetrating radar, old sonar sweeps, regional archives, and folklore that suddenly stopped being folklore. Several D-sites had been known to archaeology for decades as "anomalous ruins" — not-stone dismissed as strange geology, or structures that did not match any culture's record. The pattern retroactively named them. The difficulty was never finding the doors. The difficulty is what to do with the ones you find.

## The Unmade

Nine nodes on the lattice are **empty**. The pattern is exact there — the same geometry, the same certainty as any node that holds a door — and the site holds nothing. No not-stone, no rubble, no melt, no blast shadow, no geological scar. Natural destruction leaves traces: a subducted anchor leaves a suture in the sea floor, a glaciated one leaves crushed stone, a drowned one leaves a reef of debris. The Unmade leave nothing that a survey can find. It does not look like destruction. It looks like **removal** — someone took these doors apart, and took the evidence of them as well.

The analyses are inconclusive. The leading theory at Project Lattice is deliberate unmaking: the empire denying its own doors to the Observers as the war turned, or the Observers denying the doors to anyone at all. A minority reading is that the Unmade were never anchors in the empire's time at all — nodes of a network that predates the empire, unmade in a war older than every record either civilization kept. JPC's intelligence arm calls the Unmade the cleanest evidence that a **third hand** worked this network: the lords did not do this, no natural force did this, and the two hands that remain did not leave this signature. It is a theory. Nothing about it is proven, and nothing about it is comfortable.

## The D-Sites

Six dormant anchors, designated **D-01** onward, in the same concealment pattern as the active gates — ruins, underground, underwater, remote. Most are unremarkable watched sites: a sealed shaft, a reef, a folded hillside with a fence around it. A few are not:

| Desig. | Site | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| D-01 | The Sunken Church | Baltic shallows; a not-stone ring under 14 m of water, known to local folklore for centuries as a drowned church |
| D-02 | The Fought-Back | A Victorian alpine rail tunnel abandoned when "the rock fought back"; the heading had struck a buried lintel |
| D-04 | The Lagoon Arch | A Pacific atoll; an 1890s expedition's report of a "black arch" was filed as fiction — the arch is the anchor |
| D-06 | The Gobi Anomaly | A density anomaly mapped by a geophysical survey; the anomaly is older than the instruments that found it |

The designations carry scars of their own: D-01 through D-06 were assigned from the lattice's predictions, and several numbers were **retired** when ground truth arrived. D-03, D-05, and others closed their files as the survey found not a dormant door but a destroyed one — a node the lattice had marked as sleeping that had been dead for longer than human time. The map predicts; the ground decides.

The **two missing anchors** are Project Lattice's most wanted objects. The lattice demands them; the survey finds nothing — no ruin, no not-stone, no geology that explains an empty node. Either the pattern is wrong at exactly two points (nothing else suggests it is), or something moved them in a way plates do not.

## Why It Matters

Every dormant anchor is a door the lords could wake from the far side. The 2031 waking proved the capability, and the lords' scholars read the same classical texts Project Lattice uses; JPC intelligence believes they are mapping the same lattice from the far side — more slowly, with less to work with, but from the side that matters. Whether they have found any D-site is not known.

The D-sites have reopened JPC's oldest argument. **Destroy** — the anchors can be broken, and a door the lords cannot wake is no door at all; but demolition of a dormant anchor risks waking it. **Watch** — garrison the sites, keep them cold, and leave a loaded gun in nobody's hands; but a watched door is still a door, and the lords have all the time in the world. The argument is unresolved, which is to say: both sides are right.

The destroyed anchors are the network's only uncomplicated good news — a door the lords can never wake. All except the Unmade. A destroyed anchor is proof a door can be broken; an Unmade node is proof a door can be broken **and never have existed**. If the lords ever learn that someone unmade nine doors without a trace, they will ask the same question JPC's analysts do — and JPC does not want to be the side that teaches them the question exists.

And then there is the last page of the file. The lattice resolves along a **dominant arc**, and the arc points at a single location on Earth — a place that is not one of the 47, not a D-site, not any node the pattern otherwise explains. Project Lattice's analysts call it the **terminus**. What is there is not known. The file on it is blank, and it stays blank: the terminus is the one coordinate nobody has been sent to check.

## For Story Use

### Themes to Explore
- **The map as weapon** — knowing where the doors are is a strategic asset before any of them opens.
- **Deep time** — a war being fought on the doorstep of a network a million years old; the empires are squatters.
- **The race to be first** — dormant doors, two civilizations reading the same map from opposite sides.
- **Absence as evidence** — the loudest nodes on the map are the ones with nothing in them.

### Story Hooks
- A survey team at a D-site reports the anchor is **warm** — the script's heat, in a site that has been cold for a million years. The team's next transmission is the team's last.
- The two missing anchors are photographed in a single satellite pass — two locations, neither on the lattice, neither where the other should be.
- A D-site wakes. Nothing comes through. JPC cannot decide whether that is a warning or an invitation.
- A demolition crew at an Unmade node finds the one thing no Unmade node has: a single shard of not-stone, not rubble but **cut**, and still warm to the touch.
- The nine Unmade nodes, plotted separately, form a line of their own — and the line crosses the terminus's arc at a perfect right angle.
- The terminus coordinates are released to a member nation's survey office by accident. The survey office's reply, two days later, is a single word: *no*.
