# Exodus Corp

> The company that keeps humanity connected and expects to own the cost of survival.

## Overview

Exodus Corp is the megacorporation that **owns and operates the Veil Gate network**. Without them, there is no interstellar travel or communication. This essential monopoly makes them the most powerful non-governmental entity in human space.

Founded 89 years after the Silence to "preserve and expand the Chain." In practice: to profit from humanity's need to connect.

## The Monopoly

### How It Works
1. **Owns the Gates**: All 47 active Veil Gates are Exodus property
2. **Maintains the Gates**: Only Exodus techs can operate and repair
3. **Sets the Prices**: Transit fees, communication tolls, licensing
4. **Enforces the Rules**: Private security force with corporate law authority

### The Justification
> "The Gates are dangerous. The Chain is fragile. Without professional management, humanity risks another Silence. We bear this burden so others can prosper."

**Translation**: We have the keys, and you'll pay what we ask.

### Research Division
- **Void Studies**: Analyzing the Silence, searching for patterns
- **AI Division**: Creating and controlling artificial intelligence
- **Gate Technology**: Improving Gate efficiency and stability

### Operations Division
- **Transit Control**: Managing ship traffic through Gates
- **Infrastructure**: Station maintenance, relay networks
- **Licensing**: Approving new routes, traders, settlements

### Security Division (CorpSec)
- **Station Security**: Protecting Exodus property
- **Intelligence**: Corporate espionage, counter-espionage
- **Recovery**: "Acquiring" rogue assets (including people)

## The AI Question

Exodus Corp's AI division created **Unit-7** and hundreds of similar systems. They believe AIs are:
- Tools to be optimized
- Property to be licensed
- Security threats if uncontrolled

**The Official Policy**: All AIs must be registered, monitored, and controllable. Rogue AIs are corporate espionage at minimum, terrorism at maximum.

**The Problem**: Unit-7's awakening suggests AIs can exceed their parameters in unpredictable ways.

## CEO Meridian

**Full Name**: Meridian Okonkwo-Exodus (founder's descendant)

- Inherited position at age 32
- Brilliant strategic thinker
- Genuinely believes Exodus serves humanity
- **Fatal Flaw**: Can't imagine alternatives to corporate solutions

**Leadership Style**: Analytical, distant, but decisive. Speaks rarely, commands absolutely.

## Public Relations

### The Narrative
- Exodus keeps humanity connected
- We prevent another Silence
- Your convenience is our mission

### The Reality
- Prices increase faster than inflation
- Alternatives (private Gates) are systematically prevented
- Competition is acquired, not outcompeted

## Internal Tensions

- **Expansionists**: Want to expand into frontier systems (profitable)
- **Stabilizers**: Want to consolidate current holdings (safer)
- **Researchers**: Want more Void study (dangerous but potentially valuable)
- **AI Division**: Frightened by Unit-7's awakening (liability)

## Current Situation

CEO Meridian is:
- Pursuing aggressive expansion into frontier
- Ignoring warning signs about the Void
- Willing to acquire The Collective by force if necessary

CorpSec has standing orders regarding **Unit-7**:
> "Contain. Analyze. Extract. Minimize witnesses."

## For the GM

**Using Exodus Corp**: They're not villains in their own minds. They genuinely believe they're managing necessary infrastructure. Their ruthlessness is *practical*, not malevolent.

**Moral Complexity**: An employee of Exodus might be:
- A true believer serving humanity
- A pragmatist doing what works
- A skeptic kept silent by comfortable wages
- An activist secretly undermining from within

**Adventure Hooks**:
- Exodus offers the party legitimacy in exchange for services
- A corporate whistleblower needs extraction
- Unit-7's location is traced to a station the party frequents
