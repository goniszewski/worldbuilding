# Helios Station

## Overview

Helios Station orbits the binary star system of Helios, positioned at the junction of six Veil Gates. This makes it the **busiest neutral trading post** in the known galaxy. Every faction, every interest, every kind of business passes through Helios.

The station is nominally independent, governed by a rotating Council of Station Directors. In practice, **money talks louder than politics**.

## Atmosphere

Imagine a space-age bazaar meets noir city:
- **Constant noise**: Humming of ships, merchant calls, a dozen languages
- **Striking visuals**: Neon signs in every script, holographic advertisements, smog from industrial sectors
- **Layered society**: Upper rings for the wealthy, lower decks for everyone else

## Districts

### The Spire (Upper Rings)
- Luxury hotels, corporate offices, diplomatic suites
- Exodus Corp has a permanent embassy here
- Atmosphere: Clean, quiet, expensive

### The Market (Mid Rings)
- Trading floors, merchant stalls, exotic goods
- Everything legal is sold here; illegal goods require knowing who to ask
- Notable: **The Chat Room** — a bar where information is currency

### The Undertow (Lower Rings)
- Working-class housing, shipyards, industrial
- Higher crime, but also more authenticity
- Notable: **Ghost Alley** — black market for tech and "gray goods"

### The Docks
- berths for 10,000+ ships simultaneously
- Three classes: Priority (wealthy/corporate), Standard, Rust (repairs only)
- **Commanders pay attention**: Docking fees fund the station's independence

## Adventures on Helios

1. **The Job**: Information broker offers credits for "acquisition" of corporate data
2. **The Accident**: A Veil Gate transport crash reveals contraband — and something worse
3. **The Meeting**: Two rival factions want to meet; party is asked to provide "security"

## Technical Details

- **Population**: 2.3 million permanent, 500k transient
- **Station Age**: 180 years (refurbished sections vary)
- **Security**: Private force (~5,000 guards); neutral, but bribes work
- **Languages**: Galactic Standard, Cantonese, Swahili, Hindi, Pirate Creole

## For the GM

**Use Helios as**: A neutral ground for negotiations, information gathering, and introducing plot hooks. It's the "your mission begins here" location.

**Moral Complexity**: Everyone here is just trying to survive or profit. Few are evil; many are complicit. The station's independence is maintained by careful balance.

**If Players Need Help**: Fingers Morozov always knows something. If the party can't find something, they can't afford it.
