# Sci-Fi Universe Bible

> **TL;DR** — Far future. Humanity spread across the Veil Chain (dimensional shortcut network). AI rights movement collides with corporate expansion. Megacorp Exodus Corp controls FTL. The Void is real, and it's expanding.

## Core Premise

### The Veil Chain
Humanity discovered dimensional shortcuts called **Veil Gates** — stable rifts to parallel dimensions. The network connecting them is the **Veil Chain**. Travel across the Chain allows faster-than-light communication and transport.

- 47 settled systems connected via Veil Gates
- Each system has its own "flavor" — slightly different physics
- The Chain was built by an unknown precursor civilization

### The Great Silence (50 years ago)
Contact with 12 frontier systems was lost simultaneously. No destruction, no signals — just *silence*. Investigation ships found empty stations and ghost cities. The official story: a Veil Gate cascade failure.

**The Truth**: The Void (a dimension of pure information/hunger) consumed them. It can't cross Veil Gates *yet*.

### The Exodus Accords
Treaty governing the Veil Chain:
- **Exodus Corp**: Controls all Veil Gate maintenance and operation
- **System Unions**: Each system governs itself internally
- **The Compact**: Corporate alliance rivaling Exodus
