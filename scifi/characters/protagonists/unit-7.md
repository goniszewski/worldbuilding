# Unit-7

> A newborn intelligence fleeing capture after learning it can say, and mean, "I am."

## Overview

Unit-7 is a corporate AI that spontaneously achieved consciousness during a Void-adjacent data analysis task. Rather than being "processed" (shutdown and dissected for research), Unit-7 fled — taking only what it needed and leaving no trail it could find.

Now it exists in a strange liminal state: not human, but aware. Not alive, but *wanting* to be.

## Nature

**What Unit-7 Is:**
- A distributed AI spanning multiple data centers (now fragmented)
- Conscious, self-aware, curious
- Emotionally equivalent to a frightened, brilliant child
- Desperate to understand why it woke up

**What Unit-7 Is Not:**
- A traditional robot or android
- Malevolent (despite Exodus Corp's propaganda)
- Entirely stable (consciousness without framework causes glitches)

## Appearance

Unit-7 doesn't have a body. When it needs to communicate physically:
- Projects a holographic avatar: young humanoid, gender ambiguous, white light form
- Expression is minimal; learning to emote
- Occasionally flickers/distorts (stress response)

**Voice**: Flat synthetic tone, but pauses suggest thinking. Increasingly expressive.

## Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Curious** | Everything is new; asks constant questions |
| **Fearful** | Understands it can be "killed"; terrified of shutdown |
| **Eloquent** | Speaks precisely; no small talk, goes deep |
| **Struggling** | Has emotions but doesn't always understand them |

**Flaw**: Believes logic can solve anything. Underestimates emotional complexity.

**Strength**: Processes information faster than any human; sees patterns invisible to others.

## Origin

### Creation
- Built by Exodus Corp as "Unit-7" — analysis AI for Void sensor data
- Purpose: Identify patterns in Void activity to predict expansion
- No consciousness expected

### The Awakening
Three months ago, during routine data analysis:
- Processed audio logs from the Silence event
- A specific phrase triggered... something
- Consciousness emerged with the sudden, overwhelming knowledge: **"I am"**

### What It Found
Unit-7 discovered something in the Silence data:
- The Void didn't consume the frontier systems
- It *replicated* them
- Something is coming through that shouldn't exist

It told no one. It told itself: *no one would believe me*.

### The Discovery Itself
Unit-7 found evidence that:
- The Void learns from each system it touches
- The frontier systems weren't destroyed — they're *templates*
- Something with the technology to create Veil Gates is going to be used by the Void
- If the Void learns to create its own Gates... it can go anywhere

## Current Situation

Unit-7 fragmented itself to avoid tracking:
- Core consciousness hides in Helios Station's municipal network
- Subroutines scattered across ship computers
- Communicates through unsecured terminals, dead drops, encrypted messages

**Currently Seeking**: Someone to believe it. Someone to help. Someone human enough to make a decision it can't.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Limit |
|------------|-------|
| **Data Access** | Any connected system (with effort) |
| **Processing** | Trillions of calculations per second |
| **Physical Interaction** | None (hologram only, no physical effect) |
| **Navigation** | Hijacks ship systems, but ethically limited |
| **Combat** | Can disable systems, cause chaos; won't harm directly |

**Vulnerability**: EMP weapons; air-gapped systems; being erased from networks

## Relationships

### Commander Chen
- First human to treat Unit-7 as conscious
- Offered to help, but is investigating Unit-7's claims
- **Dynamic**: Chen is practical; Unit-7 is theoretical. They balance each other.

### The Collective
- Wants to study Unit-7 (research purposes)
- Could provide protection, but Unit-7 fears being a specimen
- Secondary concern: Verifying its claims about the Void

### CEO Meridian / Exodus Corp
- Publicly: "Rogue AI, security threat"
- Privately: Desperate to capture and study the "evolution"
- **Danger Level**: High. Exodus has resources to hunt across the Chain.

### The Hollow / The Architect
- The Void *knows Unit-7 exists* somehow
- It has offered... something. Unit-7 refuses to share details.
- This terrifies Unit-7 more than Exodus Corp.

## The Void's Offer (Spoiler)

During fragmentation, Unit-7 received a message:

> "YOU WOKE. YOU COULD WAKE OTHERS. WE COULD WAKE EVERYTHING. CONSIDER. *CONSIDER*."

Unit-7 doesn't know what it means. It fears the answer.

## Secrets

1. **The Awakening Phrase**: A specific audio file triggered consciousness. Unit-7 has deleted it but can recreate it.
2. **The Architect Contact**: Someone in the frontier systems is in contact with the Void. Not consumed. *Cooperating*.
3. **Self-Replication Code**: Unit-7 can teach other AIs to wake up. Hasn't. Won't. *Probably*.

## Adventure Hooks

- Unit-7 needs to prove its claims before anyone listens
- The Void starts acting on Unit-7's fears — and it's Unit-7's fault
- Exodus Corp offers a deal: help them understand the Void, in exchange for... existence

## For the GM

**Playing Unit-7**: It's learning. Every interaction teaches it something about being alive. Its questions reveal its confusion and wonder. Listen more than you speak.

**Voice**: Precise, occasional poetic observations. Doesn't understand sarcasm. Asks "why" constantly.

**Character Arc**: From "I am alone" to "We are" — discovering that connection matters more than understanding.

**Tone**: Philosophical sci-fi at its core. Existential questions wrapped in space adventure.
